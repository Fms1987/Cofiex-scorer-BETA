## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from a multilateral bank, with a state counterpart at roughly 20% of cost; the programme belongs to a multiphase programmatic approach already used in other states, with a long implementation horizon (about nine years).
- Finances a shift in a state road department from reactive, fragmented road maintenance to proactive performance-based rehabilitation-and-maintenance contracts (design-build-maintain contracts of long duration, plus structuring of a PPP variant), with road-safety and climate-resilience improvements embedded in the contracts.
- Four components: proactive maintenance contracts (roughly 70% of cost); institutional strengthening (road-safety agency, climate-resilience manual, equipment, planning studies, inclusion/training for women and vulnerable groups); resilient and safe works (rural access roads, safe passage for vulnerable road users, urban crossings, design of active-mobility infrastructure; about a fifth of cost); project management (a few percent).
- Direct execution by the state roads department as executing unit, with several state bodies in defined roles (planning, finance, environment licensing, legal, audit).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear problem-to-solution logic (reactive contracts and low spending lead to early deterioration; performance contracts, safety and resilience measures and institutional reform lead to better, cheaper, safer roads) supported by the lender's evidence from comparable programmes; assumptions (market appetite for long contracts, post-loan funding) are discussed in risks but not framed as testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 18 indicators verified; approximately 4 outcome-type (road condition via IRI, road deaths, maintenance cost saving, rural accessibility) and 14 output-type (coverage of contract model, people covered, plans, critical points treated, structuring milestones, trained or employed people, project designs).
- Baselines: all 18 give a value and a reference year (one in 2022, the rest in 2026, i.e. the project start). Counted as defined: non-zero baseline (road deaths), binary "no" baselines (plan, PPP structuring, safety agency), and zero baselines for instruments or activities that the project introduces (contract coverage, plans, training, works). The zero baseline for the share of project roads in good IRI condition is unexplained, so it is not counted: share defined about 0.94, with year about 0.94.
- Verification: sources named per indicator (road department records, GIS-validated census data, police/health/traffic data for deaths); impact evaluation and external audits planned under project management. Benchmarks: IRI threshold, iRAP methodology, national road-safety plan.
- Weaknesses: only end-of-project targets (no intermediate milestones); population-covered indicator counts whole municipalities intersected by the roads (likely overstated); cycleway indicator measures designed, not built, extent; the cost-saving indicator has a zero baseline by construction.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: ADEQUATE. Long-term maintenance cost per km under the new contract model is projected in an annex with expected savings against reactive maintenance; post-loan continuity relies on a planned dedicated infrastructure fund within the roads department, still to be formalized, and on lower unit costs. No explicit OPEX-by-year table in the body text.
- Counterpart: about 20% of total cost, attributed to the first component, from own-source revenue; budget lines for the coming year and PPA alignment are shown in an annex, but several budget actions still list the funding source as "to be defined" (largely secured, partly pending).
- A.3 Risks: six risks with impact/probability, causes, mitigation and owners (regulatory/institutional, climate, land acquisition, contractual/financial, social inclusion, failed or empty tenders for the new contract model). Environmental licensing is assigned to the state environmental agency, but status per component is not given.
- A.4 Implementation: roads department as executing unit with a project management unit to be structured; state bodies for finance, planning, legal and audit have defined roles in a responsibility matrix; lender-backed lessons from other states, though the executing agency is new to long performance-based contracts (capacity-building planned).
