## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type technical-assistance/systems loan (IBRD) to a state government under a public-management and fiscal-sustainability programme model (not PBL); counterpart at the 10%-of-loan level.
- Finances information systems, consultancies, training and equipment (no works) to rationalise spending: payroll and workforce planning, pension management, public procurement, public investment management, intergovernmental transfers, planning-budget integration, asset management and sector financial-management systems for health, education and social assistance.
- Three components: cross-government fiscal/financial/asset management (about two thirds of cost), sector financial management (about a third), and project and change management (small share); 14 products.
- Direct execution by the state finance secretariat through a UGP, with implementation units in partner secretariats; a technical forum shared with other states in the same programme family.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE to STRONG. Problems per spending area lead to subcomponents, products and expected results; savings are expected from better payroll, pension, procurement and asset control, but assumptions (political continuity, absorption by weaker units) appear only as risks, not as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: STRONG. 11 indicators verified, approximately 8 outcome-type (real growth of payroll and pension spending, procurement lead time, execution rate of investment, health waste reduction, education transfers) and 3 process/output-type (investment projects under new methodology, internal audit maturity level, adoption of the social assistance system).
- All 11 carry a baseline value with a 2023/2024 reference and yearly targets for a five-year trajectory; zero baselines (3) are counted because the underlying system or methodology does not yet exist.
- Each indicator states formula, frequency, polarity, data source and the responsible secretariat; verification relies on state administrative systems and national fiscal-reporting data.
- Weaknesses: the CC admits payroll projections still need refinement during preparation; some targets are flat in early years; savings figures cited in results are in BRL (not reproduced); the maturity indicator has a coarse 1-3 scale and its source field repeats the investment system.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. A sustainability plan is promised for preparation; loan covers system maintenance during execution (maintenance capped at roughly a quarter of annual systems cost) and the state budget takes over afterwards; a state public IT company will absorb and maintain systems; procurement clauses cover warranty, assisted operation and source-code transfer. No multi-year OPEX table; a high minimum IRR is announced but not demonstrated.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: 9 risks with impact/probability, including institutional capacity, procurement, authorising law (state legislature), technical design, stakeholders/political cycle; environmental, expropriation and resettlement risks marked low/not applicable because no works are eligible. Mitigation: centralised procurement commission, UGP support, change management, training.
- Counterpart: required at the 10% level; authorising state law was still being sent to the legislature ("to be secured"); no budget line cited.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP in the finance secretariat with defined coordinations (financial, M&E, procurement, IT), experience with prior multilateral operations, implementation units per partner secretariat; final arrangements to be settled after the lender's capacity assessment.
