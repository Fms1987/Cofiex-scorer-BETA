## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (FONPLATA), direct execution by a mid-sized municipal government; counterpart at the 20% minimum; no intermediation.
- Financed: urban road and mobility works (roughly 70% of cost): arterial duplication, a bypass, a one-way pair system, rural link-road widening, wide-scale road restructuring and a long segregated cycle network, plus accessible sidewalks; also reurbanisation of two informal settlements, macro/micro drainage and a linear river park (together roughly 20%).
- Components: (1) studies and designs (counterpart-funded; basic designs still being prepared), (2) infrastructure and mobility, (3) reurbanisation/environment/sanitation, (4) expropriations, (5) programme management, (6) lender administration fee.
- Header sector (infrastructure_mobility) is consistent with the content.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-delivery-result chain is laid out (fleet growth and saturated arterials, informal settlements, flooding, scarce green space) and each indicator is mapped to specific objectives and strategic areas; assumptions are not made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 8 indicators (verified), each with description, formula, unit, baseline, target, verification means and a responsible unit; approximate split of 2 outcome-type (average vehicle travel time; an ordinal public-space-use index) and 6 output-type (cycle network, sidewalk area, duplicated/one-way road length, green area, settlement population served, drainage length).
- Baselines: 6 of 8 state a value (share 0.75); none states a reference year (year share 0.0). Two zero baselines (road length duplicated; population served by reurbanisation) were NOT counted as defined because the CC does not explain the zero; the ordinal index baseline ("poor") was counted.
- Weaknesses: the green-area target is almost equal to its baseline (very small gain); the travel-time formula is just "average time" with survey-based verification and no method; the drainage target is small relative to the drainage scope described; the park area is stated inconsistently between the studies and the product sections. Verification by field measurement and works reports is present for the output indicators.
