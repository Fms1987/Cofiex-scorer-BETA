## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan from CAF to a mid-sized municipality (direct execution by the municipal government, no intermediation). Ticket in the 50-100M USD band, counterpart at the 20% minimum from municipal tax revenue; 5-year execution, roughly 17.5-year total term with 5 years of grace, floating SOFR-based rate with a spread of about 2%.
- Financed: a multi-sector urban renewal package: new and refurbished urban parks and a botanical garden, rooftop solar on public buildings, macro-drainage works at recurrent flood spots, and a set of road links, paired one-way streets and overpasses across highways that split the city; plus land acquisition.
- Components (3): sustainability (environment and renewable energy, roughly 30% of cost); urban infrastructure (drainage and mobility, about two thirds); programme management with supervision, UCP support, audit and studies (a few percent).
- Entity type: municipal government borrower; executor is the chief-of-staff secretariat with a programme coordination unit and several municipal secretariats and a planning institute in support; municipality sits in the border strip.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Four problems (lack of qualified leisure space, recurrent flooding, high energy cost, road discontinuity) each with causes, consequences, a matching solution line and expected results tied to four specific objectives; the link is plausible but assumptions are not stated as testable, and the package is sectorally diverse.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 8 indicators, approximately 5 outcome-type (park area per resident, energy savings, flood spots, population near cycle infrastructure, travel time) and 3 output-type (camera coverage of parks, share of qualified parks, installed solar capacity); each has formula, data to collect, PPA objective mapping, baseline with reference year and target year.
- Baselines: all 8 stated with a reference year (shares 1.0 and 1.0); no zero baselines.
- Verification: municipal records, geospatial mapping and official census data; travel time is computed with a web mapping tool on one pre-fixed route, which is a weak and non-independent measure. No external benchmarks cited.
- Internal inconsistencies: the energy-savings indicator has a ratio formula but an absolute monetary target (exposed to currency and tariff change); the solar-capacity indicator has a ratio formula but a capacity target; the flood indicator formula counts spots that received works, not flood occurrences; the camera indicator is mapped to women's protection and public-security objectives although it measures an asset. Targets run to a horizon several years beyond the 5-year execution.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual post-project operation and maintenance cost is quantified per component (largest for the sustainability component, small for infrastructure and management); responsible secretariats named; costs to be incorporated in municipal PPA, LDO and LOA with funding source and expense category, with service levels and preventive/corrective schedules; no legal act yet and no itemisation below component level.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. 6 risks with impact/probability and mitigations: expropriation delays, environmental licensing delays (high impact, low probability), political continuity, procurement delays and adverse weather (both high impact, medium probability), low public acceptance. Licensing flagged for parks in protected riparian areas, drainage and stream crossings; no per-work licensing status. Land acquisition is a financed product.
- Counterpart (20%): to be secured; stated source is municipal tax revenue, with municipal PPA inclusion cited; authorising law and counter-guarantee on constitutional revenues to be assessed by STN at the limits-verification stage. Creditor consent letter annexed.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Programme coordination unit is to be created by decree under the chief-of-staff secretariat, staffed by a small team that has run two earlier multilateral-funded programmes; several municipal bodies support with a RACI matrix; preparation stage mixed (some basic and executive designs, some only preliminary studies or design tenders published).
