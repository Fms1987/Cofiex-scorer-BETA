## Resumo
- Policy-based debt restructuring (IBRD development policy loan, programmatic, a later operation in a series) for a state government. Whole loan is a single financed item: proceeds prepay previously guaranteed domestic bank credit lines, to cut annual debt service and free fiscal space; no investment and no counterpart (restructuring exemption cited).
- Delivery is by policy actions, not works: roughly 15 laws, decrees and institutional steps, grouped in a fiscal component (fiscal-responsibility law and council, pension regularity, environmental revenue-sharing rule for municipalities, sustainable-investment prioritisation system) and an environment component (environmental-career law, tougher sanctions, updated deforestation targets, temporary staffing for rural land registry, bioeconomy and carbon-asset decrees).
- Coordinated by the state finance secretariat with several line secretariats; lender provides technical assistance. Disbursement is a single tranche within about a year.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG for a PBL. Two named problems with root causes (current-spending growth; persistent deforestation) lead to solutions and expected results with a clear chain; assumptions are not made explicit, and the link from debt prepayment to green investment depends on how freed fiscal space is used.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified count: 8 indicators, approximately 5 outcome-type (credit-capacity rating, current-savings ratio, deforested area, validated rural registrations, revenue-sharing coverage) and 3 output or milestone-type (forest concession area, a public exam, a temporary selection process).
- Baselines: 7 of 8 defined (0.88). Counted zeros: the revenue-sharing indicator (rule not yet in force) and the two milestone indicators (action not yet taken). Not counted: the forest-concession zero baseline, no explanation given. No baseline carries a reference year (year share 0.0).
- Verification: sources are named for most (Treasury reports, national deforestation monitoring, federal registry bulletin); targets fall in 2025-2026, beyond the roughly one-year execution horizon, which the NT flags as a timing gap.
- Inconsistencies: narrative result promises at least a 30% cut in illegal deforestation and a minimum of a few thousand validated registrations, while the deforestation indicator target is only about 10% below baseline; the savings-ratio target is a marginal change (a few tenths of a point); the CAPAG target is simply to hold the current grade.
