## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (IDB) to a state government for the benefit of its state court system, with counterpart at the 20% minimum from the state treasury.
- Finances institutional modernisation of a judiciary: governance and change management, digital transformation (digitising the physical case stock, AI in case handling, cybersecurity/LGPD, security operations centre), technology infrastructure and integration with the national judicial platform, plus a physical and digital accessibility programme.
- Delivery: direct execution by the court itself through a programme management unit attached to the court presidency, with product leaders and the court's procurement committee to be strengthened.
- Four components (governance, digital transformation, strategic resources, project management); roughly 40% of cost in digital transformation and about 40% in infrastructure and strategic resources; project management is a small share.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis draws on a national judicial council inspection (obsolete technology, last place in governance and IT-governance rankings, understaffed IT, mostly physical case stock); components map to these gaps and to the judiciary strategy, but assumptions are not explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified count: 8 indicators; approximately 6 outcome/performance (access-to-justice index, net congestion rate, IT governance index, quality seal score, productivity of judges and of staff) and approximately 2 output/process (virtualisation share, physical accessibility). Benchmarks are national judicial-council metrics with published formulas or glossary references.
- Baselines: all 8 have a stated value, but one (physical accessibility) is a zero baseline that the CC does not explain, so I count 7 of 8 as defined (about 0.88); none states a reference year (baseline_with_year_share 0).
- Verification: yearly national council data sources exist for most indicators; no explicit verification protocol per indicator. Some targets are modest or move in the intended direction only by a small step.
- Inconsistency: the virtualisation formula divides electronic by physical cases (not by total), and the productivity formula for staff reuses the judge formula label.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Only a general statement that the court can bear post-programme costs, with annual budget provision confirmed through the court's own special fund; no OPEX table for licences, cloud, security operations or staff, despite a heavily technology-based scope.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. A procurement risk list with eight risks and a mitigation each (lender procurement policy knowledge, committee capacity, contractor dependence, contract oversight, discontinuity of the solution); no risk matrix with probability/impact; environmental and resettlement sections empty (not relevant); social section is rhetorical.
- Counterpart: from the state treasury per Res. COFIEX 3/2019; the programme is aligned with the state multi-year plan, but the authorizing law was still a draft, so "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Programme management unit under the court presidency with general, financial and three monitoring coordinations, bimonthly reports to the full court; no prior lender experience stated and the procurement committee needs strengthening.
- Fiscal context: CC states the state has CAPAG A.
