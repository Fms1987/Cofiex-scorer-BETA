## Resumo
Investment loan to a state water/sanitation public company (a mixed-capital, non-dependent state-owned utility), financing expansion and rehabilitation of sewage-collection-and-treatment systems across numerous interior municipalities (many completing works left unfinished by earlier federal/state partnership programs), a single counterpart-funded water-supply treatment-plant capacity expansion, and a set of socio-environmental/institutional actions (a sludge-to-fertilizer pilot, a reforestation offset, social-mobilization and vocational training prioritizing low-income women, and an environmental-education museum upgrade). Delivery is direct, through the utility's own program-management unit. Main components: engineering/administration, civil works, the counterpart water-supply work, and socio-environmental/institutional actions.
