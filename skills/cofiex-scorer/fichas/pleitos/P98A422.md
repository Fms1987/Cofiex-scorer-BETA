## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IBRD to a state government with federal guarantee; counterpart at the 20% minimum of total cost, declared as funded from constitutional tax transfers and state revenues.
- Finances a sustainable and climate-resilient family-farming programme: resilient production systems and support to producer organisations (about a fifth of cost), environmental recovery and rural-road adaptation (about half), research/extension institution upgrading and applied-research grants (about a fifth), project management and governance (under a tenth).
- Delivery model: state agriculture secretariat with a new UGP, subprojects chosen with beneficiaries, partly via cooperation agreements with state agencies; possible use of a state development bank for credit lines.
- Targets family farmers, women, youth and traditional communities; the sponsor declared intent to compete for the environmental/climate sublimit.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree (climate vulnerability, degraded land, reduced research investment, poor rural roads) maps to nine specific objectives and four components; assumptions (beneficiary uptake, extension continuity, counterpart sharing by families) are described but not framed as testable hypotheses.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 9 indicators, approximately 3 outcome-type (adoption share, traditional-community families improving production, satisfaction) and 6 output-type (organisations supported, families and people reached, area restored, road length, women reached); heavy output weighting with no productivity or income outcome.
- Verification mechanism present for all: project M&E system plus intermediate and final result evaluations, geographic information system for restored area; yearly cumulative milestones given for every indicator.
- Baselines: all 9 counted as defined; each is a zero explained as counting only project-supported units (new-asset logic). No reference year stated for any baseline, so year share is zero. Adoption-share baseline study deferred to preparation.
- Internal notes: people-reached indicator is described as counting families; satisfaction target is irregular across years; the women indicator embeds its target in its name. The NT-level SMART view was favourable for most indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. O&M assigned by asset type: family technologies maintained by households (rough yearly maintenance share of the investment given, credit line suggested as source); associative equipment via reserve funds with stated contribution rates; public works (roads, drainage) by municipalities or the state via signed commitment terms; research centres via maintenance plans and institutional budgets. No consolidated OPEX table or legal budget line.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Eight risks with impact/probability and mitigations (contamination, procurement, community engagement, erosion, delay in state authorising law, deforestation, cooperation agreements, under-representation of traditional communities); ESF-type environmental and social instruments referenced; road maintenance works stated as licence-exempt.
- Counterpart: "to be secured" via the pending state authorising law (flagged as a risk of low probability); funding source named but no LOA/PPA line shown.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. New UGP under the agriculture secretariat, with experienced state agencies and prior multilateral-credit experience cited; many partners imply coordination load.
