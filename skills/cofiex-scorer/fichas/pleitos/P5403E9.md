## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from NDB to the Federation, executed by a federal state-owned food-supply company; counterpart at roughly 4% of cost; six-year execution.
- Finances business plans for family-farming cooperatives and associations (mechanisation, value addition, technical assistance, training) tied to several public food-procurement and supply programmes, plus plans and distribution centres for small urban food retailers.
- Delivery: demand-driven, intermediated through accredited operators who prepare and supervise productive and commercial business plans (matching-grant style, with per-plan and per-beneficiary ceilings and operator fees capped as a share of plan funds).
- Four components: management/supervision, productive development, retail supply channels, monitoring/evaluation and studies.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Problem tree (low mechanisation, thin technical assistance, weak aggregation, urban food deserts) maps to six specific objectives and a logical framework annex, but assumptions are not testable and the NT found weak coherence between indicators and objectives.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 9 indicators verified; roughly 6 outcome-type (volume, share of animal-origin and processed products, mechanised and sustainable-practice area, retail sales, women managers) and about 3 output/capacity (training counts for beneficiaries and for the proponent's staff).
- Every target is expressed as "initial year + X%" with no baseline value and no reference year, so baseline_defined_share and baseline_with_year_share are both 0 (no zero baselines counted).
- Verification: a monitoring system is announced for all indicators and data collection via the business plans, but there are no sources, frequencies or formulas for several indicators; several indicators are bare counts (managers, trained staff) without a defined denominator. No wide ranges.
- Internal inconsistencies: objective numbering differs between the objectives list and the indicator fields (some refer to objectives not listed); indicator descriptions are partly terse. Targets are to be assessed as averages across business plans at project end.
- Benchmarks: none cited beyond the PPA strategic areas (several linked per indicator, seven areas overall).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK, narrative only. Transitory-platform logic: beneficiaries progressively take over technical assistance and equipment maintenance, each business plan has a co-financing matrix, pilots/feasibility studies planned, municipal/state agreements for maintaining local public infrastructure. No OPEX/O&M quantification, no budget line or legal backing.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. NT found risk descriptions insufficient (causes and effects); one environmental risk cites land-tenure regularisation as an outcome that is not a project action. No licensing issues raised in the text.
- Counterpart: small (about 4%); no LOA/PPA evidence, to be secured.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE structure on paper (project management team inside a directorate of the executor, central and state coordination committees, local operating offices, a contracted technical manager, ROP to be written after approval), but the NT found no description of institutional roles, no organogram, no demonstration of executor capacity and a responsibility matrix that merely repeats the structure. Partner list is long (federal agencies, public banks, states, municipalities, civil society).
- Preparatory gap: no evidence of the lender's agreement to submission.
