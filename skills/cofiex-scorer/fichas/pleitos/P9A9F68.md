## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IDB to a mid-sized, relatively affluent municipal government, executed directly by the municipal education secretariat; 5-year execution, counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances a municipal basic-education programme: expansion and sustainable rehabilitation of early-childhood and primary school infrastructure (about two thirds of cost), learning quality and recovery (about 10%), science/technology and digital equipment (about 15%), digital management of the education network (about 5%) and programme administration (about 5%).
- Five components; objectives cover access (daycare seats, full-time offer pilots), learning outcomes and equity gaps by race and gender, and digital transformation of teaching and management.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem, solution and seven expected results are mapped to three specific objectives and components; each indicator is tagged with result, objective and PPA strategic area, but assumptions are not made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE (SMART form is strong, but several baselines are undefined). 27 indicators (verified), roughly 70% outcome (learning proficiency, age-grade distortion, early-childhood coverage, racial learning gaps by subject and stage) and the rest output (seats created, devices/units, managers trained); SMART layout (S/M/A/R/T) per indicator with formula and data source; benchmarks are national (SAEB, INEP, IDEB-linked) rather than creditor comparators.
- Baselines: 24 of 27 defined (share 0.889). Zero baselines counted as defined where the CC makes them structural (seats created by the programme, new evaluation model, pilots, processes optimised, managers trained); zero baselines counted as undefined for the teachers' and students' digital-competence indicators and the girls-in-STEM-clubs indicator, which are not explained. 17 of 27 also state a reference year (share 0.630); the programme-created and some zero baselines have no source or year.
- Inconsistencies: some racial-gap baselines date from 2019 while others use 2023; one full-time-offer indicator mixes a large existing base with a small target increase; targets all fall in 2032, beyond the execution period.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurrent costs of the larger network (payroll, building upkeep, meals) are expected to be absorbed by the constitutionally earmarked education revenue share, the basic-education fund, the education salary contribution and federal transfers that grow with enrolment; stated as preliminary estimates, no annual O&M table; savings from reducing outsourced private daycare agreements and from green building design are cited.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Five risks with impact/probability and mitigations (land acquisition, environmental effects of works, social resistance, delay of enabling laws, lender procurement rules); no licensing-status detail and no delivery-schedule risk.
- Counterpart: partly secured. The programme is said to be in the municipal PPA and LOA and in the next PPA cycle's preparation; an authorizing law is among the risks (low probability).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Education secretariat as executor with a UGP to be created by decree, a small multidisciplinary technical staff, supporting secretariats for works, finance, procurement, legal and environment; no fund transfers between bodies.
