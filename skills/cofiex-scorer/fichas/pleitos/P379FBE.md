## Resumo
Investment loan (direct external credit operation) financing urban drainage/flood-control infrastructure built around nature-based solutions, with an added sewage-network sub-component in the final version. Delivery is direct: the borrowing municipal government executes through a dedicated program unit under the works secretariat, with several co-executing and intervening secretariats under a RACI responsibility matrix. Main components: macro-drainage structural works in two river-basin stretches, smaller-scale micro-drainage/urban requalification, program management/supervision, and financial charges. Entity type: municipal government (subnational).

A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG — explicit causal chain from historically flood-prone micro-basins to targeted macro/micro-drainage works and nature-based solutions, to reduced overflow/flood events and improved public health, mobility and local commerce; the NT scored this dimension near its ceiling in both evaluation cycles.
A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE — 4 verified indicators (outcome-type: river/stream-overflow margin in two watercourses, % reduction of flood events in two further neighborhoods), all with stated baseline values (baseline_defined_share=1.0, none a zero baseline), but none state a reference year/period for the baseline (baseline_with_year_share=0.0) and the NT does not describe an ongoing verification/M&E mechanism. Outcome-focused rather than output-heavy (approximate split). No internal inconsistencies found; a sanitation/sewerage sub-component present in the final CC version is described narratively but not covered by a dedicated indicator.
