## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (CAF) to the Union, executed by a federal regional-development state-owned company under a line ministry; no counterpart; 5-year disbursement; long amortisation (about 18 years with a grace period of more than 5 years) at SOFR plus a 2% spread.
- Finances water supply and sanitary sewerage systems (hundreds of systems, urban and rural, in many municipalities of several states), the bulk of cost, plus a socio-environmental strengthening component (environmental education, training, rural sanitation management models).
- Smaller components cover programme management (works supervision, management support, basic designs and studies) and financing charges.
- Delivery is direct by the executing company, working with states and municipalities through technical cooperation terms; operation of the systems is handed over to municipal operators or community associations.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis uses SNIS coverage data and health-notification data; solutions (water and sewer systems, rural management, environmental education) map to expected results (quality of life, child health, fewer waterborne-disease admissions, lower emergency water-truck costs). No explicit assumptions; NT asks for a stronger cost-benefit analysis and area-specific diagnosis.

- Baselines: three stated with values (state-level household coverage and existing management systems); one zero baseline (people reached by education) not explained by the CC, counted undefined, so share 0.75. No reference year stated (year share 0.0).
- No deadlines for targets and no verification mechanism described; household coverage targets are tiny increments over very large state-level baselines.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Urban systems run by municipal autonomous utilities or concessionaires paid by users; rural systems by community associations with cost-sharing among users, following a rural management model proven in another state; legal instruments to bind operators. Maintenance costs not quantified (NT flags absence of values); training has no permanent costs.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Risks listed for legal acts, procurement (failed bids, flawed designs) and environmental impacts with mitigations; expropriation risk called low without support; municipal-level legal, resettlement and environmental-management detail missing. Licensing runs municipality by municipality (state or municipal issuers) with cooperation terms to be signed before works start.
- Counterpart: none (loan covers full cost); NT says budget adequacy cannot be verified because the proponent did not answer the budget-office questions (nature of expenditure, discretionary vs mandatory, offsetting reductions under the fiscal framework, current vs capital).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Executing company has long experience and a works-supervision plus management-support structure; municipal responsibilities undefined; proponent has no external-credit track record in the portfolio system.
