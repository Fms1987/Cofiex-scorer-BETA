## Resumo
- On-lending credit line (investment-type funding) channelled by a federal development bank to micro, small and medium firms and individual entrepreneurs across a macro-region of the country; guarantee from the Union requested.
- Single component and single product: productive credit (fixed investment, machinery, equipment, vehicles, production goods and services; working capital excluded), delivered through the bank's existing indirect product lines and accredited private/public financial agents, which bear end-borrower credit risk.
- Multisector scope, no sector targeting; eligibility criteria and the pipeline of eligible operations are to be fixed jointly with the lender in the operating regulation after the CC stage.
- Counterpart is the bank's own ordinary-resource portfolio of eligible loans (roughly 15-20% of cost), not new budget money; executor is the borrower's existing capital-markets/funding unit supported by its operating areas.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: WEAK. Diagnosis (credit constraints of small firms, regional gap) and general/specific objectives are stated, but no causal chain with testable assumptions; the CC explicitly says indicators, targets, baselines, verification means and assumptions will be set later in the operating regulation and results matrix.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 2 indicators (verified), both outcomes (relative revenue and employment growth of supported firms versus a control group); no output indicator (e.g. credit volume disbursed).
- Baselines: both stated as 0 with no explanation (zero counted as undefined, so 0 of 2 defined, no reference year); both targets are identical placeholder-looking values, not described as tied to a methodology.
- Verification mechanism: control-group design is implied by the indicator wording, but no source, frequency or evaluation method is given; the CC itself calls indicators preliminary (deferred indicator definition).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for an intermediary model. No additional OPEX; executing units already exist inside the bank; funding rests on the bank's diversified resource base (national institutional funds plus market and multilateral borrowing). No O&M table needed; no quantified debt-service source in the CC.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. No matrix; risks described qualitatively (credit risk borne by accredited agents, system-wide default context cited as a percentage). Environmental/social handled by a category C restriction, a per-operation size cap and an exclusion list; no licensing or resettlement risk. Other risks deferred to preparation.
- Counterpart: not a budget item; it is presented as eligible own-resource loans, to be certified to the lender (no LOA/PPA issue).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Experienced borrower with prior lender relationship and existing indirect-lending channel; no dedicated UGP, procedures follow the bank's regular indirect-operation rite; disbursement over 3 years, with a front-loaded schedule.
