## Resumo
Investment operation for a state government borrower, direct implementation through the state science/technology/professional-education secretariat, financed by a regional multilateral lender (IDB). Program to expand and upgrade professional/technical education: dominant component is school infrastructure coverage and quality (construction/retrofit of technical schools, equipment, mobile units, a distance-learning system, and the structuring/bidding of a PPP contract for further school expansion); secondary components cover curriculum adaptation and teacher training (including a bioeconomy/biotechnology specialisation track), management-practice tools, and program administration. Multiple submission attempts exist, with the proposed US-dollar amount and some product-level scope (including a large specialised-institute product present in earlier versions) changing across versions. Counterpart at the 20% minimum.
