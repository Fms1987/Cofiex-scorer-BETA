## Resumo
- Modality: subnational debt restructuring / recomposition of principal financed by an IBRD policy loan (DPL-type), denominated in yen, for a capital municipality.
- Proceeds replace several costly domestic bank loans indexed to the interbank rate (already federally guaranteed, so this is a guarantee substitution) with a longer-tenor loan indexed to an international reference rate, extending the profile by roughly two decades; savings estimated in present-value terms.
- Two components: fiscal sustainability (receives all proceeds; fiscal framework law and fiscal-adjustment trigger) and sustainable urban development (zero funds; non-financial commitments such as master-plan revision, zoning law, sanitation plan and protected-area management plans).
- Executor is the municipal finance secretariat, with urban and infrastructure secretariats owning the non-financial commitments; counterpart waived as a debt restructuring.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Logic from cheaper, longer debt plus fiscal-rule commitments to restored investment capacity and climate-oriented urban policy is coherent for the fiscal pillar; the urban pillar relies on legal instruments whose effect depends on enforcement, with no assumptions made testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE to STRONG. Seven indicators (verified): four fiscal (debt service to net current revenue, CAPAG grade, current savings ratio, property-tax collection) and three urban-environmental (climate-vulnerability index, protected-area management coverage, water-quality bulletin index). Approximate split: nearly all outcome-type, a few are policy-output proxies (management instruments).
- Baselines: all seven carry a stated value with a reference year (share 1.0 for both baseline fields); none is a zero.
- Verification: named public fiscal reports, the federal treasury CAPAG publication and municipal databases/bulletins; the CAPAG methodology is cited, but a CAPAG-grade target raises methodological-discontinuity risk.
- Issues: property-tax target is a relative increment tied to a valuation-table update without rate hikes; the climate-vulnerability and water-quality targets are modest; the fiscal-adjustment indicator is a trigger rule rather than a pure outcome.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no physical assets, so no O&M; sustainability rests on the fiscal framework law, continuous debt monitoring, and the present-value saving from refinancing. Debt service is to be reflected in the budget; PPA coverage is stated as to be included in the next municipal PPA (not yet secured).
- A.3 Risks: four risks (legislative authorisation and normative acts, inter-secretariat coordination, approval of the master plan and zoning law, sanitation plan delays), two rated high impact with medium probability; mitigation is participatory consultation and engagement with the council. No licensing issue since no works.
- Counterpart: waived under the debt-restructuring exception; no counterpart needed. Non-financial counterparts (legal reforms) still depend on council approval, i.e. "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: municipal finance secretariat leads, with prior experience from an earlier operation with the same lender; no dedicated UGP described.
