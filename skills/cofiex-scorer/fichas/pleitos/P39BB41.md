## Resumo
Investment operation with sovereign guarantee for a mixed-capital, state-controlled electricity distribution utility (entity type: state-linked concessionaire, not a direct municipal/state treasury borrower). Direct financing (no on-lending/intermediation) of a grid modernization and expansion program: high-voltage substations and distribution lines (plus associated land acquisition/right-of-way compensation), medium/low-voltage rural feeder upgrades and a smart-metering pilot in the capital, and a technology/innovation module (new operations control center, EV fleet and charging corridor, large-scale battery storage, mobile substations) paired with a workforce gender/diversity/inclusion component (youth apprenticeship and internship cycles). A program engineering/administration/M&E component closes the structure; this is a second-phase program with the same creditor following a prior loan nearing completion.
