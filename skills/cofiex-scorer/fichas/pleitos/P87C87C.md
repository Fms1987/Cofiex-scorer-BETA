## Resumo
Investment operation, direct lending to a state government (subnational entity), continuing a multi-cycle fiscal-modernization program series with the same lender (a third consecutive operation of the same program family). Finances fiscal/tax-administration modernization: strategic fiscal-management tools (planning/process digitalization, knowledge management, an internal gender/diversity policy, ESG-themed tax education); tax administration and litigation (taxpayer-registry integration, active-debt management system, risk-based compliance monitoring, a renewable-energy generation asset at a tax-administration facility); planning, budgeting and public-expenditure management (long-range planning monitoring, accounting/cost-management modernization, actuarial studies); and legal management for fiscal sustainability (judicial-risk management system, machine-learning document classification, judicial-deposit management tool). Delivery is direct, executed by the state finance secretariat and a dedicated, newly expanded project-coordination unit, with technology-agency support.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG — the NT scored the justification/coherence sub-item near ceiling across problem characterization, solution congruence and results coherence, each rated at the maximum for its sub-item; the causal chain from diagnosed fiscal/IT/legal-management weaknesses to four aligned components to expected results (improved governance, tax administration, expenditure management, legal management) is explicit and cross-referenced to diagnostic annexes and a fiscal-management-maturity assessment.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: quantitatively addressed — post-project O&M (mainly IT-infrastructure and new-systems upkeep) is estimated at roughly a low single-digit percent of total project cost per year, to be financed during execution from loan proceeds (technical specifications require knowledge-transfer provisions) and, after closeout, from the state's ordinary budget plus a dedicated tax-administration strengthening fund created by state law, with a specific multi-year-budget-plan program/action cited as the budget vehicle; costs are broken down by component, product and expenditure category in an annex. One of the more complete OPEX/sustainability treatments among comparable pleitos.
- A.3 Risk assessment: comprehensive, organized by category — legal/institutional acts (authorizing law pending state-legislature approval; executive governability over decrees), procurement (delay risks on terms-of-reference drafting and bidding, mitigated via technical commissions, consultant support and retention of an experienced bidding commission from the prior program cycle), land acquisition/resettlement (explicitly not applicable — project uses existing facilities, no physical expansion or property acquisition), and environmental/social (equipment-disposal risk from IT-hardware replacement, mitigated via a sustainable-logistics plan; a tax-reform-related social/regulatory-transition risk, mitigated through communication/outreach). The executing agency's familiarity with the lender's own procurement and safeguard policies is explicitly affirmed and scored at the maximum, based on experience executing two prior cycles of the same lender program.
- Counterpart: secured, legally grounded; header's verified share (9.1%) differs somewhat from the CC-stated share (10.0%) though flagged in the header as a match — likely a rounding/denominator difference (loan-only vs. total-cost base) rather than a substantive discrepancy; noted as UNCERTAIN rather than resolved here.
- A.4 Implementation: rated strong — an explicit responsibility matrix across the finance secretariat, a technology agency, external audit and the lender, plus linked bodies (planning secretariat, state attorney-general's office, administration secretariat); a dedicated, newly expanded project-coordination unit with named functional roles, explicitly strengthened based on lessons from the prior program cycle.
