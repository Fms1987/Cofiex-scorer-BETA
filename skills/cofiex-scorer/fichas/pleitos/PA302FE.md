## Resumo
- Investment loan (NDB) to a state government combining two sectors: state highway rehabilitation (about 40% of cost) and water-supply efficiency/expansion plus sewerage in a set of municipalities and an island district (about 60%, of which sewerage roughly 10%).
- Delivery is direct but split across two state executors (highway department and the state water utility) under one UGP coordinated by the infrastructure secretariat, with a project-management contractor and works supervision (together under 2% of cost).
- Components: (1) highway rehabilitation of two corridors, one metropolitan urban corridor and one long rural corridor; (2) water supply and sewerage (sub-components: supply efficiency, sewerage universalisation); (3) engineering and administration. Several water works in the same list are funded from the utility's own resources, outside the loan.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem diagnosis for both sectors (road condition and safety; water scarcity, intermittent supply and losses; low sewer coverage) maps to the interventions, and the water part follows a state phased water-security plan, but no explicit causal chain or assumptions; the two sectors are only loosely linked.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Verified count: 5 indicators (4 outcome-type, 1 output-type: km rehabilitated). Three cover water and sewerage (coverage, distribution losses, sewer coverage), two cover roads.
- Baselines: all 5 have a stated value (share 1.0), none with a reference year (year share 0.0). No zero baselines.
- Benchmarks: road-condition survey rating (national road survey) and sector-regulation universalisation targets; distribution-loss and coverage formulas are defined.
- Verification mechanism: absent as a column; formulas only. Road project foresees a pavement management system for monitoring.
- Inconsistencies: the road-condition indicator is state-wide, so attribution to two corridors is weak; the km-requalified target looks far larger than the stated scope of the two corridors (roughly a few tens of km), and its baseline is a large value for a programme-level output; the road baseline differs slightly between the indicator table and the diagnosis text. Water indicators are defined on municipalities "contemplated in the programme", including ones funded outside the loan. e riscos

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. Roads: stated to enter the state highway department's routine conservation plan, no cost figure. Water and sewerage: all works are in existing systems, so CC asserts no increase in operating cost; the feasibility study required by the new sanitation framework was approved by the sector regulator and works sit in the utility's regulator-approved investment plan. No quantified O&M table, no tariff or funding projection in the CC.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Narrative sections only, no matrix. Procurement risk low (lender uses national systems); expropriation and resettlement not expected (resettlement framework only if needed); environmental risks listed (erosion, construction pollution, vegetation removal) with environmental control plans; licensing by the state environmental agency, with the mapping and licensing schedule still to be prepared. Design maturity uneven: road designs complete, most water works at conceptual stage or pre-design, some under tender or already under way.
- Counterpart: 20% minimum; PPA programmes and budget actions for roads and water are identified; specific authorising law for the loan still pending (relies on a cooperative legislature); part of the counterpart is described as works executed by the utility with own funds.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Single UGP with sector coordinators for two executors, both with prior experience with multilateral lenders; project-management contractor planned. Cross-sector coordination is in the UGP design rather than a separate body. CC also records an earlier withdrawal at the borrower's request. GTEC provaveis
