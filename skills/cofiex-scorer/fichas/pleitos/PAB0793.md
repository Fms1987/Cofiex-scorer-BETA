## Resumo
- Investment loan (IBRD, 100-200M band) to a capital municipality for an integrated urban-coastal regeneration and tourism-strengthening programme in one deprived-yet-strategic waterfront district; direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat acting as UGP, with several sector secretariats as co-executors.
- Component 1 (roughly 70-80% of cost): public-space requalification of beachfront and structuring access roads (paving, drainage with nature-based solutions, lighting, landscaping, sports and leisure equipment, a large youth sports-and-culture facility). Component 2: housing improvement and risk mitigation in coastal social-interest zones (ZEIS), with resettlement where needed, plus tourism-oriented vocational training and micro-entrepreneur support.
- Component 3 is land provision/regularisation recognised as municipal in-kind counterpart; Component 4 is management, supervision, audit and monitoring. Counterpart at 20% of total cost.
- Terms stated: 24-month grace, 19-year amortisation (228 months total), SOFR-based variable rate plus a spread, commitment fee and front-end fee; federal guarantee.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- **A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE.** Diagnosis-to-solution-to-result narrative is detailed (fragmented public space, climate exposure, housing precarity, low productive inclusion) and each component is tied to objectives, but assumptions are not stated as testable and the intermediate-result chain is descriptive.
- **A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE.** 7 indicators, approximately 3 outcome-type (travel time, regular users of facilities, productive insertion) and 4 output-type (length of public space, households served x2, territories reached). All 7 have a baseline value, source, periodicity and a "why SMART" justification; verification is via municipal registries, georeferenced surveys and inspections (mechanism present).
- Baselines: 7 of 7 with value and reference year (2024 or 2025). Four are zero baselines, counted as defined because the CC explains them (new asset, no programme intervention yet, no equivalent facility existing). baseline_defined_share = 1.0; with-year share = 1.0.
- Inconsistencies: two indicators (households with vulnerability mitigated; households with housing improvements) share the same target and baseline, i.e. near-duplicate. The "territories" indicator is a coarse count of a few areas. The travel-time indicator is measured on a single fixed road segment. Strategic-area tags per indicator are loosely mapped.
- No external benchmark index used; the CC claims a "SMART methodology" and links indicators to the municipal PPA.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- **A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE.** Post-project maintenance cost is estimated as a percentage band of invested works value (not in currency); funding source is the municipal budget through the multi-year maintenance schedule of the infrastructure secretariat, with the youth network budgeting staff and activities for the new facility; additional ideas (property-tax uplift, PPPs for park/furniture upkeep) are aspirational. No legal act or earmarked appropriation cited.
- **A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE.** Matrix of 10 risks with impact/probability, mitigation and owners: socio-environmental engagement, climate/coastal erosion, regulatory/licensing delays, logistics interference, training dropout, cost escalation, data/monitoring weakness, administrative turnover, O&M deterioration, land-tenure/legal insecurity. Licensing not yet started (waiting for basic designs); resettlement handled through social work and land provision.
- Counterpart: municipal 20%, largely met through in-kind public land (Component 3) plus cash; no specific LOA/PPA appropriation shown for the cash part, so "to be secured". The municipal PPA is cited for alignment and debt capacity.
- **A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE.** UGP inside the infrastructure secretariat, career staff, safeguards nucleus, grievance mechanism, procurement via the municipal bidding secretariat, a high-level coordination office for oversight; preparatory actions state conceptual designs done and basic designs under way.
