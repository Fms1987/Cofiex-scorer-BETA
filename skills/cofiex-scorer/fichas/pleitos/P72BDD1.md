## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan in domestic currency (BRL-denominated, IDB) to a state government, within a multi-state federal fiscal-administration modernisation line; fiscal sector, direct execution by the state finance secretariat plus the state attorney-general office.
- Finances tax-reform adaptation of tax administration systems, fiscal governance and IT, risk management, people management, transparency, data analytics and AI, public-spending management, litigation and active-debt management, plus a women-leadership training product.
- Four substantive components (strategic fiscal resources, roughly 30% of cost; tax administration and litigation, roughly 50%; planning/budget/treasury, roughly 10%; legal management for fiscal sustainability, roughly 10%) plus project management and contingency lines.
- Counterpart at about a tenth of cost; the NT notes the Art. 36 II waiver of the 20% minimum for fiscal-management projects.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. General objective, two specific and seven complementary objectives, and a product-problem-solution logic annex; diagnosis based on a lender fiscal-maturity assessment. Assumptions (tax-reform rules arriving on time) appear only as risks, not as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 29 indicators verified, approximately 17 outcome-type (fiscal ratios, processing times, ranking position, efficiency) and approximately 12 output/process-type (training shares, AI adoption, module modernisation, new self-service counts). Each has formula, source and intermediate/final targets; verification mostly by internal secretariat reports, a few sources left blank.
- Baselines: 24 of 29 defined (0.83). Counted as defined: zero baselines for new self-service count and for risks mapped (CC says none mapped). Undefined: cost-to-collect ratio (year only, no value), women-entrepreneur training (blank), and three unexplained zeros (tax-reform skills alignment, AI adoption index, software modernisation index). Reference year explicit for about 8 indicators (0.28); most others say only "year 0".
- Benchmarks: external ranking (federal treasury fiscal-disclosure ranking), lender fiscal-maturity methodology; macro ratios exposed to factors outside project control (NT remark).
- Inconsistencies: tax-revenue index baseline far above its targets; one AI-use indicator has unit "number" but a percentage formula; one training indicator lacks a final target; blank unit in the women-entrepreneur indicator.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurring maintenance and replacement of acquired goods estimated as a flat annual percentage (about 1%) of investment, derived from depreciation, detailed in an annex; funded by the state treasury, as already done for the previous operation. No line-by-line OPEX for licences/hosting.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Seven risks with impact/probability (loan legal approval, procurement/execution, governance change after elections, tax-reform implementation delays, counterpart, environmental, social), mostly low probability; mitigations are generic (OKR-based planning, communication).
- Counterpart: stated at about a tenth of cost with a dedicated risk entry; NT notes the legal waiver of the 20% minimum; no explicit LOA line cited in the text read.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG/ADEQUATE. Experienced programme coordination unit reusing the team of the previous operation, with defined roles, annual operating plan, procurement plan, independent audit and mid-term evaluation; responsibility matrix in an annex.
