## Resumo
- Modality: single-tranche debt restructuring, policy-based in design. Loan proceeds repay, in full, several domestic credit lines already carrying a federal guarantee but at higher cost; new IDB loan on SOFR-based pricing, no grace period, roughly 25-year amortization, no local counterpart (waived as a principal-restructuring operation).
- Borrower is a capital municipality (CAPAG A+ per the letter, with recent primary and nominal deficits and rising debt service). Executed by the mayor's chief-of-staff office through a Program Coordination Unit to be created by decree, with four partner secretariats.
- Three components: (1) fiscal governance and public-spending quality (fiscal rule, data/accounting quality, voluntary tax compliance, urban reoccupation of the historic centre, socially oriented public procurement); (2) inclusive employment and income policies (diversity-oriented qualification, business-opening facilitation, anti-racism and diversity training for civil servants); (3) the debt restructuring itself, which absorbs the whole loan.
- Policy content is legislative and administrative (laws, decrees, systems), not works; the social/economic components are small in resources relative to the debt swap.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree (fiscal / economic / social) maps cleanly to components and specific objectives, with a fifth objective tying the loan to lower financial charges; assumptions are not made explicit, and the causal link from the debt swap to the social outcomes is asserted rather than tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. 11 indicators verified; approximately 6 outcome-type (CAPAG, accounting-quality ranking, voluntary-compliance revenue, share of small suppliers, opening time, jobs) and 5 output-type (inclusive-seal certifications, retrofit project approvals, training counts, vulnerable-audience share, debt-service disbursement).
- Baselines: the letter has no baseline field. Only one indicator states a baseline value (average business-opening time, no reference year); the procurement indicator is relative to a base year but gives no value. No zero baselines counted. Share defined about 0.09, with year 0.0 (judgement; CAPAG/ranking "maintain" targets imply current grades but no baseline is stated).
- Verification: official publications, administrative registries (CAGED, STN rankings, municipal systems) or the coordination unit; no independent evaluation. Targets are mostly single-year (one fiscal year), one runs to a later year; some indicators are explicitly "to be built during the project" (deferred).
- Benchmarks: STN CAPAG methodology and Siconfi quality ranking; link to PPA strategic areas is argued for nine areas, only four scored.
- Inconsistencies: a debt-service indicator is set as an absolute disbursement figure with weak SMART justification; jobs target extends beyond the operation horizon.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: ADEQUATE for a policy operation. Maintenance of systems and policies is said to be funded by annual budget appropriations; no OPEX figures or recurring-cost table. The letter announces a follow-on IDB operation to consolidate results.
- Counterpart: none required (principal restructuring); debt-service savings expected to free budget space. No evidence in the letter of PPA/LOA debt-service lines.
- A.3 Risks: four risks with impact/probability and mitigation (legal approval delays, inter-institutional coordination, social adherence, technical design). Matrix-style but generic; fiscal-trajectory and exchange-rate/interest risks of a USD loan against local-currency revenue are not treated. No licensing risk (no works).
- A.4 Implementation: UCP to be created by decree as a special condition prior to disbursement, coordinator plus M&E specialist, advisory committee meeting twice a year; the municipality has prior experience with multilateral and domestic credit operations. UGP quality adequate but not yet in place.
