## Resumo
- Investment loan (fiscal-modernisation type, from a multilateral bank's standard programme for state finance administrations) to a state government, executed directly by the state finance secretariat through a project coordination unit.
- Four components: fiscal management and transparency (roughly 30% of cost), tax administration and tax litigation including alignment with the consumption-tax reform (roughly half), financial administration and public spending (roughly 15%), plus a small project-management component (monitoring, audit).
- Heavy digital content: IT governance and infrastructure, AI-supported audit and taxpayer registry, online services, budget-system upgrades; small side items on workforce gender equity, solar generation and rainwater reuse at the finance secretariat's buildings.
- Counterpart about 9% of total cost.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Two specific objectives plus complementary objectives mapped to components and products; causal logic is stated by objective but without explicit, testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 14 indicators (verified), approximately 8 outcome-type (online service rate, cost-to-collect, tax revenue to GDP, debt-recovery rate, first-instance judgment rate, business growth, transparency-ranking score, current-savings ratio) and 6 output/process-type (leadership training, solar generation, rainwater storage, strategic-plan fulfilment, tax-reform system integration, foreign-trade portal functionalities). All use a SMART template with formula, unit and source.
- Baselines: all 14 have a stated value and a reference year or period (share 1.0 for both fields). Zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains them as new assets/functionalities or a strategic plan to be built in year 1 (training, solar generation, rainwater storage, plan fulfilment, system integration, trade-portal functionalities).
- Internal inconsistencies: the training indicator states a 40% target in text but its schedule runs to 60%; the current-savings (CAPAG) indicator text gives a final target above baseline while the yearly schedule declines slightly below it; strategic-plan fulfilment has no target definition beyond the schedule. Several side indicators (solar, rainwater, women's leadership) are only weakly tied to the declared fiscal objective.
- Verification: sources named per indicator (official business registry bulletin, utility bills, internal reports, national fiscal-rating rules) but no independent verification schedule; a monitoring-and-evaluation product and external audit are budgeted.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. Annual maintenance/replacement is quantified from an asset-depreciation method (about 1.4% of project cost per year), existing maintenance contracts are listed, and funding is anchored in the state annual budget (LOA) plus a dedicated modernisation fund fed by tax-service fees and created by law.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Matrix with about a dozen risks rated by impact/probability: legal approval of the credit, execution, governance, resistance to change (high/high), investment sustainability, counterpart, process flow, environmental, social, delay in the consumption-tax reform transition, and low project-management experience. Environmental licensing is minor (no major works).
- Counterpart: to be incorporated in the state PPA and LOA, reinforced through the modernisation fund; not shown as already secured.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A project coordination unit staffed by finance-secretariat employees, a dedicated bidding committee, procurement rules of the lender, internal control, RACI matrix and external audit; the risk table itself flags limited experience of the unit and product leaders with complex projects.
