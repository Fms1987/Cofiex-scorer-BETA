## Resumo
- Modality: single investment loan from IDB to a state government with Union guarantee; subnational fiscal-management modernisation; 3-year disbursement, 3-year grace, 25-year total term; counterpart around 10% of the financing plan.
- Finances: four thematic components/axes: fiscal governance and transparency (HR, IT, procurement, a small renewable-energy and facilities element), tax administration and litigation, financial administration and public spending (budget, accounting, debt and cost systems, public-investment/PPP management), and legal management of fiscal assets (credit recovery with analytics).
- Mostly systems, studies, process redesign and capacity building, i.e. soft investments; works are minor (adaptation of inspection posts and facilities).
- Delivery is direct: the state finance secretariat executes through a coordination unit still to be created by decree, with product leaders drawn from three state bodies.
- The CC invokes the COFIEX counterpart waiver for fiscal-management projects, yet a counterpart share appears in the financing plan.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear chain from general objective to four specific objectives to four components and products, with a vertical-logic annex; evidence from an impact evaluation of earlier operations under the same lender line. Causal assumptions are not stated explicitly.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 16 indicators verified (the header heuristic said 17); approximately 13 outcome or performance-type (cost to collect, processing times, litigation timing, revenue recovery) and about 3 output-like (workforce staffing, renewable generation, trained multipliers).
- Each indicator has formula, unit, baseline and target; one general statement fixes a single baseline year and a single target year for all, so no intermediate milestones (NT also notes their absence).
- Baselines: 15 of 16 counted as defined (all with the stated reference year). The zero baseline for sustainable-procurement share was counted (new practice created by the project); the zero baseline for strategic-plan target fulfilment was NOT counted (no explanation).
- Verification mechanism: formulas given, but no data source or responsible party in the CC body (detail deferred to an annex); tagged no_verification.
- Quirks: a zero target for a latency indicator; revenue indicators have a ratio formula but are reported in absolute currency units; NT suggests adding an effectiveness indicator showing citizen-level impact.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Main recurring costs identified (hardware, software licences, system support); during execution these are financed by the loan via terms of reference; afterwards by the state treasury under a maintenance action of the PPA, with an annual need of about 2% of project value (annex). A formal sustainability plan is promised, not yet delivered.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. Only legal and procurement risks are developed (two risks, about six mitigations); expropriation, environmental, social and resettlement sections are blank although there are some works and user-facing technology change (NT scored this item low).
- Counterpart: share around 10% shown in the financing plan; no LOA line or law cited; authorising state law to be sent to the legislature only after COFIEX approval ("to be secured").
- A.4 Implementation: coordination unit to be created by decree (general, administrative-financial and technical coordinators, planning and procurement advisers); borrower cites prior experience with the same lender, but NT reports no credit-operation experience in the last 10 years in SIGS and asks for clearer UGP role definitions.
- Preparatory work: working groups drafting terms of reference; some inspection-modernisation initiatives already under way.
