## Resumo
- Investment loan (external credit operation with federal guarantee) from IBRD to a state government, executed directly by the state agricultural extension agency with partner state bodies; no financial intermediation, but economic grants (subsidies) to farmers, producer organisations and traditional communities through a state agribusiness fund.
- Sustainable rural development in territories prioritised by a socio-environmental agrarian vulnerability index, with participatory territorial development plans as the entry point; territories and beneficiaries are selected during execution.
- Three components: resilient landscapes and sustainable production (degraded-area recovery, sustainable systems, biological inputs, environmental regularisation, water storage, rural sanitation; roughly 45% of cost), value-chain support and infrastructure for family-farming organisations and traditional communities (roughly 40%), and institutional strengthening, management and communication (roughly 15%).
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum; 6-year disbursement horizon; 25-year total term with 5 grace; variable rate (SOFR plus fixed spread).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear problem diagnosis (widespread degraded pasture, weak sanitation and connectivity, exclusion of women and traditional peoples), a long list of expected results and twelve-odd specific objectives, and a narrative causal logic from participatory territorial plans to adoption of sustainable practices, climate resilience and income; assumptions are not made explicit or testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 12 indicators verified; approximately 5 outcome-type (adoption of sustainable technologies, biological inputs, environmental regularisation, degraded-area recovery, sales growth of supported organisations) vs approximately 7 output-type (territorial plans, area under practices, water-storage and sanitation infrastructure, community networks, community projects, management systems). Each has formula, target and data source (extension agency monitoring reports, field records, organisation financial reports); no independent third-party verification stated.
- Baselines: 11 of 12 carry a zero baseline that the CC explains (situation before territories are prioritised / without project support), counted as defined under the v3 rule; the sales-growth indicator has a placeholder 0% to be set in an initial diagnosis, counted undefined. baseline_defined_share 0.92; no baseline states a reference year, so with-year share 0.
- Targets are point values with annual phasing for the first indicator; no wide ranges. Deferred element: beneficiary territories and organisations are identified only after approval, so every baseline depends on later diagnosis.
- Benchmarks: no external comparators. Internal note: the CC's own summary of beneficiary and area targets is output-heavy, with weak linkage from outputs to productivity or income outcomes (only the sales indicator captures income).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. No O&M or OPEX quantification; continued technical assistance by the extension agency and the land agency is to be covered by their annual budgets and the state PPA actions, and beneficiaries are expected to maintain assets with own resources, supported by the state environmental-services payment scheme and the agribusiness fund. Legal backing for grants exists in the fund's law and decrees.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Five risk blocks (regulatory/institutional, procurement, environmental, social, expropriation) with impact/probability ratings (mostly low, procurement medium) and mitigations; strong sector content (soil erosion, aquatic ecosystems, effluents, water-use conflict, cultural loss). Licensing: products needing environmental licences and water-use permits are listed with issuing bodies, but responsibility rests with beneficiaries and status is deferred to execution. Lender safeguard documents (environmental and social, indigenous peoples) are to be prepared. No resettlement expected. Regulatory risk rated low although the authorising law and project decrees are still to be issued.
- Counterpart: current-year budget extract and state PPA annexed with the programme actions already included; authorising law and operating manual to be secured after COFIEX approval.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Large regional extension agency with a state-wide regional network, thematic technical groups, and two prior lender-financed rural projects; a project management support firm is to be contracted and a UGP is to be created by resolution. Own-procurement capacity flagged as a risk.
