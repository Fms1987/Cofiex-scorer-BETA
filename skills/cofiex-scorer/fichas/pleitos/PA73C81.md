## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan structured as a results-based programme (NT calls it a results-based model), sovereign borrower with Union guarantee; nationwide scope with no geographic detail.
- Finances institutional strengthening of a federal public-security/justice ministry: organised-crime control, body-worn cameras and use-of-force protocol, a national training institute, drug and migration policy reform, applied research.
- Second component induces subnational action: a municipal crime-prevention programme with community-service centres (roughly 40% of cost) and prison-system effectiveness (monitoring, resocialisation, post-release services).
- Delivery: centralised procurement by the ministry's own coordination unit; assets are later transferred to selected municipalities that meet readiness criteria (criteria not yet defined). Entity type: direct federal administration, no prior external-credit experience. Admin/evaluation/audit about 1-2% of cost.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Three-problem diagnosis (concentrated lethal violence, violent illegal markets, recidivism) maps cleanly to two components and expected results, with cited international evidence; no explicit testable assumptions or results chain beyond that.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 14 indicators verified (header guess of 15 was wrong); approximately 10 outcome/impact versus 4 output-type. Several rely on treatment-vs-control impact evaluations; formulas given, but the CC table has no verification-source column (NT says sources are official and verification is indicated).
- Baselines: 9 of 14 defined (0.64). Five zero baselines counted as undefined because the CC does not explain them (three treatment-control differences, evaluation count, programme-completion measure). No reference year on any baseline (year share 0).
- Internal inconsistencies: youth homicide indicator labelled one age band but formula uses a wider one; several count-type measures carry a percentage unit. Targets have end dates but no intermediate trajectory (NT).
- Benchmarks: official national crime, prison and justice statistics; NT rates indicator selection as excellent.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. O&M cost not quantified (NT confirms). Funding rests on existing sector funds, to be reviewed, plus the ministry budget and the upcoming PPA programmes; subnational entities are to absorb running costs under a future instrument.
- Counterpart (about 1.4% of cost, far below the usual minimum) is to come from the ministry's annual budget; PPA programmes still being drafted, so "to be secured".
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK overall. Detailed procurement risk matrix with mitigations (incl. non-adherence of municipalities, executor capacity); legal-acts risk only names the authorising law; environmental, expropriation and resettlement fields blank despite construction of centres.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE on paper. Coordination unit created by ministerial act plus a strategic committee of the ministry's secretariats and a responsibility matrix; operational norm pending; NT notes no knowledge of lender procurement/safeguard policies and no external-credit track record.
- Preparation maturity: NT finds generic prior-action information; partnerships with target municipalities and executive designs not yet in place.
