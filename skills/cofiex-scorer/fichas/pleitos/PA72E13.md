## Resumo
- Modality: federal investment loan co-financed in parallel by two multilateral lenders (roughly half each), federal ministry as executor, no counterpart; ticket in the top band.
- Finances credit access for the open-broadcast TV sector to adopt a next-generation broadcasting standard, plus applications for accessibility, digital public services, emergency alerts and civic participation.
- Delivery is intermediated: external funds capitalise a sector universalisation fund, which on-lends through accredited financial agents (guarantee mechanisms, bank and non-bank credit, senior fund-quota subscriptions); credits carry no federal guarantee.
- Two components: adoption financing (about 95% of cost) and an applications/innovation credit window (about 5%).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Five problem-solution-result-objective chains, each tied to a strategic area, but the link from credit availability to actual user outcomes (receiver adoption, usage of accessible features) rests on untested assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 indicators, approximately 2 outcome-type (users with disabilities served, alert coverage of at-risk population) and 3 output/financial (credit volume contracted, signal population coverage, number of public services enabled). Output-heavy and some targets are tied to the loan size itself (credit volume target equals the whole operation).
- Baselines: all 5 are zero with a reference year; counted as defined because the standard, the credit line and the services are new (CC does not explain each zero individually, judged from context).
- Verification: sources named (national statistics, household ICT survey, regulator and ministry coverage reports, civil-defence records, bank administrative records); no independent evaluation planned.
- Internal inconsistencies: the alert-coverage target is stated as full coverage of the at-risk population while the narrative derives about 70% from funding sufficiency; the coverage targets are sized to a funding-sufficiency scenario limited to the largest metropolitan areas, even though the CC says access is not restricted to them; indicator-to-strategic-area links in the CC differ from those the NT accepted. Targets for services and disability users are modest relative to universes.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Claims no incremental recurrent costs and a revolving structure (repayments re-fund the credit instruments), but no OPEX table; the budget action for the funds is yet to be created in the fund's budget unit. Loan terms are long (30-year total, 5-year grace for the one lender detailed).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Matrix with 3 risks (domestic equipment supply capacity and cost, credit allocated to low-capacity firms, adverse macro conditions), with mitigations tied to R&D support, a production-process policy, eligibility criteria and the rate subsidy. No licensing or safeguards discussion; no data-protection risk despite government-platform integration.
- Counterpart: none required (0%); sector fund provides the governance, with a managing council by statute.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE/WEAK. UGP composition and institutional location are to be defined during preparation; sector staff are experienced in technology transitions, but the ministry has not contracted external credit in a decade, and the financial agents and instruments are not yet created.
