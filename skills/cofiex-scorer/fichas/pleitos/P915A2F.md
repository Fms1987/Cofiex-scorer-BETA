## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type external credit (IDB, 100-200M USD band) in the Subnational-Fiscal sublimit, with counterpart at roughly 9% of total cost; resources are meant for fiscal-administration modernisation under a federally proposed programme line.
- Financed: institutional and technological modernisation of a state finance department and its legal-defence office (governance, people management, IT, transparency, tax administration, budget/accounting/spend management, litigation and tax-debt collection) plus adaptation to the consumption-tax reform.
- Four components: strategic resources of fiscal management; tax administration and tax litigation; planning, budget, financial management and public spending; legal management for fiscal sustainability. Products are "models" or systems (about two dozen), with IT and tax-reform adaptation absorbing the largest cost shares (IT roughly a quarter of cost).
- Delivery model: direct, centralised execution by the state finance department (UCP inside it), no transfers to other bodies; borrower is a state government with Union guarantee. Entity type: subnational executive, repeat borrower under this credit line (third operation of a series).
- Objective hierarchy: one general objective, two specific objectives (own-revenue management; spending management) and seven complementary objectives (governance, tax reform, productivity, spending efficiency, digital transformation, transparency, environmental sustainability).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. A diagnosis (maturity assessment by the lender, a dozen numbered challenges) is mapped product-by-product to solutions and expected results, with a problem-solution-results matrix annexed and a general > specific > complementary objective tree. No explicit, testable assumptions; impact is argued by reference to a lender impact evaluation of earlier operations in the same line.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 34 indicators verified (version 3); approximately one third outcome-level (compliance, tax-take share, transparency/accounting rankings, fiscal productivity, budget execution accuracy, case-resolution time), the rest output/process or capacity indices (training shares, maturity scores, modules modernised, publications).
- Verification: a named source is given for every indicator (tax-administration systems, state audit court, STN ranking, training school, project portfolio system). Benchmarks used: federal audit-court governance index, lender IT-maturity score, OECD digital maturity model, STN ranking.
- Baselines (v3 rule): 16 of 34 defined, share about 0.47. Undefined: 5 "inexistente"/"not yet calculated"; 13 further zero baselines the CC does not explain, treated as undefined; the one zero that the CC explains (reform services not started) is counted as defined. Only 3 baselines carry a reference year, share about 0.09.
- Inconsistencies: one pilot-derived baseline (27% in class A) vs a description that gives 55% in the lowest class; two ranking indicators start at or near the ceiling (A to A; transparency index near 98%); a time indicator is expressed in "%"; a concentration index is a relative-change measure whose baseline is zero by construction; some targets are categorical bands ("intermediate", "improved"); two later-added productivity/competitiveness indicators have a zero baseline and are weakly tied to project outputs.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurring cost estimated at about 1.4% of project value per year for hardware, software and infrastructure upkeep (annexed cost sheet and maintenance plan), to be met from the state Treasury as already done for the previous operation; no category-by-year table (licences, hosting, staff) and no cited budget line for post-project IT run costs.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Seven risks with impact/probability: procurement delays and tax-reform implementation difficulty rated high/high; management change, discontinuity of products, environmental impacts, legislative authorisation and macro-fiscal deterioration rated lower. Mitigations are generic (training, OKR-based planning, fiscal-risk annex in the budget guidelines). No licensing needed (only minor refurbishment; waste and e-waste plans).
- Counterpart: amount stated (roughly 9% of total); budget action and strategic plan alignment cited, but funding line/LOA entry not evidenced in the CC text. Authorising state law still "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Centralised UCP inside the finance department with a team that ran the earlier operations in the line; legal-defence office as co-executor; partner secretariats. A post-GTEC institutional-arrangement annex is listed.
