## Resumo
- Investment-type operation (IPF) financed by IBRD: a state-government fiscal/public-management-efficiency program (Progestão-type), direct execution by the state administration secretariat, no intermediation or sub-loans.
- Finances management-system and process reforms (not physical works): HR/payroll, pension/actuarial management, public procurement, public-investment governance, asset/real-estate management, plus two strategic-sector finance systems (health, social assistance).
- Delivery model is direct: a single disbursement account, no transfers to municipalities or sub-executing entities; co-executing secretariats handle only technical implementation.
- Entity type: a large, low-density, majority-low/very-low-HDI-municipality state government with CAPAG B (current-savings indicator the weak link) and above-national-average rigidity in obligatory spending.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE but imprecise. Post-project O&M for the new systems is estimated at a band of 15–25% of implementation cost (roughly a fifth as the reference figure), to be absorbed by the state budget after closing; the NT (second version) notes the mutuário later refined this to "up to ~22%," still described by the NT as estimated rather than precise.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: sector/procurement risk is addressed (vendor-dependency and legal risk from replacing legacy systems, with mitigations via specialized procurement support and market studies) and legal/institutional risk (pending state-legislature authorization law) is flagged with mitigation in progress. No licensing, resettlement or physical-works risk applies — explicitly "not applicable" (consultancy, non-consultancy services and goods only; no works).
- A.4 Implementation (UGP): a minimum UGP staffing is defined (general, financial-management, procurement, technical and IT coordinators) housed in the executing secretariat; the first-version NT found institutional-arrangement information insufficiently specific (no stated experience), improving by the second version with more detail on team composition and sector-plan alignment.
- Counterpart: minimum 10% per the applicable Cofiex resolution for this credit line, sourced from the state treasury; legislative authorization to contract the loan had been formally requested to the state legislature at CC stage but not yet enacted — counterpart is "to be secured" rather than legislatively locked in.
