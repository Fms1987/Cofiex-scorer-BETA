## Resumo
- Investment-type operation (IPF) financed by IBRD: a state-government fiscal/public-management-efficiency program (Progestão-type), prepared while the state operates under its first Fiscal Recovery Regime (PEF/RRF); direct execution, no intermediation or sub-loans.
- Finances management-system reforms (not physical works): HR/payroll, pension management, public-procurement governance, public-investment/asset management, plus three strategic-sector finance systems (health, education, social assistance).
- Delivery model is direct, run through a Steering Committee, a central UGP and per-agency implementing units; no resource transfers to municipalities.
- Entity type: a large, high-HDI state government with CAPAG D (worst payment-capacity band) at the time of the request, which motivated the RRF adherence process referenced in the diagnosis.
