## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending credit line from NDB to a state development bank (a state-owned non-dependent financial institution) with Union guarantee; no counterpart declared; 5-year availability period.
- The bank would relend, in local currency with FX hedged by swaps, to municipalities (urban mobility and sanitation) and to private firms (renewable energy and road-fleet decarbonisation); the lender's counter-guarantee to the Union is offered via receivables or liquid securities.
- Structure: one component, almost entirely the credit line (about 96%) plus a small technical-assistance subcomponent funding project-structuring consultancy for municipalities (about 4%).
- Delivery: intermediated; the bank acts as indirect executor, sub-borrowers are selected later under eligibility criteria of the line; potential clients said to be identified.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem diagnosis by theme (unpaved roads, sanitation targets, costly electricity and non-renewable matrix, transport emissions) leads to a long-term funding line; the link from credit availability to development outcomes is asserted, not tested (market-failure argument about long tenors).
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified count: 3 indicators, all output-type (number of municipalities supported, number of firms financed in renewable energy, number of firms financed in fleet decarbonisation); no outcome indicator (no emissions, coverage or energy result).
- Baselines: two have a stated value; the third baseline is zero with no explanation, counted as undefined (share 0.67). No reference year/period in any baseline (year share 0). No verification source or method; no intermediate milestones.
- Targets are expressed as maxima ("up to") and are numerically tied to the sub-borrower pipeline; the product table also uses a generic unit count. No benchmarks used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for an intermediary. The bank has a project-monitoring unit and a credit-collection unit; states it carries no maintenance cost since it only lends; sub-borrower O&M is not addressed. Funding of the line is backed by a state budget-guidelines provision allowing external funding for municipalities and SMEs.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Legal, contracting (back-to-back credit instruments, sanctions, exclusion lists) and FX risk (long-term swaps being negotiated) are covered; environmental and social safeguards rely on the bank's own socio-environmental policy and licence-regularity checks; expropriation and resettlement sections are blank. No formal risk matrix.
- Counterpart: none declared; the counter-guarantee for the Union is proposed from receivables or liquid securities (to be secured).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Experienced credit team but short track record in external funding (one prior multilateral operation); organisational and risk-methodology upgrades ongoing; no dedicated project unit described.
