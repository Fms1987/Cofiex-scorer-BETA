## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan with an embedded debt-refinancing component, CAF-funded, to a border municipality; counterpart at the 20% minimum; 17.5-year term with 5-year grace, SOFR plus roughly 2% spread, commitment and financing fees.
- Roughly half of the cost refinances an existing external loan of the same municipality (to reshape the repayment profile after a revenue drop); roughly 40% finances urban works (road paving with stormwater macro-drainage in peripheral neighbourhoods, completion of a border-strip public-space urbanisation); small shares go to a culture/entrepreneurship centre with training programmes, a women's health centre, and project management and supervision.
- Delivery: direct municipal execution by a project execution unit under the works secretariat, with contracted builders and supervision; multi-sector, multi-theme design (mobility, drainage, leisure, health, jobs, fiscal).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Problems are described per theme and each maps to a component and a specific objective, but there is no integrated causal chain across the many themes and no testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified 7 indicators, approximately 6 outcome-type and 1 poorly fitting; every indicator has a baseline value (1.0), 6 of 7 with a reference year (0.86; the parks indicator has none). Verification sources named (national sanitation information system, municipal territorial and finance records, national statistics).
- Targets are very small increments (e.g. parks, labour-market and women's-care indicators move by a few hundredths), so the NT judged impact reduced for several areas; target dates are missing for most indicators.
- Inconsistencies: the R&D-spend-to-GDP indicator is mapped to the governance objective without any matching component; the occupied-population indicator is described as an unemployment measure but formulated as an index; units and formulas are confused (flood indicator counts events but is expressed as a share of properties; parks area per inhabitant expressed as a percentage); the paved-network baseline is already high.
- Benchmarks: no comparison benchmark used for targets (the header keyword flag likely comes from the diagnosis text); no results for the refinancing component other than a liquidity ratio.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual maintenance cost is quantified per component (roads/drainage, public space, programmes, project close-out) with funding from municipal budget lines (property tax, public-lighting contribution, agreements); refinancing said to add no new operating cost and to be monitored via CAPAG and net debt. Legal backing: LOA/PPA inclusion claimed.
- Counterpart: described as budgeted in the LOA and PPA; counterpart flows start late in the schedule (works) and run in parallel with management; "to be secured" through the authorizing law, which is itself listed as a risk.
- A.3 Risks: 6 risks with impact/probability and mitigation (authorizing law, failed tenders, weak engineering designs, environmental licensing of drainage works, social disruption, FX and input-cost variation). Licensing addressed (mostly issued by the municipal environmental body); engineering designs still being prepared for works; no climate-risk or debt-sustainability risk beyond FX.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Project execution unit with specialised consultants, supervision (technical, environmental, social) and external audit; steering committee including the legislature and the lender; RACI matrix "to be elaborated"; a prior multilateral loan is in execution by the same municipality, supporting some experience.
