Finances an integrated urban-inclusion and local-economic-development program combining: workforce training, entrepreneurship support and digital-inclusion courses; urban-mobility infrastructure connecting underserved peripheral neighborhoods plus a historic-center pedestrianization/accessibility sub-set (one asset explicitly complementary to an already-financed operation in the same area); and municipal digital-government/smart-neighborhood modernization (cloud platforms, e-government tools, a pilot "smart neighborhood").
