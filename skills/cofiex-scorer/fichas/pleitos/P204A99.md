## Resumo
- Investment programme for a mid-sized municipality, CAF-funded, counterpart at the 20% minimum; direct execution by a new dedicated UGP reporting to the mayor's office, with a steering committee and technical commission across secretariats.
- Seven components: environmental sustainability and drainage (nature-based solutions, water bodies, slope stabilisation), housing and resettlement of at-risk families, public spaces and parks, urban mobility, economic development (industrial and agrifood infrastructure, markets), institutional strengthening (administrative centre, integrated systems, training, sanitation plan), and programme management.
- Integrated urban development profile with a strong resilience and resettlement angle; the proposal states it is the largest programme the municipality has run.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Six expected-result narratives (resilience, housing, public spaces, mobility, economy, institutional) linked to specific objectives, components and PPA objectives, with plausible but untested assumptions (e.g. firms attracted to the industrial site, communities adopting maintenance).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 8 indicators verified, nearly all outcome-type (precarious housing, flood hot-spots, water-quality index, access to qualified green areas, access to qualified road infrastructure, citizen satisfaction, jobs and firms, share of water bodies in good condition); output detail (resettled families, km of works) is in the text but mostly outside the indicator list.
- Baselines: 6 of 8 defined with a reference year (share 0.75): flood hot-spots, water-quality index, green-area access, road-access share, satisfaction score, and the employment indicator (counted: unoccupied site and unimplemented agrifood centre explained as new assets, year given in the temporal criterion). Undefined: indicator 1 (baseline described only as families still in risk areas, no value; the name measures a population share but the description counts resettled families) and the water-body indicator ("no current IQA measurement").
- Verification mechanisms present and detailed for all indicators (GIS mapping, civil-defence records, laboratory sampling, census data, RAIS/CAGED, annual satisfaction survey). Benchmarks: national PPA key indicators cited for two indicators; IQA/CONAMA protocol; WHO-type proximity radius for green areas.
- Inconsistencies: indicator 1 name vs description; the employment indicator bundles three measures (firms, producers, jobs); preliminary job potential in the narrative is stated as a very wide range, not a point target; reference years for baselines differ (2024 vs 2025) across indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. O&M assigned by asset to secretariats, with preliminary annual O&M estimates in BRL by asset type, self-financing via fees for the agrifood centre and markets, concession options, O&M manuals required from contractors; no dedicated fund or legal instrument, and the capacity argument rests on projected revenue growth. CAPAG B is cited as the debt-capacity basis.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. Eight risks with impact/probability and mitigations: political discontinuity, schedule, social resistance/resettlement, maintenance sustainability, engineering, execution capacity, environmental (state/municipal licensing before works), expropriation. A resettlement plan, grievance mechanism and social safeguards are promised before removals.
- Counterpart: 20% minimum, to be provided by the municipality with a specific account; no LOA/PPA line or authorising law shown ("to be secured"). Municipal PPA attached.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP to be created by decree (not yet existing), minimal technical team plus consultants, steering committee and technical commission by decree; no prior operation of this size, acknowledged in the risk table.
