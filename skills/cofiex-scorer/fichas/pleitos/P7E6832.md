## Resumo
- Modality: PBL/DPL-style single-tranche loan from IBRD used to restructure and refinance existing domestic-bank debt (not federally guaranteed), swapping a CDI-indexed cost for SOFR plus a spread, with a long term and no grace period; the new loan itself would carry the Union guarantee. Entity type: a state government (direct administration); executor is the state finance secretariat.
- Almost all of the cost sits in one debt-restructuring product; the fiscal-sustainability and environmental-sustainability components carry zero budget and are non-financial policy conditions (legal acts) agreed with the lender, split into a fiscal pillar and a climate/green-growth pillar. Disbursement after the conditions are met.
- No counterpart (exempt as debt restructuring under the counterpart rule); estimated first-year saving stated as roughly a quarter of the restructured stock in present-value terms.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Causal logic from refinancing (lower cost, longer maturity) to fiscal space, with policy conditions on fiscal and climate reform; assumptions (legislative approval, revenue yield) are implicit, not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Verified count: 9 indicators in the judged version (the NT lists 10 in other versions), approximately all output/policy-milestone rather than outcome; no indicator for the debt-service saving in the judged version, and no explicit verification mechanism beyond the lender checking the conditions.
- Baselines: 6 of 9 have a stated non-zero value (primary result, personnel spending, asset revenue, tax revenue, clean-energy investment, conservation area). Three zero baselines (climate-proofing share, energy-efficiency share, concession tenders) NOT counted because the CC does not explain the zero. No indicator states a reference year/period (NT flags this repeatedly), hence year share 0.
- Inconsistency: the personnel-spending baseline equals its target in the table, while the narrative cites a different level and a stabilisation aim; targets are 2025-dated but baselines undated.
- Benchmarks: none cited; framing uses fiscal ratios (revenue-ratio, debt-limit).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: proponent states no additional O&M or staffing cost; the NT questions this because new environmental regulation and concessions imply enforcement costs. Debt service is already in the budget laws; the debt-service saving is the funding logic. OPEX not quantified.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: reflects legal-act risk (state legislature authorisation, Senate approval, majority claimed), FX risk and delay risk; environmental/social risk sections were first inadequate (lender safeguard compliance rather than project impacts) and improved in later version. No contracting, expropriation or resettlement risk (none applicable). Licensing: only a regulatory-simplification measure, no physical works.
- Counterpart: not required (exempt); the non-financial counterpart is a set of legal acts still in preparation (draft stage, to be secured before disbursement). Authorising state law and Senate resolution pending.
- A.4 Implementation: finance secretariat as executor with the civil-house and environment bodies for the second pillar; responsibility matrix at secretariat level, improved in later version; executor experience with external credit rated modest by NT.
