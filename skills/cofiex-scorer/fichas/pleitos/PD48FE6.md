## Resumo
Results-based (PBR/DLI) operation with a subnational development bank, structured around disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) and key performance indicators (KPIs). Financing is channelled through the bank to public- and private-sector sub-borrowers (intermediated delivery), covering decarbonization-linked credit lines and a new climate-resilience credit line, with a dedicated sub-stream for vulnerable borrowers (women-led, low-HDI, minority-led). An institutional-strengthening component funds internal capacity-building and risk-monitoring tools at the entity itself. No counterpart is foreseen given the entity's nature as a financial intermediary.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG — explicit causal chain from climate-risk diagnosis (extreme events, credit-access constraints for smaller/vulnerable borrowers) to decarbonization and resilience credit lines to reduced financed emissions, increased resilience and reduced inequality, with testable assumptions tied to vulnerable-group targeting.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE — 11 VERIFIED indicators (mix of DLIs driving disbursement and KPIs), approximately output-heavy (disbursement volumes, counts of municipalities/firms financed, staff trained) with one clearer outcome-level indicator (GHG emissions avoided).
- All 11 indicators have a stated baseline value; roughly 64% also state a reference year/period (mostly 2022 institutional data). The remaining baselines are explained zeros for genuinely new products/lines (new credit line, new taxonomy update, new sustainability-linked instrument), consistent with the v3 "defined zero" rule.
- Verification mechanism: institutional self-reporting/internal monitoring by the bank is described; no independent third-party audit or external verification source is specified per indicator.
- Benchmarks used: entity's own prior-year (2022) disbursement and emissions data; no external sectoral benchmark cited.
- No wide-range targets observed; no deferred indicators.
- Internal inconsistency to flag: the NT notes the proponent scored zero on prior-contract experience (no signed SIGS contracts), offset by full marks on portfolio-performance score since that criterion was deemed not applicable.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: O&M/OPEX is not itemized in detail, but the CC explains this as inherent to the entity's role as a financial intermediary (it does not build or operate assets), with capacity-building/system costs embedded in the institutional-strengthening component; funding source for continuity is the bank's own balance sheet and credit policies, aligned with its multi-year strategic plan.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: a risk/mitigation narrative is present (legal, environmental, social) rather than a formal numbered matrix; the proponent states it will not finance operations involving resettlement, and will only contract once formal legal/licensing steps are resolved; environmental and social safeguard screening is described, aligned with international ESG practice.
- Counterpart: none required — the CC states explicitly that, given the operation's nature and the borrower's institutional structure, no counterpart is foreseen (consistent with header counterpart_pct=0.0).
- A.4 Implementation: delivery relies on the bank's existing operational structure and technical team, with institutional-strengthening resources for governance, risk-monitoring systems and socio-environmental questionnaires; the NT flags limited track record specifically in externally-guaranteed operations (no prior SIGS-registered contracts), though overall institutional capacity is otherwise assessed as adequate.
