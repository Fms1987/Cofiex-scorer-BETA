## Resumo
Investment operation for a capital-sized municipal borrower, direct implementation through the municipal works secretariat, financed by a regional multilateral lender (CAF). Multi-theme program whose largest single component is social infrastructure and income/employment generation in socially vulnerable areas (nearly half of program value), followed by urban mobility/accessibility (road requalification and a smart traffic-signal system), basic sanitation/macro-drainage, and environment/water-resources recovery. Minor components for program management and financial charges. Counterpart at the 20% minimum, combining earmarked tax revenue and land already linked to the program.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK — the CC describes O&M actions and gives cost estimates per solution with the responsible municipal secretariat named for each, including some expected cost offsets (e.g., drainage-maintenance cost increase partly compensated by avoided flood-damage costs). However, NT explicitly states the proponent fails to present instruments or measures that actually guarantee financial sustainability, without indicating the source of the resources needed — OPEX is quantified but no confirmed funding source or legal backing. A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG — NT rates both prior-action maturity and risk identification near ceiling, covering legal risk (authorizing law still at draft-bill stage, mitigated through engagement with the municipal legislature), procurement risk, environmental risk (licensing under a digital process, mitigation plan tied to licensing), and social risk (disruption during works, a local social team to monitor impacts); the lender's environmental and social safeguard policies are described as known to the municipality. One gap flagged by NT: no resettlement or expropriation is foreseen, but the proponent is noted as failing to consider the expropriation risks inherent to works execution and corresponding mitigations. Counterpart at the 20% minimum, using earmarked tax revenue plus land tied to the program per the governing counterpart resolution; NT flags that the land was not independently appraised. A.4 Implementation: STRONG — NT notes an institutional-responsibility (RACI-type) matrix was presented, detailing required activities and responsible sectors; a UGP organisation chart was not presented but is stated to be structured per the lender's guidance once established.
