## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type credit line (IBRD) on-lent by a federal state-owned financial institution to state and municipal governments; no counterpart; 3-year disbursement; Union guarantee requested.
- Finances digital public infrastructure (broadband connectivity and data processing/storage, the large majority of cost), digital service catalogues (roughly a third), plus small shares for digital-skills training and technical assistance.
- Delivery is intermediated: sub-projects are brought by subnational borrowers on demand and screened by the lender on credit risk and economic-financial viability; no named pipeline at CC stage.
- Borrower profile: a first-time external-credit executor per the NT's portfolio lookup; target audience is mostly small municipalities with scarce budgets.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (leaning WEAK). Problem (low digital-government ranking, low uptake of the federal portal among municipalities), solutions and expected results are listed and coherent in the final version, but there is no explicit causal chain or testable assumptions; the NT notes it is unclear how results follow from the financed items (e.g. whether portal adhesion is a loan condition).
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified 7 indicators, roughly 2 outcome-type (digital services used per year, share of services digitised) and 5 output/coverage-type (subnational entities financed, municipalities enabled, share of staff trained twice, share of public buildings connected).
- Baselines: all 7 set at zero; the CC explains this as counting only entities with no prior progress (counterfactual), so counted as defined (share 1.0). No reference year stated (year share 0.0).
- Verification: absent; no data sources or intermediate milestones, targets measured only at the end of the 3-year period. One target is 100% of connected buildings and two are 80% staff trained; NT questions the definition of an enabled public servant and the formula of the service-catalogue indicator. NT also flags a portal-visits-type metric in the earlier version as poorly linked to results.
- No benchmarks used; one indicator is gender-disaggregated (women staff trained).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. CC states no significant additional costs for the lender and relies on its existing structure; NT notes no treatment of portfolio management, loss provisioning, default or restructuring costs, and no post-project O&M estimate for financed digital assets. Sub-loans to municipalities are backed by Union guarantee or the municipal participation fund, which the CC presents as lowering default risk.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. Legal-act risk described only in general terms; procurement risk declared low/not applicable (no mitigation); land and environmental-social items left blank, which the NT penalises as unjustified; credit risk of small municipalities not analysed. No demand study of subnational borrowers before submission.
- Counterpart: none (100% external financing). Alignment with the national digital strategy and the investment plan stated; PPA link described via lender's own strategy.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Responsibility matrix present but generic (departments, no named roles); operational team and experience not described; a multidisciplinary project unit is only mentioned; no external-credit track record in the portfolio system.
