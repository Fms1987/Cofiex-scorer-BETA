## Resumo
Investment operation for a sizeable municipal government (direct, non-intermediated lending), financing a multi-sector digital-transformation / smart-city programme. Main lines: expansion of public-area monitoring and sensor networks; a city-intelligence data observatory for cross-sector decision support; a regulatory sandbox for experimental public-policy design; connectivity expansion (logical network and Wi-Fi) prioritising schools and other public buildings; school digitalisation (equipment plus teacher training); digitalisation of citizen-facing municipal services; an innovation centre built from the adaptive reuse of a protected heritage building; and strategic planning instruments (ICT master plan, climate-action plan, solid-waste management plan). Executed directly by the municipality through multiple secretariats under a dedicated programme unit (UGP).
