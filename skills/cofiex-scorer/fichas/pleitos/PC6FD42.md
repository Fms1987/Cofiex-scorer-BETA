## Resumo
- Modality: IBRD investment loan (8-year disbursement, 4-year grace, long amortisation, SOFR-based pricing) with a Union guarantee, to a state government; executor is the state's strategic-partnerships office with a project management unit (UGP) to be created under it.
- Finances performance-based road rehabilitation and maintenance contracts on the state paved network, in two delivery models: roughly 70% of the maintenance block through design-build-maintain contracts and roughly 30% through PPP-type contracts with private financing; the road block is roughly 90% of cost.
- Smaller components (each about 5% or less): institutional strengthening (training, equipment, road-safety countermeasures and enforcement equipment, logistics-plan update, PPP capacity, environmental-social-governance dashboard), safer school-access crossings, and project administration.
- State counterpart at the 20% minimum, concentrated in the PPP subcomponent and drawn from a dedicated state road fund.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear problem-solution-benefit chain (pavement deterioration and lack of efficient management, so performance-based contracts, so lower lifecycle cost and better condition, safer and climate-resilient roads); assumptions on private-sector uptake and risk transfer are asserted, not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Verified count: 4 indicators; approximately 3 outcome-type (fatal victims, share of pavement in poor/regular condition, share of schools with critical crossing points) and 1 output (length of road under private management; the NT criticises it as delivery-only, not impact).
- Coverage gap: the four indicators leave institutional strengthening, decarbonisation studies and the PPP capacity-building outputs without a measure. Wording "can be measured right after contract" for all four; no explicit verification mechanism or reporting source stated.
- Baselines: all 4 have a stated value (counted); two are normalised to 100% (fatalities, school crossings), one is a share under a continuous visual survey with reference year 2023 (the only one with a year), one a network length. Year share 0.25; NT notes no reference period for baselines or targets.
- Benchmarks: road-condition survey method only; no external safety benchmark (e.g. iRAP not used).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG-leaning. Annual maintenance need estimated per km per year on a federal-agency cost basis and compared with a dedicated state road fund whose yearly revenue is a multiple of the need; the road agency takes over maintenance in phases; a state PPP guarantee fund backs PPP payments. Legal backing cited (fund law, PPP law).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: narrative by topic (legal acts, contracting, expropriation, environment, social, resettlement), no scored matrix. Environmental licensing risk rated low: maintenance and restoration activities are exempt, upgrades need a simplified licence. Expropriation and resettlement called low without supporting evidence (NT flags this). Staff shortage identified as a contracting risk, mitigated by individual consultants.
- Counterpart: roughly 20% of cost from the state road fund; authorising state law still being drafted, so counterpart is to be secured, not yet in the budget laws.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP to be created by a normative act under the executor; responsibilities detailed (procurement, financial, safeguards, PPP guarantee liaison); inter-secretariat agreements drafted, not signed; prior experience with the same lender and a works-management information system; NT notes little detail on team technical capacity.
