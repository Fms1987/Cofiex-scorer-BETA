## Resumo
- Modality: federal-level external credit (IDB) used to capitalise regional development funds that lend to the private sector through operating banks (intermediated on-lending / fund capitalisation); no counterpart.
- Financed content is a demand-driven menu of private projects in transport, logistics, urban mobility and renewable energy; no specific subprojects fixed at CC stage (a pre-identified pipeline is cited in an annex).
- Three components, one per regional fund, split unevenly (roughly 60% / 20% / 20% of the loan). Borrower and executing body: a federal ministry through a funds/financial-instruments secretariat; regional development agencies and operating banks do approval and credit risk.
- Also mentions technical support for a project-preparation and guarantee facility feeding concessions/PPPs into the funds.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem, causes (public capital shortfall, private credit gaps), solution and results chain are coherent, with a vertical-logic annex tied to PPA objectives; assumptions are not explicit or testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 11 indicators verified (approximately 8 outcome/impact, 3 output-type, e.g. count of structured concessions/PPPs, road resilience coverage, jobs).
- Baselines: only 3 of 11 stated as values (private-investment share of GDP with year; structured-PPP count at zero; jobs at zero, both zeros explained by "no projects financed yet"); 2 carry a reference year. Remaining indicators say baseline cannot be set a priori because no project is identified, to be set per financed project.
- Several targets are reference rates for the future subprojects (e.g. a generic emissions-cut rate, a travel-time cut), not portfolio targets; the CC itself admits many indicators "cannot be SMART".
- Verification: ex-post evaluation by the UGP with IDB support and an end-of-programme evaluation; results annex referenced; no data source per indicator.
- Inconsistencies: objective text refers to two regions while the operation covers three; one emissions indicator is fluvial-only though the same objective covers land modes.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: O&M of financed assets sits with private borrowers; fund administration costs are covered by fixed remuneration under existing rules; operating banks bear 100% of credit risk. No OPEX table (not needed for the ministry); no new public cost claimed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: 8 risks listed (procurement, expropriation, social impact, resettlement, legal acts, environmental, macroeconomic, execution) with impact/probability; mitigation relies largely on existing fund rules, environmental and social management plans per project and IDB no-objection for high-category projects. Risk of coordination among several lenders financing the same funds is flagged. Licensing is a prerequisite per project, handled by the agencies/banks.
- Counterpart: none (waiver); legal basis for external resources entering the funds is stated as already provided by the funds' legislation.
- A.4 Implementation: ministry secretariat as executing body, plus operational regulation to be drafted; RACI annex; UGP described only loosely, experience claimed from similar lender operations.
