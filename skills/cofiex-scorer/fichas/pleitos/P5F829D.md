## Resumo
- Investment loan (CAF) with federal guarantee for a federal-level programme: about 90% goes to raw-water conveyance infrastructure in a semi-arid region (an irrigation-perimeter stretch plus the first reach of a larger conveyance system, with pumping stations and reservoirs); no counterpart (financed 100% by the lender, commission and appraisal fees included).
- Smaller components: watershed recovery (erosion control, recharge, spring and riparian protection) and vocational training for small farmers and labour, with a youth quota; programme management (UGP, supervision, studies, audit) at roughly 10%.
- Delivery model direct: a federal line ministry plus a federal development company as executor, with a UGP to be created by ministerial decree; a state water utility is to operate the new reach afterwards under a cooperation agreement still to be signed.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-result chain is coherent (water scarcity -> conveyance capacity -> supply for human, animal and irrigation use; watershed recovery -> recharge), but assumptions are not stated and the training-to-income link is only asserted.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Six indicators (verified), approximately 4 output-type (flow capacity, area protected or recovered, people trained) and 2 outcome-type (water-security index of municipalities; raw-water flow for human use); no verification mechanism beyond "measurement"/"survey", no deadlines, no benchmark beyond a national water-security index.
- Baselines: 3 of 6 defined (non-zero values) -> share 0.5; three zero baselines (area protected, people trained, area with erosion) were counted as UNDEFINED because the CC does not explain them; the erosion indicator is a "reduction" yet starts at zero (internal inconsistency). No reference years stated, so year share is 0.
- Indicators mostly restate physical deliveries (capacity of works), the main weakness the later review also highlights.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. O&M is assigned (state utility for the new reach, executor for the irrigation perimeter and watershed work) and the CC says no incremental costs arise, but no O&M figure, tariff/funding source or legal instrument for the future cost is given; cooperation agreement still to be signed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative risks and mitigations by area (legal acts, procurement, expropriation, environmental, social); no matrix. Licensing is early: water-use grant requested, preliminary-licence process opened, many steps still pending; basic and executive designs still being completed in parallel with bidding (semi-integrated contracting). Expropriation needed but no resettlement.
- Counterpart: none required (100% lender-financed). Budget: PPA action exists and the CC cites annual capital-expenditure allocations, but the programme was withdrawn for budget inadequacy, so the fiscal-space question was unresolved.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Experienced federal executor and permanent bidding commission, but the UGP is only to be created and the borrower has no prior external-credit track record in the system.
