## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / financial intermediation. A federal development bank (state-owned) borrows from IDB with sovereign guarantee and extends indirect credit to micro, small and medium enterprises and small individual producers.
- Purpose: productive investment credit (fixed assets, energy efficiency, digitalisation, bioeconomy) in territories of several strategic biomes; follow-on to an earlier COFIEX-approved programme of the same type for one macro-territory.
- Delivery: indirect automatic operations through accredited financial institutions that carry the credit risk; the bank is borrower and sole executor.
- Two components split by territory (roughly three quarters and one quarter of the volume), plus counterpart waiver under Art. 34 of the Normative Resolution.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear problem-solution chain (credit rationing, collateral gaps, high rates, regional inequality leads to credit access leads to productivity, jobs, sustainable chains), supported by survey data, but assumptions are not stated as testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Four indicators (verified), all outcome-type or portfolio-share: two quasi-experimental impact measures (revenue and employment versus a control group, via an automated impact-evaluation model), one share of credit in lower-HDI municipalities, one share of credit to taxonomy-sustainable projects.
- Baselines: all four stated as zero and each zero is explained (matched control group, or no prior reference for the programme), so counted as defined (share 1.0); no reference year given, so year share 0.
- Verification: named sources (formal-employment registry, municipal HDI, internal portfolio reports with the bank's sustainability taxonomy). Benchmarks: prior programmes cited for the modest impact targets.
- Weak points: impact targets are small increments over control; the bank's own taxonomy is the sole classifier for the environmental share; distribution indicator uses a low-frequency HDI source.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no OPEX issue (no works); the bank uses its own staff and systems without extra funding, accredited institutions bear credit risk, and sub-projects must present an operation/maintenance plan at credit analysis. Not quantified.
- A.3 Risks: six risks with impact/probability and mitigation (legal-institutional, portfolio/credit with a stated pipeline, environmental, social, procurement by sub-borrowers, expropriation/resettlement). Environmental screening excludes high-risk sub-projects and follows lender safeguards; licensing handled at sub-project level.
- Counterpart: none; waiver justified under Art. 34 of the Normative Resolution, financing spread evenly over four years.
- A.4 Implementation: experienced bank teams and a track record of prior lender operations; no separate UGP. Detailed eligibility criteria are described as jointly agreed with the lender but not detailed in the CC.
