## Resumo
- Modality: guarantee-backed external credit (EUR, European investment bank) to a state government to fund its public contributions (aportes) under an already-contracted sponsored-concession PPP for urban rail mass transit in a capital city; funds go only to the implantation phase, not to operation.
- Second request for the same project: complements an earlier, partially approved request whose amount was cut to fit the PPP limit; the combined lender support letter covers both requests (multi-tranche logic).
- Components are three public-contribution lines paid on milestones validated by an independent certifier: civil works (about three quarters of cost), permanent way (small share) and additional works enabling a later expansion phase (about a fifth).
- Delivery by the concessionaire; the state acts as grantor and its finance secretariat as borrower representative. Counterpart at the 20% minimum.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear chain from lack of high-capacity transit in vulnerable peripheral areas to a new line, with four specific objectives (emissions, travel time, network coverage/ridership, enabling a later expansion phase); assumptions on modal shift and demand rest on functional-study modelling, not stated as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 indicators verified, all outcome-type (approximate: emissions avoided, door-to-door travel time, aggregate travel-cost savings, population coverage, ridership); no output indicator for the enabling-works objective. Data sources named (bus-operator and census data, ticketing records, concessionaire reports, origin-destination studies).
- Baselines: all 5 defined (1.0) with a reference year (1.0); no zero baselines counted. Emissions baseline is a city-wide sector total, not project-specific; travel-time baseline is internally inconsistent (stated 30 minutes with year 2028, while text gives about 90 minutes by bus today); travel-cost baseline is a multi-part set of estimates.
- Verification: partly ex-ante modelling; ex-post measurement depends on concessionaire and operator reporting. Targets are all set for the first year of full operation, after the loan period.
- Benchmarks: PPA strategic-objective linkage and SDGs cited; no external standard benchmark.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Operation and maintenance are the concessionaire's responsibility, funded by fare revenue and public counter-payments under the contract payment matrix; assets revert to the grantor at term. No quantified OPEX or subsidy trajectory in the CC; external funds explicitly exclude operation.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE/STRONG. Six risks with impact/probability and concrete mitigations: geotechnical, utility interferences, archaeological, expropriation and resettlement, social conflict, public-funding delays. Contractual rebalancing clauses and an independent certifier; licensing handled mainly through the concessionaire's programmes.
- Counterpart: described as backed by multi-year plan budget allocation and a state authorising law covering the combined amounts of both requests; at the 20% minimum.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Defined roles for grantor, regulator, finance secretariat and concessionaire, with an accredited independent certifier validating physical milestones before payments, and a responsibility matrix in an annex.
