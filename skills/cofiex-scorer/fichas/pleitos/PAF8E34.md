## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan with intermediated delivery (second-tier credit line / on-lending) to a sub-national development bank, with sovereign guarantee; the bank is the sole proponent and an indirect executor, final borrowers being municipalities and private firms of the state.
- Finances credit lines (existing and new) for two themes: energy transition (renewables value chain, public distributed generation, energy efficiency, low-carbon fuels, industrial decarbonisation, e-mobility) and digital transition (connectivity, public-service digitalisation and interoperability, digital education/health/security, supply-chain digitalisation); plus a small technical-assistance component for product design and borrower project preparation.
- Three components: energy (roughly 40% of cost), digital (roughly 60%), technical assistance (a few percent). Counterpart at program level is zero; private sub-borrowers must contribute at least 20% of their own sub-project.
- Rates and terms: variable base rate plus spread, 20-year term with 5 years grace; ticket band 100-200M USD; execution horizon of 5 years.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree per theme (market barriers, SME exclusion, uneven municipal digital maturity) maps to credit-line solutions, but the chain from credit availability to sub-project quality rests on untested assumptions about demand and pipeline; the objectives list is long and partly repeated across components.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 18 indicators verified (version 4 of the CC): approximately 5 outcome-type (emissions avoided, jobs supported, SME productivity change x2, share of projects linked to SDGs) and about 13 output/count-type (efficiency projects, connected public units, municipalities digitalised or joining platforms, rural schools, education institutions, TA contracts, TA-supported borrowers, two low-IDH allocation shares).
- Baselines: all 18 state a baseline value with a reference year (share 1.0 and 1.0). Three non-zero baselines (SDG-linked share, emissions avoided, jobs) from bank records; 15 zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explicitly justifies each as a new indicator for the program.
- Verification: data sources listed generically (IDH from official statistics, an in-house CO2 calculator from a past technical cooperation, annual sustainability report); no independent verification agent and no per-indicator frequency; no external benchmarks cited.
- Weak points: several outputs are very small relative to the programme scale and do not capture sub-project quality or leverage; one leverage indicator is expressed in local currency; indicator 8 unit is described as a percentage while the target is a count (internal inconsistency).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for the lender-side (bank strategic plan covers the program; funding diversification; FX swap hedge priced indicatively; renewal of external lines assumed afterwards); no quantified O&M table for financed sub-projects, which is borne by final borrowers.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: seven risks (contractual, environmental, FX, expropriation/resettlement, social, climate, legal/institutional), all rated low impact and low probability; demand risk argued via a much larger indicative pipeline than the loan; safeguards via exclusion list and social/environmental policy, with environmental licensing checked at sub-project credit analysis. Self-rating looks uniformly optimistic.
- Counterpart: none at program level (guarantee counter-collateral from constitutional revenue shares and own tax revenue, with an authorising law); private sub-borrowers contribute at least 20%.
- A.4 Implementation: no UGP; the bank itself coordinates through its international funding, planning, operations, credit-analysis, risk and legal units under a detailed responsibility matrix; long track record with other multilateral lenders (without sovereign guarantee).
- CC states that GTEC revisions requested in an earlier round were incorporated (indicators, strategic areas).
