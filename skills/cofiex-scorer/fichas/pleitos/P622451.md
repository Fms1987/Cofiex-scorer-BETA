## Resumo
- Investment operation, direct execution by a capital-district government (subnational-general subtype), financed by a single multilateral lender with a legally earmarked counterpart at the regulatory floor (20%).
- Finances urban drainage and paving works in two vulnerable, flood-prone urban areas, a dedicated social-accompaniment program for an informal-settlement population, institutional strengthening of the works-executing agency's digital-engineering capacity, and program management/supervision.
- Delivery model: direct, through a project-management unit under the executing works secretariat; civil works dominate the budget (roughly 90%), with smaller shares for consultancy/institutional strengthening and social-program activities.
- Entity type: capital-district (Federal District-level) government, direct administration.
