## Resumo
- Investment programme by a state government, financed by IDB, counterpart at about the 20% minimum (part of it in the form of inmate remuneration already budgeted).
- Finances prison-system modernisation and resocialisation: replacement and upgrading of facilities, work, education, health and cognitive-behavioural programmes for people deprived of liberty, security systems and fleet, a risk-based management model, wastewater treatment, human-rights training for staff.
- Four components (infrastructure; assistance and treatment; security and management; programme management/audit); direct execution by the state prison administration secretariat through a dedicated UGP to be created by decree.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Three problems (inadequate facilities, fragmented assistance services, weak management/security) map to three solution blocks and expected results, built around a risk-need-responsivity management model and recommendations of the supreme court on the prison system; assumptions are implicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Seventeen indicators verified (approximate split: nine outcome-type such as reoffending share, work and education participation, sentence-remission days, incident and escape rates; eight output/process-type such as security-equipment score, new-capacity increment, staff training, wastewater treatment, model adoption). Each has formula, unit, baseline note and annual periodicity; sources are administrative systems and a national prison-management benchmark seal.
- Baselines: 14 of 17 have a stated value (0.82); only about 4 also state a reference year (about 0.24). Zero baselines counted as defined: wastewater treatment (new asset) and risk-model adoption (explained as not yet institutionalised). Zero baselines counted as undefined: cognitive-behavioural programme share, new-capacity increment, human-rights training share (no explanation given).
- Issues: indicator definitions are heavy on administrative proxies; the capacity indicator is defined as an annual increment, so a zero base is conceptually odd; the remission indicator mixes a monthly and an annual figure in its note; targets are mostly end of year five.
- Benchmarks: national prison-service quality seal and national prison statistics.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual running costs of the investments were projected and shown to be a small single-digit share of the secretariat's yearly budget, with in-house maintenance teams, planned contracted maintenance for complex equipment, and a self-financing logic for prison work programmes; no multi-year table and no legal instrument cited.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: five risks with impact/probability and mitigation: need for a state authorising law and fiscal compliance, delays in procurement specifications, expropriation and resettlement around new facilities, environmental impacts of construction (impact studies planned), prison riots by organised crime. Licensing and resettlement are real issues for new facilities.
- Counterpart: about 20%; budget inclusion not demonstrated, and the authorising law is still pending (flagged as a risk).
- A.4 Implementation: dedicated UGP with specialist coordinators (procurement, M&E, works, safeguards) to be created by decree, reusing the state's IDB-compliant programme management system and an existing prison performance-monitoring programme; RACI matrix annexed.
