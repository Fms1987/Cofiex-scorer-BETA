## Resumo
- Intermediated investment loan (IBRD) to a state development agency, with a state counter-guarantee to the Union, financing digital transformation at municipal level through credit and technical assistance.
- Four components: municipal digital foundations (connectivity, service digitalisation, civil-servant training), sector digital applications (health, education, transport, energy, smart city), credit lines for micro and small enterprises (with an inclusion window for women- or minority-led firms), and technical assistance.
- Delivery via the agency's credit operations to municipalities, public companies, concessionaires and MPEs; executor is the agency plus final borrowers; no counterpart. Five-year execution.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Six specific objectives each tied to a diagnosed problem (territorial asymmetry, low municipal IT capacity, weak infrastructure, low MPE maturity); causal chain from credit and TA to digital outcomes is stated, assumptions about borrower demand and absorption are not explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 10 indicators verified; approximately 6 outcome-type and 4 output/volume-type (municipalities reached, servants trained, applications count, TA operations, credit volume). Each has formula, annual targets, data source and link to objectives; verification mechanism present (agency records, audits, diagnostics, operator reports).
- Baselines: all 10 have a stated value. Zero baselines (new programme or new credit line) are explained in the CC and counted as defined; one baseline is an estimate. No reference year is given for any baseline, so year share is 0.
- Weaknesses: SMART self-assessments are thin for several indicators; one indicator is a satisfaction-type scale used for digital maturity; telemedicine waiting time is a single-sector outcome in a multi-sector loan; the cyber target reaches 100%.
- Benchmarks: federal digital-government strategy and a national digital strategy cited; no international benchmark.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL/ADEQUATE. O&M (connectivity, licences, cloud, support, cybersecurity) listed only indicatively and left to final borrowers, who must show budget capacity at credit contracting; no quantified OPEX table. Efficiency gains and TA are the stated offsets. Agency keeps the credit lines after loan closing; a digital-transformation committee is planned for post-implementation governance.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: 9 risks with impact, probability and mitigation (political change, technology readiness for AI, agency allocation capacity, service portfolio maturity, environmental and resettlement effects of digital infrastructure, borrower project-preparation capacity, monitoring of a wide portfolio, need for a state authorising law for the counter-guarantee). Licensing/resettlement handled through credit-eligibility studies.
- Counterpart: none required; the state counter-guarantee law may still be needed ("to be secured").
- A.4 Implementation: UGP under the agency's business directorate with a minimal specialist team (finance, procurement, environment, legal, TA); agency has an established credit operation and systems, but a consolidated digital-services portfolio is still maturing.
