## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan with Union guarantee, small ticket (<50M USD band), counterpart at the 20% minimum, subnational borrower that is a mid-sized municipality; direct execution by the municipal public works secretariat through a UGP.
- Finances urban road-mobility works: grade-separated interchanges and an access road on a federal highway crossing the urban area, recovery of a long network of local streets with drainage and accessible sidewalks, cycle paths, plus tree planting.
- Three components by structure: studies and works (about 90% of cost, mostly works), expropriation (a private green-belt strip), and programme management (supervision, UGP, external audit, lender fee).
- Basic designs are ready for tender for most works; cycle paths only at concept stage; environmental licensing starts after executive designs.
