## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a mid-sized municipal government, executed directly by the municipality (no intermediation); 5-year execution, 5-year grace and 210-month total term, SOFR-based pricing, counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances integrated urban stormwater/macrodrainage, creek-basin rehabilitation with nature-based solutions and green/protected areas, new urban roads and bridges plus rural crossing upgrades (component 1, roughly 80% of cost).
- Remaining components: a smart-city/digital-governance package (digital services portal, geoinformation, integrated operations centre, LED street lighting and solar generation; about 10% of cost), a small housing/resettlement component for families in risk and protected areas, and project management/studies/licensing support (under 10%).
- Four specific objectives (flood exposure, urban mobility safety, rural-urban access reliability, municipal management capacity) mapped to a dozen PPA strategic objectives.

- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators (verified), mostly outcome-type (approximate judgment: about 5 outcome, 2 output-like, e.g. population served by drainage works, green area protected); each has a formula, data source and baseline/target with years.

- Benchmarks: only national statistical sources; no creditor/LAC comparators.
