## Resumo
- Modality: standard investment loan in local currency (BRL-denominated IDB operation) to a state government, direct execution by the state education/science-and-technology secretariat via a PMU to be set up after approval; counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances vocational and technical education (EPT) expansion: new and refurbished climate-resilient technical schools, laboratories, mobile training units, a distance-learning platform, and structuring of a PPP for further school construction.
- Five components: school-to-work transition for green markets; infrastructure coverage and quality (by far the largest share of cost, roughly two thirds or more); course offer, curricula and teacher training; management practices (climate adaptation, data and management systems); programme administration (PMU, audit, impact evaluation).
- Targets youth in vulnerable territories, with an inclusion agenda for indigenous, quilombola and riverside students; strong bioeconomy / green-jobs framing in a low-IDH state.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-results narrative is coherent (skills mismatch, low coverage outside the capital region, dropout, youth informality -> infrastructure + curricula + teacher training + school-to-work transition), but there is no explicit causal chain with testable assumptions; results are listed as expected outcomes.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 18 indicators verified, each tagged to a result and specific objective with a 2032 horizon; approximate split about 10 outcome-type (enrolment, completion, dropout, repetition, NEET rate, teacher perception) vs about 8 output-type (teachers trained, share of curricula adapted, schools meeting sustainability criteria, schools under PPP, entrepreneurs formalised, regions covered).
- Baselines: none stated in the indicator blocks (zero of 18 with a baseline value), hence baseline_defined_share 0.0 and no reference years; only targets are given. No verification mechanism or data source per indicator; monitoring is delegated to a quality-monitoring system and impact evaluation planned in the components.
- Internal inconsistencies: the target for the NEET indicator differs between the results list and the indicator (different age bands and values); dropout target also differs between sections. No benchmarks cited for targets. Several indicators are 100% compliance targets that are near-tautological.
- Inclusion indicators (vulnerable communities completing EPT, beneficiaries of student assistance) are present but not disaggregated by gender.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE/PARTIAL. Recurrent costs are argued qualitatively: per-student transfers from the national basic-education fund (higher weights for vocational/integral courses), a federal vocational-training programme for modalities the fund does not cover, and state natural-resource royalties; efficiency and resilient design to lower maintenance. No quantified O&M/OPEX table or multi-year projection.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Six risks with impact/probability and mitigation (legal acts, procurement, land acquisition, environmental, social, resettlement); licensing under the state environmental agency, safeguards support from the lender; no resettlement planned (municipal land donation preferred). No sector-specific delivery risks (PPP structuring delay, teacher retention, demand for courses) in the matrix.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum; disbursement schedule shows counterpart in every year; an authorising state law and regulating decree are still to be obtained (rated high impact, medium probability); alignment with the state PPA is documented with programme references.
- A.4 Implementation: PMU linked to the secretary's office, five functional areas, planned to be structured only after approval; supplemented by consultants under lender rules; legal, finance, planning and audit bodies as partners; separation of design, execution and supervision for works. Secretariat itself is undergoing restructuring.
