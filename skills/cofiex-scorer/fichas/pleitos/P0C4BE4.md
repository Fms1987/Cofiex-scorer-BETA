## Resumo
Investment operation for a large municipality, executed directly by the municipal public-works/maintenance secretariat (direct delivery model). Finances urban drainage and flood-control works (macro- and micro-drainage, a flood-monitoring/early-warning system), ecological recovery and urbanization of degraded urban water bodies and their protected riparian areas, urban-mobility works (new/upgraded roads and grade-separated crossings to improve connectivity and encourage non-motorized transport), and a resettlement/compensation plan for households in intervention or risk areas, alongside a program-management component (supervision, environmental/social management plan, evaluation, audit). The large majority of funds go to civil works, with a smaller share for technical studies/consultancy and for the monitoring system and program administration.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE — the CC sets out a clear problem diagnosis (water-resource degradation, insufficient drainage, compromised urban mobility, risk-area occupation) with a direct, explicit nexus between objectives, components/products and expected results; the NT's technical evaluation score explicitly credits this alignment. No formal testable-assumption chain is diagrammed, so it falls short of STRONG. A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. VERIFIED 7 indicators, directly tied to the specific objectives and judged by the NT as simple to measure and well correlated with expected results. baseline_defined_share 0.86 (6 of 7 indicators state a numeric baseline); one indicator (ecological recovery of a protected riparian area) has an unexplained zero baseline, counted as undefined under the v3 rule. baseline_with_year_share is lower, about 0.57: a reference year is stated for the baselines of the flood-points, civil-defense-complaints, macro-drainage and waterborne-disease indicators, but not for the two urban-mobility travel-time indicators. The NT flags, as its one specific criticism of the indicators, the absence of intermediate milestones/targets showing expected evolution over the implementation period — i.e., only final targets are given, not a trajectory. No sector benchmark is cited and no wide-range targets were found.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK — the CC states only generically that post-project operation and maintenance of the drainage and road assets will rely on budget appropriations and the municipality's own non-financial resources (staff, machinery), with no future cost detailing and no named funding source for maintenance; the NT explicitly scores this item unsatisfactory for lacking a cost breakdown and a funding source for upkeep of the resulting works. A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE — legal-act, procurement, expropriation, environmental and social risks are each discussed with proposed mitigations (an environmental and social management plan to be prepared; a resettlement plan with a registry/consultation process prioritizing women-headed households for compensation, and reported prior success with similar resettlement cases); the NT nonetheless flags that the authorizing legal instrument was still only in draft form and that risk detailing was uneven across topics, and separately notes schedule risk tied to a third-party road-concession operator for works near shared infrastructure. Environmental licensing is deemed low-risk by the proponent because intervention areas are municipally owned, though a formal social/environmental management plan is still pending. Counterpart: at the 20% minimum band, to be met from municipal budget resources, with the standard federal guarantee mechanism and a sub-national counter-guarantee instrument tied to municipal revenue-sharing transfers. A.4 Implementation (UGP): no dedicated new program-management unit is proposed; execution responsibility sits with the existing municipal public-works/maintenance secretariat coordinating other municipal bodies, without a structured institutional-responsibility matrix detailing individual roles — a gap the NT also flags — and the proponent reports no external-financier-executed project in roughly the preceding decade.
