## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (IDB), a fiscal-modernisation programme of a state government, executed directly by the state finance secretariat through a UGP; no intermediation.
- Three components: (1) fiscal management and transparency (governance, people, IT, materials, transparency), roughly 40% of cost with IT dominant; (2) tax administration and tax litigation (taxpayer registry, enforcement intelligence, collection, tax-reform convergence), roughly 40%; (3) financial administration and public spending (budget, accounting, debt, assets/liabilities, costing), roughly 20%; plus a small project-management block.
- Cross-cutting themes: data-driven fiscal management, gender-equity leadership training, small on-site green items (renewable energy, wastewater), PPP/investment management model.
- Counterpart at roughly 9% of cost; the CC invokes the Res. COFIEX 03/2019 exemption for fiscal-management projects.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Seven diagnosed fiscal problems (from an IDB fiscal-maturity diagnostic) map onto three axes, three specific objectives and three expected results; no explicit assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Three indicators verified (all outcome-type, none output): cost to collect, real growth of state VAT-type revenue, and budget execution discrepancy. All three have formulas and baseline values (baseline_defined_share 1.0), but no reference year is stated (baseline_with_year_share 0.0). No indicator covers the digital/data-governance result; verification timing and monitoring are given only for one indicator (NT 1.3: 0.204/0.300).
- Indicators are described by the NT as simplistic; no external benchmarks used; no wide-range or deferred indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurring costs (hardware, licences, system support) estimated at about 3% of programme cost per year; funded during execution from loan proceeds and afterwards from the state budget custeio line, with a tax-administration modernisation fund as a complement. A sustainability plan is only promised for the preparation phase (NT 2.2: 0.282/0.300).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: PARTIAL. Legal-act and procurement risks (delayed terms of reference, slow bidding) have mitigation measures drawn from earlier PROFISCO-type operations; environmental, social, expropriation and resettlement sections are left blank without justification (NT 3.2: 0.282/0.400).
- Counterpart: small and legally exempt under the fiscal-management carve-out; state authorising law already exists; counter-guarantee conditions to be presented later.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE to STRONG. Finance secretariat executor with a UGP (coordinator, administrative-financial and technical sub-coordinators, procurement and M&E advisers, product leaders) and prior IDB experience. NT portfolio-performance score was unsatisfactory (30-50 band) (NT 3.3: 0.280/0.400).
