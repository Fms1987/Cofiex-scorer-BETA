## Resumo
- PPP-linked investment loan (EIB, EUR) to a state government to finance public-partner contributions (aportes) paid to a private concessionaire building a high-capacity urban rail line under a sponsored concession.
- Financed items are civil-works milestones (yard buildings, rough structure of stations, station-body tunnels); concessionaire executes and is paid on pre-defined events; the concession is well advanced physically. The last version carries a counterpart at the 20% minimum (earlier version had none).
- One component, delivered through the state PPP secretariat as grantor, a sector regulator for supervision and an independent works certifier; loan managed by the state finance secretariat.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear chain from missing high-capacity transit in a peripheral area to shorter trips, modal shift, lower emissions and wider coverage; assumptions (demand ramp-up, integration with the existing network, timely completion) are implicit rather than testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 indicators verified, all outcome-type (approximate): emissions avoided, average trip time on the corridor, avoided travel and operating costs, population coverage near stations, system passenger entries. Each has formula, target year, data sources and objective link; data sources named (operator, transit agency, census, ticketing).
- Baselines: all 5 have a stated value and a reference year; counted as defined. Zero baselines: none. One baseline is internally inconsistent (trip-time baseline year equals the target year while the text gives a different current value); emissions baseline is a city-wide total, not corridor-level.
- Targets are demand-model estimates (modeled, not measured); one objective (future extension readiness) has no indicator by design.
- No benchmark framework beyond national plan areas and SDGs; the cost indicator mixes three monetised components.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG/ADEQUATE. O&M is the concessionaire's duty under a long-term sponsored concession; operating costs are quantified from the concession business plan and shown as fully covered by fares, public counter-payments and ancillary revenue; assets revert to the grantor at term; economic-financial rebalancing clause exists.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: 7 risks with impact/probability and mitigation (geotechnical, utility interferences, archaeological, expropriation/resettlement, social conflict, public funding of contributions, state authorising law). Matrix exists; licensing/archaeology and resettlement handled via approved social-management plan.
- Counterpart: budget line under the state multi-year plan cited; state authorising law (currency conversion) still "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: strong institutional chain (grantor secretariat, regulator, independent certifier, finance secretariat) and prior credit from other lenders fully disbursed; no UGP in the classic sense, with the concessionaire as executor.
