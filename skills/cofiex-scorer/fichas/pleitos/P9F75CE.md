## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (IBRD) for a state government, multi-component, executed directly by the state planning secretariat with a project management unit (UGP) and several partner agencies.
- Finances water-security foundations: water-resources governance and regulation, hydrometeorological monitoring, dam safety and rehabilitation, watershed revitalisation with nature-based practices, bulk-water supply infrastructure for semi-urban centres, rural dispersed water/sanitation solutions, climate-risk agricultural zoning and irrigation diagnostics, plus inter-state knowledge exchange.
- Three components (strengthening bases for water security; resilient water services; partnerships and project management); counterpart is concentrated in the works-heavy services component, at the 20% minimum overall.
