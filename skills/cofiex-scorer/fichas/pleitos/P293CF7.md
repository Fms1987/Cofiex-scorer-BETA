## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / credit-line funding. A federal development bank borrows from a multilateral development bank (the CC text names the NDB as lender, while the ficha header says IDB) with Union guarantee, floating-rate funding, term up to about 20 years and a disbursement/grace window of up to 4 years, to fund its own lending for climate-resilience and productive recovery after a major flood disaster.
- Financed: single component and single product (investment in facilities, machinery, equipment and associated working capital) across many eligible sectors (transport and mobility, water and sanitation, logistics, telecom and energy, agriculture and agro-industry, social infrastructure, SMEs, subnational governments and state enterprises).
- Delivery: intermediated; final beneficiaries are firms of all sizes and subnational governments, financed directly or via accredited financial agents under the bank's ordinary credit process. Portfolio to be defined jointly with the lender; sub-borrowers not yet identified.
- Entity type: federal state-owned development bank; disbursement spread evenly over four years.
