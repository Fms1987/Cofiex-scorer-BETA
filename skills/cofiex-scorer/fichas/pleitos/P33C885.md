## Resumo
- Results-linked investment loan from IBRD ("disbursements linked to indicators") to a capital municipality, continuing two earlier operations with the same lender; ticket band 100-200M USD with counterpart at 50% of total cost (far above the 20% minimum), Union guarantee, 5-year disbursement, evenly spread over years 2-5.
- A single component split almost equally across three sector sub-components: health (facility and technology upgrades, maternal-child care management, digital health and tele-services), education (learning recovery, early-childhood quality, full-time schooling) and social assistance (SUAS strengthening, new standardised social-assistance facilities, benefits process redesign, support to homeless and older people, a family-integration programme).
- Direct execution by the mayor's office through an existing UGP with municipal finance, legal and the three sector secretariats as technical support; external audit by the municipal audit court under a cooperation agreement.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis in three sectors with benchmarks (national education plan targets, WHO maternal-mortality parameter, national infant-mortality average) and a link between sector problems and products; no explicit causal chain or assumptions, and product-to-indicator mapping is loose.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Only 6 indicators for a three-sector programme with a dozen products: a composite social-programme management index, 5th-grade reading fluency, prenatal-care coverage, syphilis/HIV testing coverage in pregnancy, individuals served by a new family-integration programme, and support professionals contracted. Roughly 4 outcome or coverage measures and 2 input/output measures (approximate judgement). Most sub-components (works, digital health, full-time schooling, early-childhood quality) have no indicator.
- Baselines: all 6 show a value. One is zero (individuals served by the new family-integration programme, still being structured, so counted as explained). baseline_defined_share 1.0; the earlier heuristic value was 0.83. No baseline reference year or period (0.0).
- Verification: NT stresses that data sources for the indicators are not given and that targets are tied to end of project without periodic data-collection dates. Disbursement-linked design is declared but no table of linked amounts or protocol appears in the CC.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Argument rests on fiscal-responsibility compliance, the municipality's fiscal capacity (CAPAG) and recurring costs being absorbed in the municipal budget and strategic plan; the CC states the project adds no unplanned expenditure. No forecast of maintenance or recurring costs (new facilities, staffing, tele-health, full-time schooling), which the NT flagged.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal act risks stated optimistically (good relations with the legislature), procurement and staffing risks generic, expropriation not foreseen with reference to the lender's environmental and social framework, and environmental and social risks listed in an annex with that framework as mitigation. Resettlement not addressed in the main text.
- Counterpart: 50% of cost, financial; authorising municipal law cited, UGP created by prior decree and law, activities included in the current PPA with the next PPA in preparation; recurring-cost increases said to be in the budget plan.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG/ADEQUATE. Existing UGP with institutional continuity from earlier phases, detailed responsibility matrix in an annex, external audit arrangement, extensive lender experience; weaker point is the track-record rating from the SIGS portfolio.
