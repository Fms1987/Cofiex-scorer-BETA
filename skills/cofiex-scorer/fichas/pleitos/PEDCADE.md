## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (IDB flexible-facility product) to a state-owned water and sanitation utility, with a Union guarantee and a state counter-guarantee law still to be enacted.
- Finances a multi-municipality package of water and sewer works plus state-wide bulk-meter and customer-meter modernisation; loss reduction and intermittency reduction are the central goals.
- Main components (five): metering expansion and renewal; water-system expansion and efficiency works (some under performance-based remuneration); retrofit of two treatment plants; sewer-system expansion in a handful of interior municipalities; administration, supervision, M&E and audit.
- Delivery: direct execution by the utility through a programme management unit inside its special-programmes management, supported by a hired management/supervision firm; integrated design-build style contracts.
- Counterpart is below 5% of cost and is supplied in kind through one treatment-plant expansion that is also a listed product; the utility is a state company so the 20% default does not bind.
- Programme is one of three parallel external-credit requests from the same sector family (other lender, same plan), presented as complementary.
