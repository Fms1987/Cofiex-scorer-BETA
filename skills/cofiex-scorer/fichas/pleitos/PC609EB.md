## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type external credit from IDB, no counterpart (0% in header), proponent is a federal line ministry.
- Finances multi-year public contributions (aportes) to federal road PPPs/concessions; loan funds are released to the private partner against contractual milestones, not spent on direct works.
- Three components: (C1) public contribution to concession/PPP projects, about 99% of the cost, with sub-components tied to specific road corridors in different stages of structuring; (C2) institutional strengthening studies on PPP contract models for lower-traffic roads, technology and handback; (C3) programme management (external audit, UGP support), each of the last two well under 1%.
- Delivery model: intermediated through concession contracts supervised by the sector regulator; the executing secretariat has no recent experience with multilateral loans.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Causal logic (public contribution de-risks private bidders, mobilises private CAPEX/OPEX, improves service level, safety and climate resilience) is stated in the problem/solution narrative but assumptions are not made explicit as testable hypotheses.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK to ADEQUATE. 10 indicators, approximately 7 outcome-type and 3 output-type (capital mobilised, works delivered, environmental performance score); a macro-level indicator (share of logistics investment in GDP) is weakly attributable to the programme.
- Verification mechanism present: sources named per indicator (sector regulator, road agency, federal police accident data, national statistics office); benchmark used is the regulator's environmental performance index and road level-of-service classes.
- Baselines: 7 of 10 counted as defined (one real 2024 value; six zero baselines counted because they refer to assets/contracts not yet in existence at "year 0"). Two relative indicators (travel time, climate repair cost) define the baseline only as a future first-year measurement, and the service-level indicator restates its target as baseline: all three undefined. Reference year counted for the same 7 (year 0 = contract signature).
- Inconsistencies: several targets are expressed over 10-30 year horizons tied to contract signature, not to the loan timeline; the works-completion formula is inverted (planned/realised); the capital-mobilised formula mixes ratio and level.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Operation and maintenance sit with the private partner for the full concession term, funded by user tolls; reversion to the federal road agency at term end, with the CC giving an order-of-magnitude per-km federal maintenance cost as the fallback. Free-flow tolling default risk acknowledged.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Six risks with impact/probability and mitigation (empty auctions, expropriation/resettlement, socio-environmental damage, toll evasion under free flow, licensing delay, social conflict); licensing and resettlement shifted contractually to the concessionaire.
- Counterpart: none required (0%); no LOA/PPA counterpart issue, but the programme's own budget path for later public contributions is not detailed.
- A.4 Implementation: WEAK to ADEQUATE. New UGP inside the executing secretariat plus a steering council and support from the regulator, the planning/structuring company and the road agency; candidly states no recent multilateral-loan experience, with IDB training planned.
