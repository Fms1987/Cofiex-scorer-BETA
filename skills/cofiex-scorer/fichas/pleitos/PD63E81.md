## Resumo
- Investment loan (IDB, BRL-denominated, under a conditional credit line for state productive-development projects) to a state government; executed by a project unit in the governor's-office orbit with sector secretariats as co-executors.
- Funds a state productive-development and competitiveness programme: (1) technology adoption, technical extension, market access and cooperatives for strategic sectors (roughly 15% of cost); (2) innovation and institutional strengthening (innovation environments, applied-AI research capacity, a new investment-promotion agency, investor single window, export platform, trade promotion, a biotech cluster; roughly 30%); (3) logistics, requalification of strategic road sections on one state highway (roughly 50%); (4) programme management (about 3%).
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum; the sponsor presents the design as replicable across states. Priority sectors: agro-industry, pharmaco-chemistry/biotech, bioeconomy, ICT and AI, logistics.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (low-complexity export basket, concentrated destinations, weak innovation conversion, logistics deficits, fiscal-incentive model under pressure from tax reform) links to one sectoral pillar plus three enabling axes and direct/indirect results lists; no testable assumptions stated, and the programme claims links to many PPA objectives.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 21 indicators verified; approximately 6 outcome-type (technology adoption share, high-value-market access share, mid/high-tech export share, SME exporters, income-concentration index, road quality rating) and approximately 15 output-type (units supported, environments, startups, centres, patents, agency projects, platform users, integrated procedures, women-led and Black-led firms supported, road length).
- Baselines: 9 of 21 have a stated non-zero value with a year (baseline_defined_share and baseline_with_year_share both 0.43). 12 baselines are zero (adoption and market-access shares, extension coverage, supported units, supported cooperatives, agency projects, investment value, platform exporters, integrated procedures, the two inclusion indicators, road length) and the CC gives no explanation for the zeros, so none were counted, though most are "project-supported beneficiaries" counts where zero is arguably inherent.
- Verification: each indicator lists data sources, mixing external statistics (national trade, patent, national statistics and highway-survey series) and project/agency registries; SMART tables per indicator, annual monitoring with end-of-programme consolidation; no independent verification or evaluation design beyond a monitoring product in component 4.
- Weaknesses: one indicator's name (AI R&D projects) conflicts with its monetary-unit baseline; one startup baseline is approximate; the income-concentration index is state-wide and hard to attribute; the road-length target is fixed while the road sections are to be defined in later studies (deferred identification); numeric targets all set to the final year.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Narrative only: new institutions, platforms, innovation environments and road upkeep to be absorbed into the permanent state structure, with own appropriations "foreseen" in the multi-year plan and LOA, progressive absorption of recurring costs, and complements from private partnerships, fees for specific services and cooperation. No OPEX/O&M figures, no multi-year table, no named budget lines; relies partly on future revenue growth.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Eight risks with impact/probability and named mitigation owners: legal/regulatory (including the law needed to create the new agency), procurement, environmental (licensing, safeguards plans), social (grievance mechanism), expropriation/resettlement, institutional capacity, political-electoral continuity (high impact, medium probability), and low uptake by firms. Sector-relevant and well structured, but no quantification.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, to be programmed by the finance secretariat with PPA/LDO/LOA compatibility; no LOA line or authorising law evidenced (to be secured).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A dedicated project unit still to be created in the governor's-office orbit, with seven sector bodies as co-executors, a steering committee to be set up, and a responsibility matrix annexed; capacity argued from sector experience rather than from prior lender-financed operations. Coordination across many secretariats is a structural risk.
