## Resumo
- Modality: direct investment loan to a state government, with a 20% financial counterpart from the state treasury (matches header).
- What is financed (generic): road-network rehabilitation and long-term performance-based maintenance contracts; family-farming technical assistance and productive-chain strengthening; inclusive/sustainable tourism development; rural environmental-management strengthening.
- Delivery model: direct public investment, executed by state secretariats/agencies under a dedicated project-management unit (UGP) subordinate to the state planning secretariat.
- Components: (1) transport & logistics infrastructure (majority of the budget; performance-based long-term road-maintenance contracts); (2) sustainable rural development (family-farming extension, cooperatives); (3) regional/sustainable development (inclusive tourism, rural environmental management); (4) project-management unit. Entity type: state government.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: O&M responsibilities assigned per component to the relevant state agencies (roads, rural-extension, tourism/environment), referencing earmarked budget actions, but NT scored the "maintenance/replacement cost forecast" sub-item at zero — no quantified post-program cost estimate was presented.
- Counterpart: secured — a 20% financial counterpart from the state treasury, distributed across components and disbursed pari passu with execution (consistent with header).
- A.3 Risk: legal risk low (state authorizing law already enacted by the legislature prior to the CC); resettlement/expropriation risk identified as possible for road-widening works but flagged by the NT as only partially addressed — assessed as a future-execution contingency, with no prior survey of its magnitude; environmental risk addressed via a planned EIA/Environmental Management Plan and declared adherence to the lender's safeguard standards; CC asserts works should not directly affect indigenous/traditional communities and may even improve their access/safety (proponent's claim, not independently verified by the NT).
- A.4 Implementation (UGP): subordinate to the state planning secretariat, with institutional-arrangement and operational-structure sub-items all scored at the ceiling by the NT, citing relevant experience from a prior lender-financed operation. Flag/internal tension: the same NT separately scored "experience with an external financial agent" at its floor value, stating the borrower showed no qualifying signed operation in the last decade (excluding operations under one year old) — in apparent tension with the UGP's own prior-operation experience credited elsewhere in the same document.
