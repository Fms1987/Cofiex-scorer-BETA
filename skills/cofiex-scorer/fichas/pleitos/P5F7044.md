## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan with Union guarantee to a municipal government (FONPLATA), direct execution by a municipal infrastructure secretariat with a programme management unit (UGP) to be created by municipal decree.
- Finances two works packages in a mid-sized urban municipality: open/closed channelisation of a stream with complementary micro-drainage (roughly three quarters of total cost) and a new short road link that relieves a congested access corridor (under a tenth of cost); plus consultancy for management/supervision, studies, external audit, land expropriation and the lender fee.
- Roughly 85% of cost is civil works (goods category); counterpart at the 20% minimum. Disbursement horizon of four years; preparation still at preliminary design stage (macro-drainage design and traffic studies under way).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-result chain is coherent (flooding of an uncanalised stretch and corridor congestion lead to works that lead to fewer floods and shorter trips), but there is no explicit assumptions list; environmental co-benefits (fewer emissions, bank protection) are asserted, not measured.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 9 indicators (verified), all outcome-type in nature (approximate judgement): 4 on mobility (travel times by car and by bus, reasons for not using public transport) and 5 on drainage (households and area exposed to floods, drainage spending, flood-damage road spending, property devaluation). No outputs among them.
- Baselines: all 9 have a stated baseline value (no zero baselines), so baseline_defined_share is 1.0; none states a reference year or period, so baseline_with_year_share is 0.0.
- Weaknesses: no verification mechanism, data source or measurement dates; the formulas are differences while the target column holds levels, so the target semantics are ambiguous; several are weakly attributable proxies (property devaluation, per-property drainage spending). NT notes missing deadlines and data availability.
- No external benchmark index used (no IDEB/SNIS-type standard).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. O&M is quantified as a yearly allocation of about 0.5% of investment, carried by the executing secretariat's own budget lines; legal backing and a dedicated O&M budget line not shown.
- Counterpart: to be secured. The executing secretariat is to build annual budgets with planning, procurement and finance bodies, citing Res. COFIEX 3/2019; no LOA line or authorising law shown yet. Alignment with the municipal PPA is stated (road paving and drainage actions) and the programme has municipal council approval.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative risk blocks (legal, procurement, expropriation, environmental, social, resettlement) with mitigations, but no matrix with probability/impact. Licensing: municipal licensing for low impact, otherwise state environmental agency; water-use permit from the state water authority; possible vegetation removal with compensation.
- Resettlement: a small number of families to be relocated under a social-rent law, plus expropriation of adjacent strips; costs earmarked in municipal budgets and in the loan (about 5-6% of cost); final numbers pending detailed design. No indigenous or quilombola populations affected.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP to be created by decree (draft attached), RACI matrix, consultancy for management and supervision; no prior experience with external lenders identified (NT scored the experience item low).
