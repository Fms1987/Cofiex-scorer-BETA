## Resumo
- Investment loan for a municipal government, direct execution by the infrastructure secretariat; roughly 80% civil works, remainder studies/design and financing fee.
- Components: multiple detention/retention reservoirs along one urban drainage basin, hydraulic reinforcement of road crossings and a culvert along an avenue, plus linear-park implementation (ecological restoration, leisure/sport infrastructure, environmental-education use) on public land within the same catchment.
- All works sited on existing public land, so no resettlement or land acquisition is needed for this stage.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum; Union guarantee requested; 3-year disbursement period.

Coherent chain (recurring flooding at specific chokepoints and a green-space deficit -> detention storage, crossing upgrades, linear parks -> fewer flood events, more usable green area) with explicit historical justification (decades of flood events, a fatal incident cited); assumptions on private co-funding of park maintenance are not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK/borderline ADEQUATE. 6 indicators verified; mix of outcome (flood-prone-segment count, green-area ratio, road-blockage time, bus-line disruption) and output/coverage-type (population reached by parks, students reached by environmental education).
