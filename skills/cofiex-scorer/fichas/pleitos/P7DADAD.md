## Resumo
- Modality: debt restructuring / refinancing (DPL-style policy-based loan, IBRD, 100% federal guarantee, counterpart waived under the debt-restructuring exemption).
- Finances early repayment of a selected set of domestic CDI-indexed bank loans (commercial and federal-bank lines, part already federally guaranteed), swapping them for a longer SOFR-based IBRD loan; the guarantee is a substitution, not new exposure.
- Single component, no physical works; delivery is direct by the municipal finance and planning secretariats of a capital municipality.
- Stated aim: lower debt cost (positive NPV on every refinanced loan) and ease short-term budget pressure by lengthening the profile; municipality presents it as strategic refinancing, not distress (CAPAG A).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Short causal chain (swap costly short debt for cheaper long debt, NPV gain, lower debt-service share of revenue, more investment room); no explicit assumptions on rates/FX or on how freed space reaches social investment.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 2 indicators (verified), both outcome-type fiscal measures: NPV saving of the restructured loans and debt service as a share of net current revenue (target reduction by 2027). Reduced count is structural for this modality.
- Baselines: both stated with value and reference year (2025); baseline_defined_share 1.0, with-year 1.0. No zero baselines.
- Verification: data sources named (municipal finance and planning secretariats) but no independent verification mechanism; no external benchmarks. Indicator 1 baseline is a present-value figure, formula wording ambiguous (new minus old NPV, sign unclear).
