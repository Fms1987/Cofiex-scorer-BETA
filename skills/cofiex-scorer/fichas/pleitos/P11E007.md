## Resumo
Investment operation for a state government, executed directly by the state health secretariat (direct delivery model, no on-lending). Finances primary-care network expansion and equipping, decentralization/regionalization of medium- and high-complexity care (regional outpatient specialty units, intensive-care capacity, psychosocial-care and rehabilitation facilities), diagnostic-support services (public-health laboratory network and cold-chain logistics), and health-information/telehealth modernization (electronic records, teleconsultation). A dedicated program-management unit (UGP) component covers execution support, works supervision and mid-term/impact evaluation studies. Framed as a second phase building on a prior operation financed by the same lender.
