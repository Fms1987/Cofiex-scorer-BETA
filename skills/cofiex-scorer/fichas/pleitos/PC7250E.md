## Resumo
- Guarantee-backed global credit loan to a wholly Union-owned federal guarantee agency to create the initial financial reserve (backing) of a redesigned export-credit-insurance scheme; the Union fund remains only as last-resort backstop. Indemnities would be paid first from the new reserve.
- Almost the entire loan finances one asset-formation product (constituting the reserve assets, to be invested in low-risk, liquid instruments); the other two components are non-financed institutional products: operational set-up and regulation (actuarial note, risk-underwriting rules, insurance-regulator registration, presidential decree and trade-council resolution, public hiring) and expansion of MSME access (system adaptation, digital marketplaces, tailored portfolio-coverage products, strategic plan and cooperation agreements).
- Intermediated model: the agency underwrites and indemnifies; end beneficiaries are exporters, with emphasis on MSMEs. No counterpart (100% federal ownership); lender technical cooperation is provided separately and pro bono.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Two explicit problems (scheme tied to the federal budget so indemnities compete with other spending; concentration on large exporters with negligible MSME share) map to two objectives and a layered risk-sharing logic (reserve first, Union last), supported by legal opinions. Assumptions on regulation approval and actuarial sustainability are acknowledged as risks but not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 2 indicators (verified), both outputs of volume: annual value of operations approved under the new scheme and approved financing to MSMEs, each measured 365 days after implementation. No outcome indicator (e.g. exports by MSMEs, claims ratio, budget-impact reduction, reserve solvency).
- Baselines: 1 of 2 stated as a value (MSME volume); the other is 0 for a not-yet-existing product, which the CC implies by being new (counted as defined here, giving 0.5); no reference year for either (year share 0).
- Verification means refer to an annex of indicators; the main text gives no data source or evaluation design. Targets are single points but the MSME target mixes currencies/units with the first indicator (dollar billions vs local-currency millions), and the text also cites longer-term MSME and beneficiary projections not carried into the matrix.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE on paper. Reserve invested in low-risk assets plus premium income is meant to become self-sustaining and service the loan; leverage ratio of the existing fund is cited as a reference, a cost comparison against Treasury and domestic banks/other MDBs is provided (annex), premium-sharing split with the Union still to be set by norm. Personnel costs from operating revenue, not from the loan. No stress-tested actuarial result in the CC (actuarial note is a future product).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: PARTIAL. Legal/regulatory risks identified with mitigations (decree can be revoked by Congress; collegiate resolution may lack quorum; disbursement conditioned on regulation); HR/hiring delay risk with fallback; environmental/social handled via due diligence and Equator-style alignment. No formal matrix; market/credit/FX risk of the reserve only addressed by future investment and underwriting policies.
- Counterpart: none required (federal state-owned borrower). Contingent exposure of the Union as last-resort remains and is the core fiscal point.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Uses the existing guarantees management area, but needs significant staff expansion and public hiring pending state-owned-company oversight approval; no separate UGP; responsibilities split among executive managements, RACI matrix annexed.
