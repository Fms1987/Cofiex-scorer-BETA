## Resumo
Investment operation for a state government, delivered through a flagship academic/referral public hospital acting as executing entity alongside the state health secretariat. Finances modernization of clinical/assistance areas and medical equipment, workforce training and retention programs, digital-health systems (electronic patient record, patient-tracking app), waste-management/energy-efficiency (ESG) upgrades, and project management/governance. Delivery model: direct, state-borrower with a dedicated project-management unit to be created by decree.

A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE — verified count of 15 indicators (the header's heuristic count differs; this ficha uses the verified count), mixing outcome indicators (patient/staff satisfaction, work-accident rate, absenteeism) with output/process indicators (equipment downtime, record-system rollout, waste-per-ton, vendor due-diligence coverage); outcome-vs-output split roughly balanced, leaning output-heavy on the digital/process side. Baselines: about 73% of indicators state a baseline value under the strict rule — four indicators start at an unexplained zero (construction-completion %, record-system rollout %, project-delivery-proportion %, obsolete-equipment-replacement %) that plausibly reflect new/not-yet-started tracking but are not explicitly justified as such in the reviewed text, so they are counted as undefined. None of the baselines state a reference year. No indicator verification mechanism (source/frequency beyond the measurement cadence) is described. One benchmark-style metric (an international patient/staff-satisfaction index methodology) is used. No wide-range indicators found.
