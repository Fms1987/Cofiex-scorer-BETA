## Resumo
- Multi-sector investment loan (CAF, small ticket, band <50M USD, 5-year disbursement) to a mid-sized municipality; direct execution by a project unit under the municipal governance secretariat with line secretariats as co-executors and a contracted project-management office.
- Five components: (1) health and education infrastructure (primary-care units, a specialised inclusion centre, fire-safety and electrical retrofits; roughly 30% of cost); (2) basic sanitation and urban infrastructure (urban stream channelling and valley-edge urbanisation, urban park, protected-area restoration, landfill cell expansion, active mobility; roughly 40%); (3) digital transformation of municipal services (health scheduling, environmental-licensing system, e-government; roughly 15%); (4) gender and social inclusion (vocational courses, neurodiversity support, humanised-service training; under 10%); (5) programme management (about 3%).
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum. Sector keyword in the header is "other"; the programme is an integrated urban-development package.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Seven numbered problems map to five specific objectives, components and products, and each indicator carries an explicit coherence statement with its objective; no stated assumptions or results chain beyond that.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE (close to STRONG). 10 indicators verified; approximately 6 outcome-type (flood-prone points, water-quality class, green area per inhabitant, digital scheduling share, licensing processing time, poverty share) and approximately 4 output-type (primary-care service volume, landfill remaining life, sensory-room coverage of neurodivergent students, cycle-network length per capita). Each has a SMART table, data source, annual measurement and a 2030 target.
- Baselines: all 10 have a stated value and a reference year or period (baseline_defined_share and baseline_with_year_share both 1.0). Two zero baselines were counted because the CC explains them: landfill remaining life (current capacity stated as exhausted) and digital scheduling (the system does not exist yet).
- Benchmarks and verification: a standard state water-quality index, the national single registry for poverty, municipal health and education registries, geospatial mapping and civil-defence records; data collection is municipal, with no independent verification or evaluation product.
- Inconsistencies: the primary-care indicator's SMART text cites unit counts different from the component's physical targets; the water-quality indicator is described as a class but measured in the SMART text as a share of stretches; a cycle-network target is mislabelled in units; baseline for the water indicator is a coarse class ("regular") with two different years; the poverty indicator is acknowledged as only indirectly linked to its objective.

Project unit across eight bodies with a RACI matrix, a technical directorate with multi-disciplinary staff, a contracted project-management office of several dozen consultants, high governance-maturity certification, track record with federal transfers; CC admits RACI complexity as a coordination risk.
