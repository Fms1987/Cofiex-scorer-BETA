## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type external credit (IDB, <50M USD band) in the Subnational-Fiscal sublimit; counterpart at roughly 10% of the loan (about 9% of total cost); borrower is a small state government with Union guarantee.
- Financed: modernisation of a state finance department and its partner bodies, with emphasis on adapting to the consumption-tax reform: strategic governance, people management, IT and security, transparency and fiscal education, tax administration and tax litigation, budget/financial systems, on-site renewable energy and rainwater reuse, sustainable and inclusive public procurement, debt collection and court-ordered payments.
- Five components: governance and fiscal transparency (roughly a third of cost); tax administration and tax litigation (about 40%); financial administration and public spending (about a fifth); legal management (a few percent); project management (a few percent). The tax-reform harmonisation product is the single largest item.
- Delivery model: direct execution by a coordination unit in the finance department; partner bodies (legal-defence office, comptroller, planning, administration, climate-change institute) execute their products under a cooperation agreement still to be signed. Entity type: subnational executive, repeat user of this credit line.
- Eight complementary objectives, including gender equality, environmental sustainability and transparency, sit beside the two core revenue and spending objectives.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Eleven numbered challenges (from a lender maturity assessment and state planning documents) map onto solutions and expected results per axis, with a horizontal problem-solution-result matrix annexed and an objective tree; impact claim leans on a lender impact evaluation of the first operation in the line. No explicit testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 16 indicators verified; approximately 7 outcome/status measures (rankings, tax-cost efficiency, tax-to-GDP ratio, debt recovery, current savings, firm growth, strategic-plan delivery) and approximately 9 output/system-delivery or training measures.
- Verification: every indicator has a named source (internal reports, state revenue and budget data, transparency ranking, national business map); each carries a self-assessed SMART block that is mostly boilerplate. Benchmarks: national transparency radar and CAPAG current-savings band.
- Baselines (v3 rule): 15 of 16 defined (about 0.94); 14 carry a reference year (about 0.88). Zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains them (no solar generation yet, no systems integrated yet, no judgments in the new electronic process yet, no capacity built yet); the women-entrepreneur training zero is unexplained and counted undefined. The carbon-asset indicator has a value without year (the NT says baseline and year are missing).
- Inconsistencies: debt-recovery indicator labelled a rate with a currency unit and a percentage target; the current-savings target says 92% while the annual path falls; the tax-reform integration indicator gives one system count in its baseline data and a different one in its target text; first-instance judgment indicator has identical numerator and denominator; the training indicator has different annual figures in text and schedule; a monetary carbon indicator has a baseline equal to a past one-off flow.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual post-project upkeep quantified at about 1.4% of project value in an annexed cost sheet, existing maintenance contracts annexed, and recurring budget provision cited in the current annual budget law; the programme-level multi-year plan includes the credit line's objective. No year-by-category table.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Eleven risks with impact/probability and mitigations: legal authorisation, execution delays, governance turnover, resistance to change, sustainability of investments, counterpart, monitoring, environmental, social, tax-reform delay, low UCP experience (the last rated high impact / medium probability). Environmental licensing not required (no civil works of size); e-waste and energy-use mitigations.
- Counterpart: "to be secured": mitigation says it will be placed in the multi-year plan and annual budget with a specific reserve; no existing budget line shown for it.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A coordination unit inside the finance department, a procurement committee and product leaders formally designated; the CC itself flags low experience of the unit and product leaders in complex projects and proposes training and mentoring; the unit is to be created by state decree.
