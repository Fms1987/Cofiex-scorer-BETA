## Resumo
- Guarantee operation (not a loan): an IDB guarantee, with federal sovereign backing and a counter-guarantee from the federal executing institution, covering about a year of lease payments owed to a private investor-lessor under a long-term built-to-suit contract for a public vaccine and biopharmaceutical plant. Ticket 100-200M USD band, no counterpart.
- The guarantee replaces a domestic guarantee-fund mechanism judged too costly; the works themselves are privately financed through the lease, with ownership reverting to the public producer at the end of the contract. Earlier public budget investment (land, site preparation, core production equipment) is already made.
- Single component and single product (the guarantee); the disbursement period is only a nominal reference. Entity type: federal public health-industry foundation unit, with the health ministry as purchaser.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (clear chain from guarantee to a successful tender, plant delivery, higher domestic production capacity and wider access to immunobiologicals; diagnosis is detailed and coherent, assumptions on tender success and operation not tested).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 distinct indicators (the cost-comparison indicator is split into two scenario rows, giving 6 table rows; the NT also counts 5): production volume of exclusive vaccines bought by the national programme, overall production capacity, product portfolio breadth, cost comparison between two guarantee mechanisms, and share of production processes digitalised. Mix is approximate: about 3 output/capacity, 1 financial-structure, 1 modernisation indicator; no health-outcome indicator.
- Baselines: all 5 have a stated value; the one zero baseline (digitalisation) is counted because the CC presents it as unprecedented. No baseline year and no target dates are stated, so baseline_with_year_share = 0.0 (NT also notes missing deadlines). Verification: management production reports (present but internal).
- Weaknesses: the cost indicator measures guarantee-mechanism choice rather than service delivery; targets lack timeframes.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG on paper. An external feasibility study (annexed) shows lease payments, staffing and operating costs covered by the producer's own sales revenue, in a conservative scenario, without health-ministry budget support; revenue upside from products not modelled. Operating-cost detail sits in the annex, not in the CC text.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Two main tender risks (bid pricing and default-guarantee availability) with mitigants; legal acts, audit-court review of the tender, environmental licences already held, no expropriation or resettlement. Gaps per NT: no failed-tender scenario; guarantee requires specific legislative authorisation, anticipated by the CC; staffing of a large workforce depends on hiring routes still under discussion.
- Counterpart: none required (guarantee); reimbursement if called would come from the producer's mandatory purchase-related budget line, with sovereign backing.
- A.4 Implementation: federal executing institution with a RACI matrix; NT accepts it because the private lessor builds and maintains, but notes unclear knowledge of lender procurement and safeguard policies.
