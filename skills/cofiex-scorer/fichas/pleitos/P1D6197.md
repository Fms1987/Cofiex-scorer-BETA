## Resumo
- Investment-type loan (IBRD, USD band below 50M, counterpart roughly 9% of total cost, well under the usual 20% minimum) to a state government under a multi-state fiscal-management programme; direct execution by the state, no on-lending.
- Public-expenditure efficiency and public-financial-management package, 3 components: (1) cross-cutting systems for payroll and workforce planning, pension-benefit auditing, procurement automation, investment/PPP management, strategic planning, asset management and cybersecurity/cloud (about two thirds of cost); (2) a hospital and health-finance management platform (roughly a quarter); (3) project and change management (a few percent).
- Almost entirely goods, software and consultancy; no civil works, so environmental and resettlement risks are declared not applicable. Delivery through a project management unit housed in a development agency, with implementation units in the line secretariats.
- Three attempts: the first two were not approved at the meeting, the third was approved.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problems by theme (payroll, pensions, procurement, investment, assets, health finance) map to 11 products and expected results, and many results cite federal strategic objectives; the chain from systems to fiscal savings is asserted rather than shown, with no explicit assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE to WEAK. 18 indicators (verified), approximately 9 outcome-type (personnel spending ratio, pension savings, procurement savings, tender duration, execution rate, patrimonial revenue, health waste and revenue) and 9 output/adoption-type (audit coverage, price-database use, PPP projects, plan alignment, GIP share, process automation, system roll-out, digital transformation). Each has formula, source agency and a 5-year annual target path; verification relies on state administrative systems, no independent verification.
- Baselines: only 3 of 18 carry a baseline value (personnel-to-revenue ratio, tender duration, investment execution); the rest start at zero or are index/cumulative measures with zero by construction, which the CC does not explain, so none of those zeros was counted as defined. None states a calendar reference year (only an "Ano Base" column), hence the with-year share of 0. The health-waste baseline is flagged in the CC itself as to be revised.
- Inconsistencies and risks: the context cites a personnel-spending ratio different from the indicator baseline; several formulas are complex relative-to-base indices that need a baseline not yet measured; some targets are very steep (audit coverage to 100%, tender time halved).
- Benchmarks: internal-audit capability model level 3 and the World Bank programme forum are cited; no comparators for savings estimates. Wide-range indicators: none.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE to WEAK. System upkeep is financed by the loan during execution; afterwards costs pass to the state budget, with the project unit to prepare cost projections and a sustainability plan during preparation. Recurring costs are not quantified in the CC; the cost-benefit analysis and a return threshold are deferred to preparation. The state IT agency is to host and maintain the systems; contracts require knowledge transfer, warranty and source-code transfer.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. A matrix of 9 risks with impact, probability and mitigations: institutional capacity, procurement, legal acts (an authorising state law still to pass), technical design, stakeholder continuity over political cycles, plus environmental, social, expropriation and resettlement rated not applicable because there are no works. Licensing risk is not relevant.
- Counterpart (about 9%): small annual amounts scheduled across the 5 years; no budget instrument (LOA/PPA/law) cited, so effectively to be secured. The authorising law was said to be in preparation for submission to the legislature.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A steering committee by decree, a project unit by ordinance with the World Bank minimum staffing, implementation units in each co-executing agency, and a responsibility matrix; no transfers between agencies. The state has a running IDB-financed fiscal-modernisation loan, but the CC says the final arrangement will be fixed after the lender's capacity assessments.
