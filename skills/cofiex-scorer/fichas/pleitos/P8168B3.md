## Resumo
- Modality: performance-based credit line (DLI/KPI-linked, PBR-style) for climate action, extended to a state-owned development bank acting as financial intermediary; disbursements and a spread discount are tied to indicator attainment rather than to civil works.
- What is financed (generic): decarbonization and climate-resilience credit products originated by the intermediary to public- and private-sector sub-borrowers, plus an institutional capacity-building sub-component (staff training, risk-methodology upgrades).
- Delivery model: intermediated/on-lending — the bank originates loans to sub-borrowers from its own balance sheet using the external line; no direct public works are executed by the proponent itself.
- Components: (1) decarbonization (green-taxonomy update, GHG-linked sustainability loans, mitigation loans incl. a vulnerable-client line); (2) climate resilience (capacity-building and resilience-linked loans, incl. a vulnerable-client line); cross-cutting targeting of low-IDH municipalities, women- and minority-led firms. Entity type: state development bank (financial intermediary) with federal guarantee.

- Counterpart: none required — explicitly waived given the proponent's legal nature as a financial intermediary (not "to be secured": not applicable).
