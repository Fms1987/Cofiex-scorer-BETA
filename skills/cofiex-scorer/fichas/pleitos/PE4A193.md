## Resumo
Investment operation for a capital municipality, financed directly (not intermediated), combining water/sanitation
infrastructure (water supply, sewerage, urban drainage) with urbanization (roads, public spaces), housing
resettlement/regularization, institutional strengthening (M&E system, capacity building) and programme management/UGP
support. Delivery model is direct execution by the municipal administration with a water/sanitation utility responsible
for O&M of water and sewer networks. Entity type: capital municipality (direct borrower).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE — the CC lays out a clear diagnostic-to-intervention narrative (sanitation deficit and
flooding in a low-income riverside area → integrated sanitation/drainage/urbanization works + resettlement +
institutional strengthening → improved health, mobility and reduced flood exposure), but does not formalize testable
assumptions or a causal-chain diagram.
A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE — 9 VERIFIED indicators, baseline_defined_share 0.78 (7/9); the two undefined baselines
are zero values for a bike-lane-length indicator and a resettled-population share, both plausibly "new asset" zeros
(no such infrastructure/process existed before the programme) but the CC does not state this explanation explicitly,
so they were counted as undefined per the baseline rule. baseline_with_year_share is 0.0: the indicator table gives
baseline values and targets but no explicit reference year per indicator (only general diagnostic data elsewhere cites
a benchmark year). Outcome-vs-output split is approximate: about 3 indicators read as outcome-type (environmental
water-quality load, waterborne-disease incidence, untreated-sewage discharge) and about 6 as output/coverage-type
(sewerage coverage ratio, flooded-area ratio, sidewalk/paving ratio, bike-lane length, resettled-population share).
No verification mechanism (source/means of verification) is stated for any indicator in the table. Benchmark used:
a national sanitation information system benchmark in the diagnostic section. No wide-range or deferred indicators
identified; no internal inconsistencies found beyond the unexplained zero baselines noted above.
