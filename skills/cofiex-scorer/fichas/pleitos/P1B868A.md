## Resumo
- Modality: external credit operation with Union guarantee, essentially a subnational debt restructuring (refinancing of CDI-indexed debts with domestic banks) from IBRD, USD band 200-400M, single-year disbursement, no counterpart (Art. 3, II of Res. COFIEX 03/2019 exempts principal restructuring).
- Only the debt-restructuring component carries financing (100% of the loan); the fiscal-sustainability and green-economy components are policy/legal measures at zero cost, in the style of a policy-reform package.
- Policy measures: debt-management unit strengthening, tax-compliance and rate realignment, spending-decree update, a public-investment management framework (project bank), a corporate green-seal regulation, low-risk activity list expansion, one-stop business registration, and a renewable-energy siting decree.
- Borrower is a state government; executing secretariats for the fiscal and green pillars are different bodies (finance/planning vs. environment/commercial registry).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem (revenue loss after tax-rate legislation, rising personnel spending, costly CDI-linked debt, no investment project bank) maps to measures and expected outcomes; assumptions (legislative approval, decree issuance) are described as low-risk but not framed as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 6 indicators verified (approximate split: 2 outcome-type fiscal ratios, 4 output/institutional-type); all 6 have a baseline value, none states a reference year; zero baseline counted for the investment-management framework (new instrument, implicitly explained). Hence baseline_defined 100%, with-year 0%.
- Benchmarks: CAPAG letter grade (encoded numerically) and a current-savings ratio; the debt-restructuring component itself (the only funded one) has no dedicated indicator.
- Verification mechanism absent: no data source, frequency or intermediate targets; the NT also flagged missing timing of target verification.
- Inconsistencies: current-savings ratio target is a marginal improvement, small relative to the stated fiscal stress; green-seal indicator starts from a very small base so targets are tiny absolute numbers.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. No O&M (no physical assets); sustainability rests on lower debt service in early years (relief then partly offset by longer tail), legal instruments (decrees/laws) for the reforms. Debt service already in LOA/LDO/PPA, so PPA inclusion argued as duplicative (Art. 7 par. 7, Res. Senado 43/2001).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: PARTIAL. Only legal-act risk is developed (authorizing state law plus counterpart-type reform laws, mitigated by draft texts and legal review); contracting, resettlement and expropriation sections blank; environmental/social impact described only as positive. Market risk of exchange/CDI/SOFR mentioned but not as a matrix.
- Counterpart: none required (principal restructuring exemption). Early-repayment penalty acknowledged in the financial analysis (annexed technical note).
- A.4 Implementation: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. Responsibility matrix was judged generic by the NT (broad secretariat named without teams); no UGP described. Regulatory-licensing gap for renewable siting is flagged in the diagnosis.
