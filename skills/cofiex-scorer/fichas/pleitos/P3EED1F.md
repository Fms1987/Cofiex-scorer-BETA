## Resumo
Investment operation, direct lending to a state government (subnational entity). Finances an integrated climate-resilience / disaster-risk-reduction program combining structural flood-control works (river-channel improvements and water-retention structures, with associated environmental-compensation programs), basin-level risk assessment and planning, expansion/modernization of a hydrometeorological monitoring and early-warning system, and local capacity-building/institutional-strengthening (including a social-protection measure for vulnerable groups and a small-business recovery credit line). Delivery is direct, executed by the state government through a dedicated program-management structure coordinating several state secretariats.
