## Resumo
- Investment loan (external credit operation with federal guarantee) from AFD, EUR-denominated, to a municipal government acting through a central planning secretariat's project coordination unit; direct execution by municipal secretariats, no intermediation.
- Integrated urban-environmental programme with six components: studies for socio-environmental recovery of vulnerable areas, the recovery works themselves (informal-settlement upgrading, land regularisation, resettlement into social housing, recovery of occupied conservation land; roughly a quarter of cost), studies for river rehabilitation, river rehabilitation and flood-mitigation works with a linear park using nature-based solutions (roughly 60% of cost), municipal planning (housing, climate and gender plans), and programme management.
- Applies under the environmental/climate sublimit. Counterpart at the 20% minimum, offered in kind (municipally owned land with independent appraisals) rather than in cash. Loan terms: 20 years total with 5 grace, variable-rate (Euribor plus spread), 0.5% commitment and appraisal fees.
- 60-month execution horizon; indicator targets run to the end of the programme.

Benchmarks: water-quality index used; no external comparators.
- Wide-range or deferred indicators: none seen; the satisfaction and biodiversity indicators have loose measurement design.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual upkeep of the park, public areas and urban infrastructure is estimated by responsible secretariat (order of magnitude stated in BRL, not reproduced) and to be absorbed in existing budgets; housing upkeep falls on families and condominiums; street lighting goes into the municipal PPP contract. No multi-year O&M table; budget lines to be created in LDO/LOA at execution time; future plans' funding to come through PPA/LDO.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. A matrix of 17 risks with impact, probability and mitigation (regulatory/licensing, authorising law, UCP continuity, procurement, contractor default, involuntary resettlement, social, environmental). Licensing is flagged as a medium-probability, high-impact risk for the river works, mitigated by early dialogue with licensing bodies and prior baseline studies. Resettlement follows the lender's safeguard standard, with lender-funded technical assistance for the resettlement framework planned.
- Counterpart: land owned by the municipality, appraised independently; PPA/LOA of the current year annexed; the authorising law is drafted but awaits COFIEX approval before going to the council (flagged as a risk) - to be secured.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. UCP created by decree, senior career staff across financial, procurement, civil-works, sanitation and environmental roles, a standing procurement commission, and prior experience with other multilateral loans (not with this lender).
