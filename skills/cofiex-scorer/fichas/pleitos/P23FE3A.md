## Resumo
Investment operation, direct lending to a state government, executed by a state-owned urban-rail (metro) operator. Finances acquisition and implementation of signaling/train-control, electrical-power, auxiliary, and telecommunications systems for an existing metro line's physical extension (civil works financed separately/already underway), plus management/supervision consultancies and an independent safety assessment. A smaller technical/institutional component funds sustainable-engineering and climate-resilience practices, PPP-structuring studies for social housing on surplus operator land, and integration of harassment-reporting channels. Delivery model is direct execution by the long-standing sector SOE, not intermediated.
