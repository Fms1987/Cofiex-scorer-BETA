## Resumo
- Modality: policy-based loan (PBL/DPL) with a single disbursement, second operation of a two-step programmatic series, for a capital municipality under a federal fiscal-balance plan (PEF). No investment works; the header sector label (fund intermediation) is a keyword artefact.
- Content: prior actions in two pillars: (1) fiscal and pension management (centralised pension administration regulation, green tax incentives and a voluntary carbon-credit market framework); (2) low-carbon, climate-resilient urban development (bus/BRT financial and operational model with digital ticketing, active-mobility network plan, low-emission district decree, low-carbon municipal fleet procurement notice).
- Delivery: direct, by the municipal finance secretariat as coordinating nucleus plus a pension institute and a transport secretariat as executing units. Single financial component carrying 100% of the loan; policy products carry zero cost.
- Financial terms: 20-year term, 12-month grace, SOFR-based rate, 0.25% commission; counterpart waived under the fiscal-restructuring exception (0%).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (fiscal deterioration, arrears, pandemic shock) leads to two pillars and a list of prior actions, linked to the first operation of the series; the causal chain from reforms to fiscal outcomes is stated but assumptions are not made testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Verified count: 2 indicators, both fiscal outcomes (current savings ratio and net cash availability), both with baseline values and reference year (2020) and a target year (2023); both baselines counted as defined, no zero baselines. Share with baseline 1.0 and with year 1.0. No verification mechanism stated. Pillar 2 (urban/climate) has no outcome indicator at all, only policy products with unit targets of 1 (decree/law/contract/notice), so the thematic half of the operation is output-only. Reduced indicator count is typical of the PBL typology.
- Inconsistency: a tax-incentive bill is still pending in the legislature while the corresponding product is listed as a prior action; the net cash baseline is described as negative but entered as a positive figure.
- Benchmarks: none cited for the indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for a fiscal operation. Sustainability rests on a municipal fiscal-regime law that triggers containment measures if CAPAG falls to C or D, plus the federal fiscal-balance plan; no OPEX table (not applicable to a PBL). The CC itself admits that part of the recent fiscal improvement came from one-off privatisation proceeds.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. No matrix; a single procurement risk (bid challenge) with a legal-office mitigation; environmental and social risks declared positive/none; no licensing, expropriation or resettlement items.
- Counterpart: none required (fiscal-restructuring exception); PPA alignment declared with the municipal PPA (central objectives and cross-cutting themes cited).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Coordination by the finance secretariat with prior experience of a similar municipal PBL; executing units named; responsibility matrix only referred to annexes.
