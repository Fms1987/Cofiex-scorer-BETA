## Resumo
- Modality: direct investment programme of a mid-sized municipality (interior city, relatively low IDHM) financed by a regional development bank; 80% loan / 20% counterpart (minimum).
- Multi-sector urban package: sustainable mobility (arterial roads, cycling infrastructure), environment and energy efficiency (LED street lighting, solar plant, recycling facility), territorial/fiscal management modernisation, social housing and slum upgrading, local economic development (wholesale market, public market, digital training hub).
- Delivery: municipal executing agency with a programme management unit (UGP) and RACI responsibility matrix; studies and designs bundled in a first sub-component, programme management and supervision as a small second component.
- Disbursement horizon up to 5 years.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problems-solutions-expected results chain is coherent across five thematic areas; expected results are described loosely and assumptions are not made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified count: 9 indicators; roughly 8 result-level (travel time on two corridors, cycling users, selective waste collection coverage, LED share, solar share of supply, households lacking decent housing, trained minority-group people) and 1 impact-type (extreme-poverty headcount in the social registry).
- Baselines: 6 of 9 with a stated non-zero baseline value, all with a reference year (2023 or a stated month); 3 baselines are 0 (selective collection, solar share, minority training) and were NOT counted as defined because the CC does not explain them, though they plausibly reflect new assets.
- Verification: data sources are official platforms/registries available to the municipality; targets have only a reference year, no progressive milestones.
- Coverage gaps per NT: no indicators for the headwater protection objective or the public-building revitalisation/innovation objective; no additional impact indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Maintenance assigned to municipal secretariats under existing maintenance contracts (roads, cleaning, lighting) funded from own tax revenue and the street-lighting contribution, already in LDO/LOA; only a small share of those contracts is earmarked for the new works, and post-programme maintenance costs are not quantified for the programme itself.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal, contracting and environmental risks tabulated per product; licensing responsibility assigned to municipal bodies per product. CC states no expropriation/resettlement risk, while the NT notes some projects are under analysis for expropriation and housing on public land deserves more consideration.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, to be provided from constitutional tax transfers/revenues; budget inclusion by specific law after COFIEX recommendation, so "to be secured"; PPA actions are listed for each axis, but the NT found the PPA annex hard to trace.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG-to-ADEQUATE. Complete institutional arrangement with UGP, RACI matrix and technical staff with procurement and works-supervision experience; borrower has no external-credit experience in the last 10 years.
- Prior works: executive designs, environmental licensing and land matters reported in progress.
