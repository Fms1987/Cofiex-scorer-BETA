## Resumo
- Modality: investment-type external credit (IDB, 50-100M USD band) in the Subnational-Fiscal sublimit, with counterpart at roughly 9% of total cost; borrower is a state government with Union guarantee and a very strong fiscal profile (top CAPAG grade stated in the CC).
- Financed: modernisation of a state finance department and partner bodies: governance, risk, people and IT, public procurement, transparency and fiscal education, advanced data/AI, tax administration (registry, collection, inspection, administrative litigation, taxpayer services, consumption-tax reform readiness), budget/cash/accounting/treasury systems, on-site solar and rainwater reuse, and legal-defence modernisation (debt collection, court-ordered payments).
- Five components: governance and fiscal transparency (roughly 40% of cost); tax administration and litigation (about a third); financial administration (about a sixth); legal management (under a tenth); project management (a few percent).
- Delivery model: centralised execution by a coordination unit inside the finance department with product leaders, a dedicated tax-reform team and two governance committees; partner bodies execute their products under cooperation agreements, with no fund transfers. Entity type: subnational executive, repeat user of this credit line.
