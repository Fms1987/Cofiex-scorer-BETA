## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IBRD to a capital municipality, with federal guarantee; the proponent asked for the annual environmental/climate sublimit. Counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Financed: an integrated upgrading of low-income hillside and valley communities in one urban basin, using nature-based solutions (green and hybrid slope and drainage works) to remove landslide and flood risk, plus resettlement and housing improvement, water and sewer coverage, street and public-space works, green-area recovery and social/labour-market work.
- Two components: about 85% urban and socio-environmental works; about 15% institutional strengthening (risk-management tools, replication studies, staff training) and project management.
- Delivery: direct execution by a municipal planning foundation hosting a dedicated UGP, with several municipal secretariats as co-executors.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG-to-ADEQUATE. A logical framework links problems, solutions, results, targets and specific objectives, and each indicator names the objective and the PPA strategic area it feeds; assumptions (population adherence, new informal occupation) appear in the risk section rather than in the causal chain.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 10 indicators, roughly 6 outcome-type (risk-area elimination, resettlement, housing improvement, water and sewer coverage, training coverage, replication pipeline) and 4 output-type (approximate judgement). Data come from annual primary collection by the executing foundation; no independent verification is described, and intermediate targets sit in an annex not available to me.
- Baselines: 5 of 10 have a stated baseline value (share 0.5): initial risk area, water coverage, sewer coverage, public-space area per inhabitant and the replication count. Resettlement, housing improvement, road length, green area and training coverage give only a denominator or universe, with no stated starting value (implicit zero not explained, counted undefined). No baseline states a reference year, so the with-year share is 0.
- Targets are mostly 100% coverage by the end of the project; some are ambitious (public open space per inhabitant from a very low level to a many-fold increase; sewer coverage from a low base to universal).
- Benchmark use: PPA strategic-area key indicators and an existing habitat index are referenced; no external benchmarks.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE on institutional allocation, PARTIAL on figures. O&M is assigned by asset type (utility operator for water/sewer, municipal maintenance secretariat for roads, drainage, squares and green areas, public-order secretariat for lighting and cleaning), with a co-management model that employs community maintainers and a maintenance and monitoring plan already prepared for part of the area. No annual O&M cost table or committed budget lines are given.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: five risks (legal, inter-institutional, resettlement, social adherence, technical). Resettlement is rated medium impact and high probability, the only high-probability item, mitigated by prior large-scale resettlement experience and an existing resettlement framework to be detailed into a plan. Technical risk stems from preliminary rather than executive designs. Environmental licensing is not a stand-alone risk item.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, to be secured; the authorising law is still to be sent to the municipal council.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP with dedicated full-time director and managers (infrastructure, social, environmental, resettlement, financial, procurement), a coordination committee and a permanent technical articulation nucleus; the model replicates earlier UGPs of the borrower with several MDBs.
