## Resumo
- Modality: external credit to a federal development bank (state-owned financial institution) with Union guarantee, for on-lending to private sub-borrowers in green and sustainable infrastructure (renewable energy, logistics, sustainable urban mobility, others to be agreed with the lender).
- Delivery: intermediated. The bank lends under its ordinary credit policies and the multilateral disburses to the bank, possibly by reimbursement of eligible, already-contracted operations; portfolio and eligibility criteria to be defined jointly with the lender after the CC.
- Structure: one component and one product covering 100% of cost; counterpart from the bank and sub-borrowers at 50% of total cost; 7.5-year total term, 24-month grace, six-month SOFR plus a lender spread of 84 bps, 0.25% upfront fee and up to 0.25% commitment fee; disbursement over 4 years, first-time relationship with this lender.

- A.1 Theory of Change: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. Rationale is funding diversification and macro context plus climate (NDC) alignment; the chain from credit line to sector outcomes is asserted, not decomposed by sub-segment, and no assumptions are stated.

Verified count: 4 indicators, approximately 3 outputs/financial volumes (financing granted, average sub-loan term, total investment mobilised) and 1 outcome-type indicator (GHG emissions reduced per year, expressed as planned vs. realised percentage, with no tonnage baseline). No verification mechanism, no measurement dates (NT flagged this), no benchmarks.
- Internal inconsistency: the percentage-based emissions indicator has no absolute reference, so it cannot be verified independently of the bank's own reporting.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. No OPEX/O&M quantification or post-programme instrument (NT scored this item very low); sustainability is implicit in the bank's standing credit activity and funding policy.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. No matrix; sub-borrower credit, procurement, expropriation and resettlement risks are assigned to the bank or sub-borrowers; environmental risk handled through socio-environmental classification and licensing, with safeguards to be agreed with the lender; procurement risk deemed immaterial because financing targets already-contracted operations.
- Counterpart: provided from the bank's ordinary funds and sub-borrowers, in the 50% share; stated as foreseen in the bank's investment budget (not an LOA/PPA item).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Executor is the borrower bank with an established credit-approval flow and strong prior track record with external lenders; no detail on familiarity with the lender's procurement and safeguard policies.
