## Resumo
Investment loan (direct, not PBL/DPL and not on-lending) for a subnational entity. Finances urban mobility and infrastructure works (road and bridge construction/resurfacing, stormwater drainage and paving in informal neighborhoods, pedestrian/cycling infrastructure) plus urban public-space and sports/leisure equipment, organized under an infrastructure component, an urbanization component, a neighborhood-requalification component, an urban-equipment component and a program-management component. Delivery is direct execution by the municipal government through a program management unit to be created by decree, supported by an outsourced management firm, with several municipal secretariats acting as co-executors.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. O&M is assigned qualitatively to an existing municipal secretariat and said to be absorbed into the municipal budget, but no quantified multi-year O&M table is given; future funding partly rests on an expected (not guaranteed) increase in municipal tax revenue from property valuation.
A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. The CC presents a structured, category-by-category risk discussion (legislative authorization, procurement, expropriation, environmental, social, resettlement) with mitigation measures per category, though without a formal probability/impact matrix. Environmental licensing: most works are expected to be exempt; the remainder require licensing before bidding, under a named environmental secretariat. One component requires partial expropriation, already budgeted and in progress. No resettlement is expected.
Counterpart: to be secured — the authorizing borrowing law was still under discussion in the municipal legislature at CC submission; CAPAG is stated as tier B.
A.4 Implementation (UGP): ADEQUATE but not yet operational — the program unit is to be created by decree only after loan signature, building on a preparation unit; day-to-day management relies on an outsourced management contractor plus several co-executing secretariats under a responsibility matrix.
