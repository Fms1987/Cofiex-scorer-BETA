## Resumo
- Modality: policy-based loan (development policy financing) from IBRD, USD band 200-400M, one-year disbursement, general budget support with no counterpart (PBL exemption under Res. COFIEX 03/2019), fourth version of the letter.
- Second operation in a programmatic series (a first operation preceded it), with a fiscal pillar and an environmental pillar; all funds sit in a single umbrella component while the many policy components carry zero value.
- Fiscal pillar: fiscal-responsibility law, ICMS-type ecological transfer to municipalities, social housing and sanitation investment space, investment prioritisation system. Environmental pillar: environmental career plan and staffing, tougher sanctions, deforestation plan update, forest concessions, carbon-credit asset transfer, bioeconomy strategy.
- Borrower is a state government with a large forest area; CAPAG historically B or better.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG-to-ADEQUATE. Two problems with root causes (current spending growth after the pandemic and a severe drought; persistent deforestation and fires), each mapped to measures and expected results; an annex links strategy, prior actions and indicators. Assumptions are legislative approval and staffing, not stated as testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators verified (approximate split: 4 outcome-type, 3 output/institutional-type: a rating, a transfer-rule share, deforested area, validated rural registries, concession area, a public exam, a temporary selection). All 7 have baseline values; the CC footnote explains that a zero means not yet performed through 2023, so 4 zero baselines counted as defined with that year. Hence baseline_defined 100%, with-year about 57%.
- Benchmarks used: CAPAG, a national satellite deforestation monitoring series, and a rural-registry bulletin; verification sources named for most indicators.
- Internal inconsistencies: CAPAG target equals baseline (maintain rating, methodology-change risk); several of the twelve specific objectives are linked only to CAPAG, an indirect proxy; no intermediate targets in the programme window (NT suggestion).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Fiscal pillar reduces recurrent cost pressure; environmental pillar's main recurring cost (environmental analysts) is not quantified, to be borne by the state treasury and growing revenue (forest concessions, carbon credits, registry fees, fines); targeted fund-raising goal stated in BRL terms in the CC.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: PARTIAL. Legal-act risk handled; contracting risk of the public exam and temporary hiring not listed; environmental/social risks limited to a few items with unclear mitigation (NT view).
- Counterpart: none (PBL). Authorizing state law needed; most reform acts already drafted or delivered (NT counts about two thirds of the normative acts as documented), PPA alignment confirmed.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. All bodies named with detailed responsibilities, but procurement/safeguard know-how and team experience not described; no dedicated UGP (policy loan).
