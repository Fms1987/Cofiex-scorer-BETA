## Resumo
- Modality: policy-based loan (development policy loan) from IDB to a state government for debt restructuring: the whole loan prepays costly short-tenor domestic debt indexed to the CDI, already federally guaranteed, and replaces it with a SOFR-indexed long-tenor facility (22 years total, 2-year grace); the guarantee stays with the Union. No counterpart (restructuring waiver).
- Components: C1 fiscal sustainability and medium-term planning (new fiscal regime, payroll audit, climate governance and budget tagging, tax-reform adaptation) and C2 spending efficiency (public investment management, sustainable procurement, an evidence/intelligence centre, social-organisation contract framework) carry no loan funds and function as non-financial prior-action conditionality; C3 debt restructuring absorbs the entire amount.
- Execution by the finance and planning secretariats with support from the pension institute, environment, administration and health bodies; no PMU. Beneficiaries are framed as the whole state population plus civil servants and a climate-exposed region.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Five diagnosed problems (fiscal pressure and debt profile, spending inefficiency, social/infrastructure gaps, climate vulnerability, tax-reform risk) are mapped to reform actions and then to expected results; the chain from reforms to solvency is plausible but assumptions are not made explicit, and the link between debt swap savings and investment capacity is asserted.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 8 indicators verified; approximately 3 outcome-type (current-savings ratio tied to CAPAG, debt-service saving, tax-adaptation share) and about 5 institutional/process-type (payroll audit coverage, budget-tagging themes, investment-appraisal share, results-agreements coverage, restructured management contracts). Each has formula, source and 2027 target (debt saving targets a 2030 cumulative flow).
- Baselines: all 8 have a stated value (share 1.0); 7 of 8 carry a reference year (0.88). I counted the zero baselines as defined because they refer to reforms not yet in place (new audit, tagging, appraisal methodology, agreements, restructured contracts) and the debt-saving zero is a no-project counterfactual (that one has no year).
- Inconsistencies: the CAPAG-linked indicator is defined as an expenditure-to-revenue ratio (lower is better) but its target is a value above the baseline expressed as a rating threshold, and the target sign is garbled in the source; the strategic-area section cites indicators (CAPAG maintenance, a transparency-of-contracts target) not matching the indicator list. No benchmarks cited.
- Verification: data sources named (fiscal management reports, national fiscal database, budget system, planning secretariat monitoring); the lender verifies policy-matrix compliance before disbursement.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Reforms are anchored in a constitutional amendment and a complementary law already enacted, plus decrees still to come; post-project costs are limited to permanent staff (already in regular budgets) and possible software/data licences, said to be provided in the annual budget law. No quantified OPEX table. Debt-service relief and advantage analysis are delegated to annexes.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Four risks with impact/probability: legal (authorising state law still before the legislature), environmental agenda implementation (low), institutional coordination among six bodies (medium/medium), social/reputational. No works, so no licensing; no macro-financial risk (rates, exchange, revenue volatility) in the matrix.
- Counterpart: none (waiver for debt restructuring/principal recomposition); the non-financial counterpart is the policy matrix. Authorising law is in process, not yet secured.
- A.4 Implementation: no PMU; permanent staff of the finance and planning secretariats, with the lender verifying the matrix; coordination tied to prior state experience with multilateral operations. Institutional coordination is the declared medium-level risk.
