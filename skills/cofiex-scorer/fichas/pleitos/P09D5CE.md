Finances urban-environmental requalification along a degraded urban watercourse: drainage/flood-risk reduction works, green-space and park infrastructure, resettlement and compensation for occupants of the intervention area, community-development actions (incl. a gender/race component), and institutional modernization (territorial-management and environmental-monitoring information systems).
