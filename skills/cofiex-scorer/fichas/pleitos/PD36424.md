## Resumo
Investment loan, direct execution by a state government, with an MDB lender. Finances a rural bioeconomy and sustainable-production programme covering forest/agroforestry/agropecuary value chains, environmental-services incentive mechanisms, and land/environmental regularisation. Three components: (1) productive bioeconomy development and market access (roughly 80% of cost), (2) institutional strengthening of environmental-services incentive systems, (3) project management/UGP and M&E. Includes a gender/youth and traditional-communities inclusion angle.
