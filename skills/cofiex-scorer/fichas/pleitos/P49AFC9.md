## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / intermediated credit line (second phase of an earlier programme with the same lender), borrowed by a multi-state regional development bank under sovereign guarantee with a state counter-guarantee.
- Finances sub-projects of municipalities and private borrowers, selected later: basic sanitation, renewable energy and efficiency, urban and rural infrastructure including port logistics, rural water-resource management, plus a small technical-assistance line for feasibility studies and executive designs.
- Roughly 30% sanitation, 30% renewable energy, about 30% mobility/urban-rural/port infrastructure, under 10% rural water, a few percent technical assistance. Goal includes reaching small municipalities that cannot borrow directly from multilaterals and encouraging PPPs and concessions.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum over the loan, from final borrowers or the bank's own funds (not for public-sector borrowers); 3-year disbursement; a PMU inside the bank.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Sector diagnoses (sanitation coverage gap, energy vulnerability to drought, logistics and rural water stress, small-municipality access to credit) map to five specific objectives and the components; no explicit assumptions, and the NT said expected results were loosely defined.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified 4 indicators, all portfolio-share or financed-volume measures of the bank's lending (output/intermediation level, not service outcomes such as coverage or energy delivered); none for sanitation outcomes, PPP uptake or small-municipality reach beyond lending volume.
- All 4 baselines stated (two percentage shares, two monetary stocks) so baseline_defined_share is 1.0; the two stock indicators define the baseline year (2023) in the formula, the two share indicators give only the 2024-2027 window, so year share is 0.5. Targets are point values, no ranges.
- No verification means or benchmarks beyond the bank's own portfolio data; objectives (PPP stimulation, small-municipality access) lack matching indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. No O&M estimate for financed assets (NT noted this); mitigants cited are multidisciplinary sub-project screening, FX risk passed to final borrowers with natural-hedge or low-repayment-burden criteria, low default history, municipal sub-loans secured by revenue-share pledges.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE for a financial intermediary. Environmental and social risk system with category assignment, dedicated socio-environmental unit, safeguards against involuntary resettlement and environmental-compliance conditions; specific risks mostly not identified because sub-projects are not yet known; no matrix.
- Counterpart: provided at the 20% minimum by final borrowers or the bank; secured through borrower selection, not yet by instrument; a controlling state offers the counter-guarantee as in earlier operations.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG-to-ADEQUATE. Dedicated PMU with defined roles for procurement, finance, safeguards and technical analysis; experience with the first phase fully committed within a year; staff expansion. Lender-portfolio performance in the NT was weak.
