## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (NDB) to a capital municipality with Union guarantee; counterpart at the 20% minimum, direct execution by the municipality through a UGP to be created, coordinated by the planning secretariat with line secretariats as operational executors.
- Finances an urban-infrastructure programme: arterial road widening with bus lanes and cycling infrastructure (largest single item, over 40% of cost), a grade-separated crossing, road rehabilitation, basic infrastructure in participatory-budget streets, and storm-drainage pumping/macro-drainage; plus a public-investment project bank (about 12%, funded entirely from counterpart) and programme management (under 5%).
- Roughly 80%+ of cost is civil works; executive designs for several works did not yet exist at CC stage.
- Long-tenor NDB terms (about 5 years disbursement and grace, 24 years amortisation), floating base rate plus a small fixed margin.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problems (mobility, road safety, flooding, weak investment planning) map to seven specific objectives and a list of expected benefits, but assumptions are implicit and the project bank component has no measurable logic.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 10 indicators verified: approximately 3 outputs (km of cycling, km of bus corridors, area of rehabilitated pavement), 5 outcomes (accident index, complaints, cycling use, firms installed, queue length at one junction) and 2 process/management indicators (on-time procurement plan, disbursement deficit). No indicator for the project bank.
- Baselines: 8 of 10 have a stated baseline value; 2 zero baselines (firms installed, cycling use) are NOT counted as defined because they are relative-change indices starting at zero by construction, not explained as a new asset (baseline_defined_share = 0.8). None states a reference year (baseline_with_year_share = 0.0).
- Weaknesses: place-specific single-intersection and single-neighbourhood indicators, data from a navigation app for queue length, index-type formulas with a base of 100 or 0, no independent verification mechanism and no benchmark (no ISO-type or road-safety standard); the technical note called the set non-SMART.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Maintenance is assigned by asset type to named municipal bodies "with budget or third-party resources", but there is no OPEX estimate, no funding line and no legal backing.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE on licensing and procurement (urban, already-anthropised areas; licensing body identified; exemption hypotheses cited), but contradictory on expropriation/resettlement (stated as not foreseen, yet property acquisition and resettlement appear in the works scope) and on stakeholder acceptance; legal-act risk text focuses on CC approval rather than execution.
- Counterpart: authorising law for the credit still being drafted; PPA cited but alignment thin; the counterpart is concentrated in the project bank (about 60%), part of the works and half of programme management, and an in-kind land contribution is mentioned without value or destination. Status: "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP to be created by administrative act with four coordinations and consultant support, prior multilateral-loan experience, but a bill for temporary posts was still pending and the disbursement period looks ambitious for works still lacking executive designs.
