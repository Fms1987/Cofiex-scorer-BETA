## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IBRD to a state government, with state counterpart at the 20% minimum; execution horizon of roughly nine years.
- Finances a pilot of performance-based road maintenance and rehabilitation delivered through PPP-style long-term contracts (12+ years) on regional road networks in one river basin; a minority of the cost covers road-safety works, structural/bridge rehabilitation, climate-resilient drainage and a lane-widening element.
- Four components: (C1) PPP-based maintenance and construction, roughly two-thirds of cost, including PPP structuring studies and a public contribution to the concession counter-payment; (C2) institutional strengthening (climate studies, training of civil servants and of women and Black people, weigh-in-motion equipment); (C3) road-safety interventions near schools and health facilities plus iRAP star-rating; (C4) project management and M&E.
- Delivery: direct execution by the state roads department through a UGP, supported by finance, legal and planning bodies; private partner selected through a PPP tender.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Seven problems are mapped one-to-one to solutions and then to thirteen expected results (reactive maintenance, crash deaths, climate damage, inclusion, overload enforcement, capacity, drainage), but assumptions are not stated as testable and the causal chain from PPP contracts to fewer deaths is asserted.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 18 indicators verified; approximately 4 are outcome-type (roughness index share, road deaths, contract coverage, cost saving) and about 14 are outputs or counts (people trained, critical points treated, institutions treated, bioengineering works, signage).
- Baselines: 3 of 18 hold a non-zero stated value (roughness share, deaths, share of network under long-term contracts); only 2 of those state a reference period; the contract-coverage baseline has no year. About 15 baselines are 0 or 0% without any CC explanation (new outputs, cumulative counts), counted as undefined under the v3 rule.
- Verification: administrative records of the roads department, crash and death-certificate data, periodic (annual or biennial) measurement; no independent verification or impact evaluation named beyond an M&E component.
- Benchmarks: international roughness index and iRAP star ratings; cost-saving claim benchmarked against an analogous IBRD programme in another state, with a deliberately more conservative assumption.
- Inconsistencies: "people benefited" target equals the whole population of the two regions (not a measured effect); one tourism-signage indicator is weakly tied to stated objectives; a climate-plan coverage target is a small share of the state network; the road-death baseline and target are small absolute numbers sensitive to noise.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: PARTIAL. Argument rests on lifecycle-cost savings of performance-based contracts versus input-based contracts (HDM-4 economic analysis cited with a positive IRR and NPV, large claimed savings over 20 years, a second cycle financed by the state's own funds); no explicit post-project O&M budget table, no funding line or legal act for the post-loan period; toll revenue is mentioned only as a possible future option.
- A.3 Risk assessment: ADEQUATE. Nine risks with impact/probability and mitigation (legal acts, payment, PPP tender litigation, bidder under-capitalisation, failed tender, expropriation, environmental, social, occupied land); sector risks covered; environmental licensing for functional recovery said to be exempt, with ESF instruments to be prepared; PPP-specific bid and performance guarantees described. Weak point: no assessment of PPP contingent fiscal liabilities.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, to be secured; the CC cites alignment with the state multi-year plan and programme lines but does not show a budget line, LOA allocation or enabling law (the state legislative authorisation for the loan is still pending). State CAPAG stated as C.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP within the roads department with procurement, financial, safeguards and engineering staff; RACI matrix annexed; prior experience with the lender cited; safeguards documents and procurement strategy still to be prepared.
