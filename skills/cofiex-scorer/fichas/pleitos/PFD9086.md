## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (IBRD) to a capital municipality, direct execution by the municipal education secretariat with a works secretariat as partner; no on-lending or transfers between bodies.
- Finances early-childhood education expansion: new and refurbished full-time day-care/pre-school buildings with surrounding drainage and paving, river-based service centres for riverine children (special-needs and early-childhood), a demand/supply vacancy monitoring system.
- Component 2 covers minimum quality standards, a quality-assessment system, teacher and school-leader training, pedagogical materials; Component 3 is a new UGP. Roughly 90% of cost sits in the infrastructure/access component.
- Counterpart at about 20% of total cost; approximately five-year execution.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (low day-care and pre-school coverage versus national averages, no demand-mapping or quality system) links to access, quality and management components and to a list of expected results; assumptions are not explicit or testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE/WEAK. 10 indicators verified, approximately 5 outcome-type (coverage of day-care, pre-school, high-vulnerability children, quality standards, women labour insertion) and approximately 5 output-type (quality-assessment rollout, training, schools built/refurbished, river centres, accessible materials). Each has formula, data source (municipal secretariat, school census) and target.
- Baselines: 6 of 10 defined (0.6); counted zero baselines for the new-asset indicators (schools built/refurbished, river centres) and the not-yet-existing quality-assessment system, as the CC explains these are new. The training indicator has an unexplained zero, counted as undefined. Quality-standards baseline "to be computed in 2025", and two indicators (accessible materials, women labour insertion) have placeholder targets and baseline "to be defined in preparation" = undefined. Reference year stated for 4 of 10 (0.4): the three coverage indicators and the assessment indicator.
- Benchmarks: PPA and national education plan alignment cited for coverage indicators; national early-childhood quality parameters mentioned. No independent verification mechanism beyond municipal data; no impact evaluation named.
- Inconsistencies: expected-results list includes learning proficiency, repetition and dropout outcomes that have no indicator; deferred criteria for selecting school sites.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Maintenance via existing network-wide contracts and a planned school-council decentralised-funds scheme; services outsourced or paid from enrolment-linked earmarked education revenue. Routine per-school maintenance cost is quantified (per unit per year); extraordinary repairs only as a broad range; no 5-year OPEX table or consolidated incremental cost for the new facilities.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Six risks with impact/probability and mitigation: procurement, hiring, land acquisition/expropriation, environmental impacts, resettlement, pedagogical implementation. Licensing and land risk acknowledged; mitigation generic (18-month priority plan, competitive salaries, World Bank social/environmental framework).
- Counterpart: to be secured; CC states that a state-level authorising law and a regulating decree will be processed only after approval (internally odd for a municipal borrower); no LOA/PPA line cited for counterpart.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE/WEAK. UGP to be created by legal act inside the education secretariat with consultants plus civil servants, sector support nuclei and a bidding committee; organisation chart and staffing deferred to preparation.
