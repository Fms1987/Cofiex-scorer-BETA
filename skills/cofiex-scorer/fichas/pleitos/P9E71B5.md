## Resumo
Investment loan to a state government, direct (non-intermediated) delivery through the state's own project-management unit. Finances road rehabilitation/maintenance under long-term performance-based contracts plus road-safety works, alongside a family-farming support package (extension services, productive inputs, cooperative/organization strengthening) and a smaller rural-tourism promotion line; a fourth, much smaller line funds rural environmental-governance strengthening and the implementing unit's own operating costs. The road-maintenance line absorbs roughly 70% of total cost, well above the rural-development and tourism lines combined.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem framing (road-network maintenance backlog, family-farming vulnerability, rural-tourism potential) is coherent and well contextualized, but the NT repeatedly (across all three versions reviewed) flags that the roads/logistics strand and the rural-development/tourism strand are weakly tied together, and that two environmental-governance sub-components lack a clearly established contribution to the project's own logic chain. A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. VERIFIED n_indicators=22; baseline_defined_share=0.05 (only the pre-existing "network in good condition" indicator carries a real baseline value; the other 21 indicators show an unexplained "0.00" baseline with no CC narrative justifying it as a genuine new-asset/counterfactual zero, so they are counted as undefined); baseline_with_year_share=0.0 (no indicator states a reference year for its baseline). Outcome-vs-output split is approximate and output-heavy: most indicators count beneficiaries reached, cooperatives formed, buildings delivered, or km-equivalent points fixed, rather than measuring an outcome; only productivity/income-increase and accident-reduction indicators read as outcome-level. A verification annex (methodology/progression spreadsheet) was supplied but judged insufficient by the NT because it lacks a calculation memory or baseline reference for several indicators, and in the latest version the annex was not updated after the loan amount was reduced, creating an internal inconsistency between indicator targets and the revised financial envelope. Benchmarks used are limited to a national road-condition survey and a cross-state comparison for expected maintenance-cost savings; no wide-range indicators were identified.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL/WEAK. OPEX is quantified only for the road-maintenance line, matched against a dedicated state transport fund's recent collection levels; across all three NT reviews the same gap is flagged for the other three components, where no O&M cost estimate or funding source is given beyond a generic reference to "good maintenance practice." A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE in the final version, WEAK in the first two. Early versions concentrate risk identification on the roads component and leave legal, environmental and social risks for the other components thin; the final version substantially deepens environmental/social risk treatment (soil and water contamination from paving inputs, vegetation suppression, interactions with traditional/indigenous communities, gender-based-violence mitigation tied to civil-works labor influx) and adds a fuller legal-risk narrative. Environmental licensing risk is rated low by the proponent, resting on an existing state licensing law and a named environmental agency; land acquisition/resettlement risk is rated low (works mostly within existing road right-of-way) but flagged as needing case-by-case reassessment during engineering design. Counterpart (20% of total cost): SECURED — a state legislative act already authorizes the borrowing above the pleaded amount, and the counterpart is budgeted pari passu from treasury resources. A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE — the executing unit is already operating, with over a decade of experience running loans from the same lender and declared familiarity with its procurement rules, though a SIGS portfolio check found no qualifying external-credit operations by the proponent in the lookback window, so the "proponent experience" sub-criterion itself scored zero (quality sub-criterion scored as not applicable).
