## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a small metropolitan-area municipality (first external credit operation for the borrower), with federal guarantee; direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat with a programme management unit (UGP).
- Multi-sector urban programme: components cover urban mobility/road and sidewalk requalification, stormwater drainage and slope stabilisation, social infrastructure (squares, sports facilities, schools, health units), environmental recovery, tourism/heritage, and institutional strengthening with digital services.
- Cost mix is roughly 40% mobility, 25-30% social infrastructure/environment, about 15% drainage/sanitation, about 10% programme management, small shares for digital/institutional; counterpart at the 20% minimum of total cost.
- Very broad scope relative to the ticket; drainage and slope-risk works respond to recurrent flooding and landslides.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Problems are well diagnosed (flooding, landslides, mobility, weak municipal management) and each objective maps to an indicator, but there is no explicit causal chain or testable assumptions; several benefits are asserted rather than argued.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. 8 indicators, roughly 6 outcome-type and 2 output-type (approximate judgement); each is tied to a strategic area, with data sources named, but no formal verification arrangement (independent verifier/audit calendar) is described.
- Baselines: 6 of 8 stated as values (share 0.75), all with reference year 2026. Two zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains them (no tourism activity yet; no online services yet). Travel-time and degraded-area-recovery indicators have no baseline value ("to be surveyed").
- Internal inconsistencies: flooding-spot reduction is 80% in the results section, 50% in the objectives and 60% in the indicator; risk-area stabilisation is 100% in one place and 40% in another; the baseline-year (2026) post-dates the submission.
- Weak spots: tourism visitors target is a percentage over a zero baseline (mathematically undefined); the public-space indicator is an area measure with unspecified units; transport-use indicator depends on an integration with the metropolitan system not yet in place.
- No benchmarks (IDEB, SNIS, iRAP, ISO) are used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. The CC quantifies only the annual cost of running the programme/management structure (in BRL, by category), not O&M of the built assets (drainage, slopes, roads, schools, squares, digital platform). Funding sources are generic (PPA/LDO/LOA linkage, possible municipal fund fed by land-use and PPP revenues, federal transfers) with no committed legal instrument.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: four risks (legal, procurement, environmental, social), all rated low probability; the environmental risk lists licensing delay and construction impacts with mitigation; licensing is by the state environmental agency because the municipality has no licensing capacity. Landslide/resettlement works have no dedicated resettlement-risk treatment.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, to be secured; municipal authorising law is only sent to the council after COFIEX recommendation, and inclusion in the LDO is still a risk item.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP with five management units plus supervision consultancy; the borrower admits it has no prior experience with an MDB and relies on a team still being assembled.
- Financing section carries boilerplate that mentions another lender's terms (internal inconsistency).
