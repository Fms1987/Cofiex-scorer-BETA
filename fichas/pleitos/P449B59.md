## Resumo
Investment operation for a state government borrower, direct implementation through the state infrastructure secretariat. Dominant component is a proactive road-maintenance policy covering a sizeable share of the paved state network, bundling conservation, rehabilitation and maintenance under single long-term contracts. Secondary components: climate-resilience interventions on the road network (drainage, slope stabilisation, critical-point elimination), road-safety measures and enforcement equipment, rural-tourism incentives (signage, promotion), metropolitan urban-mobility support (an integration terminal and low/zero-emission fleet), institutional strengthening (asset registry, road-management systems, staff training) and program administration. Counterpart at the 20% minimum.
