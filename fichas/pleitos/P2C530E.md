## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan with federal guarantee, executed by a federal development bank as borrower and on-lender (IDB source, no counterpart; counter-guarantee offered in public securities above the debt value).
- Finances a credit line for subnational governments (state and municipal) to contract innovative public-procurement arrangements with startups: one sub-component for pilot/testing contracts, one for supply contracts of validated solutions, split roughly half and half.
- A second component (capacity-building cycles for municipalities) has zero budget in this operation; it is funded by separate grant/technical-cooperation resources of other institutions, and is meant to feed the credit pipeline.
- Delivery is intermediated: the bank on-lends to sub-borrowers, which run the contracts; the bank's existing international-programmes unit acts as UGP at no cost to the loan.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Four objectives map to four expected results and the chain capacity-building to credit to contracting is stated, but assumptions (municipal demand, legal comfort with the procurement modality) appear only as risks, not as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 6 indicators, approximately 1 outcome-type (subnational share of national R&D spending) and the rest outputs (contracts signed, municipalities contracting, supply contracts, value purchased from regional startups, municipalities trained); output-heavy.
- Baselines: 5 of 6 counted as defined (count baselines with year, one share baseline with year, and one zero baseline explicitly justified because the credit line is new). The capacity-building indicator has an unexplained zero baseline, counted as undefined. All stated baselines carry a reference year.
- Verification mechanism present (external procurement observatory, national science ministry statistics, bank monitoring systems); the R&D share indicator depends on irregular data releases, so no intermediate targets.
- Internal inconsistency: the R&D-share indicator shows a target value below its baseline, apparently an increment rather than a level; the purchase-value indicator has no intermediate targets.
- Benchmarks: none beyond a startup-mapping report used to size the regional share.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for a credit line. No OPEX table; the bank states no maintenance cost structure applies, and requires each sub-borrower to show formal budget provision for operating what it acquires, checked at credit approval. Loan term is long, with a grace period of 90 months.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Five-risk matrix with impact/probability and mitigation (low municipal demand, innovation failure, difficulty managing the contracts, bank staff learning curve, shortfall of capacity-building funds). No licensing risk (not applicable).
- Counterpart: none (0%); sub-borrower credit analysis includes fiscal checks (PVL) and capacity assessment.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP is an existing in-house unit staffed by the bank with no loan funds needed; an innovation unit of the bank supports; RACI matrix annexed. Partner institutions are outside the loan and their commitments are grant-based, not contractually tied to the loan.
