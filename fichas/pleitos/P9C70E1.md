## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (IBRD, sovereign guarantee, ticket band above 400M USD) to a federal ministry acting as borrower and executor; 5-year disbursement, 20-year total term, floating SOFR plus about 1.5%, grace up to about 8 years; no counterpart (100% external).
- What is financed: a capital injection into three regional development funds, which then lend to private borrowers for a broad menu of sectors (sustainable logistics, renewables, bioeconomy and forest concessions, sanitation, health/education PPPs, innovation); split across the three fund products roughly 40/30/30.
- Delivery model: intermediated/on-lending; regional development agencies screen eligibility, accredited operator banks appraise, lend and bear credit risk, private borrowers execute and operate. Single component, three products (one per fund); a project-management unit is to be created inside the executor. Companion to an earlier operation with another development bank for the same funds.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (low end). Logic is more-credit-to-the-funds, then more private investment, jobs and emissions cuts; assumptions (absorption capacity, additionality, fund demand about six times supply) are asserted rather than tested. The diagnosis centres on restoration infrastructure in deforested areas, whereas the solution is a multi-sector credit envelope, a mismatch in focus.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 6 indicators verified; approximate split 3 programme-level outcome/impact (emissions reduction, forest area under concession, minimum jobs created) and 3 financing outputs (fund resources invested, growth in projects financed, innovation projects supported). The two environmental indicators are not directly attributable to the fund capitalisation (NT said so).
- Baselines: only 4 of 6 counted as defined (baseline_defined_share 0.67): the numeric fund-resources baseline plus three zero baselines accepted because they are with/without-project counterfactual deltas; the emissions and forest-area zero baselines were NOT counted (changes from a base year whose level is never stated). Only 2 baselines carry a reference year/period (baseline_with_year_share 0.33). The NT, more strictly, found a numeric baseline for just one indicator.
- Verification mechanism absent (no data source, evaluation or reporting arrangement); formulas are given but several are vaguely worded or mix units; targets for the programme-level indicators are not tied to the pipeline, which is not yet identified.
- Internal inconsistencies: objectives list roughly ten sector goals but indicators cover only a few; funds-invested indicator mixes budget-bill figures with the annual loan inflow.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for the executor, weak for the assets. O&M of financed assets rests entirely with private borrowers (assessed by operator banks case by case); the funds' administrative structure is said to absorb the extra volume with no new hiring or systems, funded by reflows (principal and interest) and fixed operator-bank remuneration. No OPEX figures, which is natural for a fund.
- Counterpart: none required; budget authorisation is "to be secured" (per the budget-office NT the operation is not in the next annual budget bill and will enter a later one or a specific law; expenses are financial, so they do not hit primary-result or spending-cap rules).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Two risks only (economic slowdown reducing credit demand, borrower default) with mitigations (excess demand, collateral required by operator banks, flexibility to reallocate among lines). Environmental, resettlement and licensing are delegated entirely to borrowers, regional agencies and operator banks, applying World Bank guidelines; no screening of excluded activities or ESMS detail at CC stage.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Mature on-lending channel operating for about two decades, RACI matrix and funds flow annexed; PMU still to be created by ministerial act; the NT noted the executor had no externally financed project under execution in the system (experience criterion scored half), though it cites past operations with the same lender.
