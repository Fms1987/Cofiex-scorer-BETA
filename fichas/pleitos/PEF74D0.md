## Resumo
- Investment loan (IBRD) with federal guarantee to a state government, ticket band 50-100M USD, counterpart at the 20% minimum (state treasury, current-expenditure nature); 5-year disbursement, front-loaded (about 30% in year 1).
- Multi-theme digital package, four components: a fibre "digital belt" (backbone for the interior, data-centre modernisation, digital monitoring tools; roughly 35% of cost), digital government (service portal, interoperability, data observatory, equipment; roughly 20%), digital skills for civil servants and citizens with a gender focus (roughly 10%), and a smart-energy component (building efficiency retrofits and distributed solar generation; roughly 35%).
- Delivery model direct: a recently created state development agency executes with a UGP whose structure is still to be defined; a state IT company gives technical advice.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Three linked problems (poverty/exclusion, precarious connectivity, thin digital services) mapped to four components, with a proof-of-concept argument from a neighbouring state's earlier digital-belt programme; causal assumptions (private uptake of the backbone, usage of services, savings) are not made explicit or testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Ten indicators (verified; approximately 6 output/capacity and 4 outcome/performance type, e.g. service-catalogue size, data-centre downtime, PUE, energy intensity of buildings). Formulas given for all; the CC says availability is monthly and targets refer to project end, but there is no timetable or verification plan, as the NT itself notes.
- Baselines: 6 of 10 defined with non-zero values (share 0.6); four zero baselines (two connectivity-coverage counts, digital-transformation projects, installed distributed generation) not explained in the CC and counted as undefined; the building-efficiency baseline is a normalised 100%. The CC states that baselines use latest 2022/2023 data, so the year share is also 0.6 (applied to the six defined baselines).
- Internal inconsistencies: the semi-arid coverage target differs between the indicator table and the component text; the gender indicator is defined as share of trained women servants, while the component text frames it as share of IT posts held by women; data-centre PUE baseline is given as a 1-2 range plus a point value.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. No post-project O&M, licences, hosting or replacement costs are quantified; the CC argues that digital-service savings (literature-based) would fund the whole programme, with no state budget commitment or legal backing for recurring costs.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK to ADEQUATE. No matrix; legal risks (state authorising law, Senate resolution) and FX risk named; no cybersecurity/data-law infrastructure risks beyond a security product; expropriation and environmental risk dismissed because cabling follows existing roads ("dig once"), though it is unclear whether those roads already exist; no licensing status.
- Counterpart: to be secured. State multi-year plan for the next cycle was still under deliberation, authorising law not yet enacted; the CC invokes a comfortable current-expenditure ratio and borrowing space.
- A.4 Implementation: WEAK. Executor is a newly created development agency with no track record, UGP composition undefined, no mention of lender procurement and safeguard policies; the state's past external-credit portfolio performance was rated poor by the NT.
