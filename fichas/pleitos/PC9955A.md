## Resumo
Investment loan to a state government (direct execution), co-financed by two external lenders under a shared cofinancing framework, financing international connectivity infrastructure (a new subsea-cable link and its terrestrial receiving/integration facility), modernization and expansion of the state's domestic data network and data-center, interconnection support for small/medium regional broadband providers, connectivity for public facilities and schools, satellite-based community internet access for remote and traditional communities, and a digital-skills training program prioritizing low-income/vulnerable groups and women. Delivery is direct, coordinated by a state planning secretariat and implemented through the state's ICT public enterprise. Entity type: state government (subnational).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG (full marks at this NT sub-criterion). Post-completion sustainability is funded through sale of excess capacity on the new connectivity infrastructure, cost savings versus third-party capacity contracting, and projected state tax-revenue growth from the resulting economic activity; an NPV/IRR analysis and a weighted-average-cost-of-capital simulation are presented for a defined post-construction operating horizon (band-range figures, not exact amounts).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG (full marks). A risk matrix with a dedicated mitigation/response plan covers legal/institutional risk (including coordination risk with a federal sibling fiber-infrastructure program), procurement/contracting risk, environmental and social risk (environmental-licensing delay, occupational/community health and safety, information security, construction nuisances); resettlement/expropriation risk is explicitly stated as not applicable (no land acquisition required).
- Counterpart: secured — grounded in a specific state budget-related law already enacted, not merely a pending LOA inclusion.
- A.4 Implementation (UGP quality): STRONG (full marks). Institutional coordination sits with a state planning secretariat; implementation runs through the state's ICT public enterprise, with a RACI institutional-responsibility matrix, a named initial team with stated experience, and a cofinancing framework between the two lenders that consolidates fiduciary/compliance monitoring through a single lead bank.
