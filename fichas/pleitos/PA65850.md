## Resumo
- Modality: debt restructuring through an IBRD Development Policy Loan (PBL) denominated in Japanese yen, 100% federal guarantee (substituting guarantees on loans already guaranteed), no counterpart (restructuring exemption); disbursement tied to a policy matrix annexed to the proposal.
- Finances early repayment of principal only on a selected set of domestic bank loans (CDI- or SOFR-indexed) of a state government; interest, fees and prepayment penalties are not paid from proceeds.
- Single component; fiscal narrative framed with a "transversal" promise that freed fiscal space will go to urban mobility, road climate-resilience and renewable energy policies, but no investment or indicators for them.
- Delivery: state finance secretariat as executor, planning secretariat as coordinator, sector secretariats supplying policy-matrix indicators.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear fiscal chain (replace costly short debt with cheaper long yen debt, lower debt service, currency diversification, freed space); the link from freed space to mobility and energy outcomes is asserted, not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 3 indicators (verified): NPV saving, number of debt contracts managed (an administrative output), and share of external debt exposed to the US dollar. About 2 outcome-type, 1 output-type. All three have base year and target year.
- Baselines: all 3 stated with value and year (share 1.0, with-year 1.0); the zero baseline of the NPV indicator is counted as defined because it is a counterfactual (no saving before the operation).
- Verification mechanism not stated; no external benchmarks. Targets are point values, but the contract-count indicator is of low strategic value. The policy matrix (annex) sets disbursement conditions, which supports results monitoring, but the matrix content was not in the readable text.
- Mismatch: sector-themed objectives (mobility, energy) lack any indicator.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no O&M; the case rests on a positive NPV across all yen, euro and dollar scenarios, an estimated prepayment penalty that is small relative to the NPV gain, and debt service already present in LOA, LDO and PPA (so no PPA inclusion needed).
- A.3 Risks: 4 risks with impact/probability and mitigation (contracting, legal acts for policy-matrix measures and the authorising law, creditor resistance to prepayment rated high probability, currency); currency risk mitigation argues yen stability and diversification away from dollar exposure, with no hedging analysis. No licensing risk.
- Counterpart: waived under the restructuring exemption (Art. 3, II, Res. COFIEX 03/2019 cited); authorising law still pending, with the state expecting quick approval.
- A.4 Implementation: finance secretariat with experienced public-debt staff and prior IBRD operations; responsibility matrix annexed; roles of sector secretariats defined only as providers of matrix indicators.
