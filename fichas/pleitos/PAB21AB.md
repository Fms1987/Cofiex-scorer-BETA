## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan with federal guarantee from the NDB, borrower is a listed mixed-capital state energy company; 4-year disbursement, 5-year grace, 15-year amortisation, floating-rate EURIBOR-based cost.
- Finances two lines: replacement of legacy sodium/mercury street luminaires with LED (about 15% of cost) and a utility-scale solar PV plant (about 85% of cost), plus small project-management and lender-fee components.
- Delivery: turnkey contracting by tender under the state-enterprise procurement law; execution unit within the company; operation outsourced.
- Preparation with the lender was concluded earlier; counterpart at about one sixth of cost.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-STRONG. Clear problem-solution-results chain for two lines (inefficient, hazardous legacy street lighting; hydrological concentration of the company's generation), with expected benefits listed; assumptions are not made explicit, and several results (crime, accidents, employment) are asserted without indicators.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 9 indicators verified, approximately 3 output-type (LED coverage share, installed renewable capacity) and 6 outcome-type (energy consumption, shares of consumption covered, avoided carbon, jobs). Each has a baseline year, target year and a calculation formula; progressive targets are documented in an annexed calculation memo. No independent verification mechanism or data source beyond the company's own records.
- Baselines: 4 of 9 have a stated non-zero value, all with a reference year (LED share, energy consumption, installed capacity, share of solar in consumption). The 5 indicators with zero baseline (supply ratios of the plant, avoided carbon, jobs) were not counted since the indicator table does not explain the zero (they are for a new plant). Share with year: 0.44.
- Weak points: three overlapping supply-coverage ratios; the jobs target is derived from an industry multiplier, not measured; carbon indicator mixes lifetime avoided emissions with an annual figure; an inconsistency in plant size between the CC and later NT figures.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M quantified for both the LED stock and the solar plant (relative to the old lamps, running cost about halved); LED warranty of 10 years; maintenance outsourced via tenders under the state-enterprise procurement law; 30-year lighting concession gives legal backing. Funding source is energy savings plus plant revenue; a cash-flow model and business plan are annexed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-STRONG. Risks listed by category (legal, procurement, land, environmental, social) with mitigations, plus an annexed risk matrix. Expropriation and resettlement avoided (leased or ceded public land, uninhabited). Environmental licensing declared unnecessary; hazardous-lamp disposal and recycling handled by company procedures. Land cession and the authorising law for counter-guarantees are still pending (to be secured).
- Counterpart: from the company's own resources (non-dependent state company, outside the minimum-counterpart rule), about one sixth of cost; not tied to LOA/PPA.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Project execution unit under the planning and risk directorate, with a RACI matrix annexed; preparation done jointly with the lender over about two years, contract drafts ready; team profile described; knowledge of lender procurement/ESF policies stated. No prior COFIEX-registered external credit in the last ten years, although preparation of this very project was approved earlier.
