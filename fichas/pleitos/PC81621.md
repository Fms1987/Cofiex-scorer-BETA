## Resumo
- Modality: federal-level external credit (Union as borrower, USD band >400M, NDB) whose proceeds capitalise three regional development funds, which then on-lend reimbursable credit to private firms through federal financial institutions acting as operating agents.
- Split across the three funds follows the legal sharing rule; three components, one product each; disbursement over roughly four to five years with a front-loaded first tranche.
- Eligible uses are open-ended: ports and rail logistics, transmission and renewable energy, bioeconomy and circular economy, irrigation, agro-industry. No pipeline of subprojects presented.
- No counterpart at the loan level (100% external); final borrowers post counterpart and collateral to the operating banks, who bear 100% of credit risk; funding costs are passed through to final borrowers.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Causal chain (infrastructure gap -> more fund capital -> more firm financing and private leverage, funds covering a minority-to-majority share of each investment) is stated, but without testable assumptions or a pipeline; the NT called the thematic context thin for the complexity.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 5 indicators verified, all outcome-type in intent (fleet use, energy cost, irrigated area, agribusiness exports, emissions). All 5 have a stated baseline value but none states a reference year, hence baseline_defined 100% and with-year 0%.
- Indicators are not attributable to the financing (national or regional aggregates), unit labels are inconsistent with the absolute-looking values, and formulas mix percentages with absolute baselines; the NT gave zero out of the indicator-coherence criterion.
- Verification mechanism absent: no source or periodicity for most indicators; the targets given are year-fixed to the end of the period, with no intermediate values.
- Alignment of indicators to the funded components is loose (e.g. an emissions indicator and fleet indicator with no matching component).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. No incremental O&M for the ministry or funds; O&M of financed works falls on borrowing firms; administrators' remuneration is a fixed percentage already in force. Debt service by the Union is meant to be covered by passed-through charges (financial cost, term and rate stated; floating SOFR-based rate, long term, semiannual amortisation).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: PARTIAL. Narrative on macro slowdown, exchange-rate depreciation and borrower default, with reallocation between funds/lines and bank-required collateral as mitigants; no matrix. Licensing, expropriation and resettlement are delegated to regional agencies and operating banks and deferred to subproject stage.
- Counterpart: not applicable at the loan level; borrower-level counterpart required by operating banks. Programme line in the PPA and alignment with the national regional development policy and regional plans.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE on paper. Governance is inherited from the funds' existing legal framework (regional development agencies approve projects, councils deliberate, banks operate); the borrowing ministry had no prior experience with international lenders in the ten-year window per the NT.
