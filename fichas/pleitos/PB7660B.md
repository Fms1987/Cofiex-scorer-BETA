## Resumo
- On-lending credit line: a federally owned regional development bank borrows from a BRICS-led lender under a federal guarantee, with a counter-guarantee in federal securities, and on-lends to public and private infrastructure borrowers in its area of operation. No counterpart (per the COFIEX manual).
- Single component and subcomponent, funded as works across four product lines: transport logistics (roughly 40% of the line), renewable power generation and transmission (about a third), basic sanitation (about a fifth) and connectivity (a small remainder). Multi-year disbursement window, back-loaded to the first years.
- Delivery is intermediated: the bank applies its own credit pipeline, licensing and ESG rules; projects are not identified at CC stage. Currency risk is hedged by swap and the hedge cost is passed through to final borrowers.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: WEAK. Strong sector diagnosis (logistics imbalance, sanitation gaps, renewable potential, connectivity), but no causal chain from credit to results and no assumptions on demand, pipeline or additionality; the six expected results are stated as coverage/volume advances.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Six indicators (verified), approximately four outcome-type (power share, jobs, sanitation and water coverage, connectivity) and two capacity/output-type; sources are named for most (national sanitation information system, regulator statistics, port statistics, national statistics office), but there is no verification schedule or attribution method beyond an input-output job estimate.
- Baselines: all six state a baseline value (share 1.0); five give a reference year (2021 to 2023), the connectivity one gives none (share 0.83). No zero baselines.
- Inconsistencies: several indicators are labelled percentage but the baseline cell holds an absolute quantity (installed capacity, jobs, population served, port tonnage) against a tiny percent target, so units and targets do not reconcile; the connectivity indicator measures regional coverage that the programme alone cannot move.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no OPEX table; the bank will require final borrowers to prove formal allocation of funds for operation and maintenance as a credit-approval condition. Strong low-default record asserted, loan terms of 20 years including 5 grace, floating benchmark plus a spread, hedge cost not included.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: textual rather than a matrix: legal framework, credit risk (standard infrastructure credit pipeline), currency risk (swap hedge), environmental licensing required of every project under national law and the lender's use of national systems; involuntary resettlement judged unlikely but covered by law; demand risk declared absent.
- Counterpart: none required. Project pipeline: only a sector-level potential-demand table for public sponsors, no named subprojects or eligibility criteria.
- A.4 Implementation: bank headquarters management team plus state superintendencies; experience as borrower from several multilaterals cited; responsibility matrix is in an annex.
