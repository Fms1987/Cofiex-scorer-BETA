## Resumo
- Modality: programmatic policy-based loan (DPF/PBL), single disbursement within about one year, federal guarantee, no counterpart. The source text names IBRD as creditor and a ticket above the header band; header lender/band look inconsistent with the source.
- Finances a policy matrix rather than assets: no pre-identified use of proceeds; budget support to the federal government.
- Three components of reforms: (1) tax-system and fiscal-framework reforms (consumption tax, fiscal rule, taxation of investment funds and offshore income); (2) financing of the ecological transformation (green intergovernmental transfer, sovereign sustainable bond framework, credit-bureau rules, deforestation programmes, a private-capital/FX-hedge programme); (3) social inclusion (social registry quality, school feeding). About a dozen policy products plus one general "policy matrix" component carrying all funds.
- Delivery: direct, executed by the federal finance ministry via coordination and execution cores; complementary to a sibling IDB policy loan sharing several products.
