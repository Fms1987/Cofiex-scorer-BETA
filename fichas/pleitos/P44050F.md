## Resumo
Direct investment operation (external credit), subnational municipal borrower, multilateral lender (IDB), ticket band <50M USD, counterpart at the 20% minimum backed by constitutionally-earmarked municipal tax revenue. Finances a multi-theme urban-regeneration program upgrading secondary neighborhood commercial/service hubs outside the primary downtown core: active-mobility and cycling infrastructure, public-building rooftop solar generation, selective-waste-collection infrastructure, rainwater-garden/green-infiltration drainage works, green public-space requalification, mixed-use social-housing units, public cultural/recreational facilities, and small access improvements near existing collective-transport stations. Delivery is direct to the municipal government, executed by the municipal works/infrastructure secretariat with several specialized municipal bodies in support; a program management unit and steering committee are to coordinate execution. Entity type: capital/metropolitan-pole municipality.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: funding sources are identified (municipal budget, a dedicated municipal urban-development fund fed by urbanistic/building-right charges, and a public/civil-society partnership channel for public-space upkeep); an annual per-area maintenance unit cost is stated in the CC, but the evaluating opinion's own management-section review notes that no specific post-project maintenance cost figures are highlighted there, and recurring-revenue adequacy is not stress-tested.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: a matrix covers legal/institutional, procurement, expropriation, environmental and social dimensions. Legal/institutional risk flags possible overlap of responsibilities with other public and private entities near existing collective-transport stations, mitigated via formal inter-institutional agreements. No resettlement is required (expropriation areas reported to have no occupied residential units). Licensing: the evaluating opinion states these interventions are not subject to urban-impact or environmental licensing under municipal legislation — an exemption claim rather than a licensing process completed.
- Counterpart: secured via constitutionally-earmarked municipal tax revenue (per the applicable COFIEX counterpart resolution), not "to be secured."
- A.4 Implementation: a program management unit and a steering committee (combining staff from the lead secretariat and supporting urban-planning body) are planned to coordinate execution; the evaluating opinion flags that no civil-service hiring is planned to staff the program team, a continuity risk. The borrower's disbursement-performance portfolio indicator is rated "regular" (50-70% range) in both evaluated versions.
