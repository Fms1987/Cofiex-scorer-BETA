## Resumo
- Investment loan from IDB to a state government for a broad digital-government programme, counterpart at the 20% minimum, ticket band <50M USD, with a Union guarantee; executed directly by a central administration secretariat through a new UGP, with other secretariats as executing units under implementation agreements.
- Three substantive components plus programme management: (1) public-management transformation (data governance and a data lake, digital-government policy and governance, innovation lab, AI-enabled corporate systems, state digital cartography, which is the largest single product); (2) digital public services (citizen journey, service digitisation, payment gateway, biometric identification, public-security service bus, smart-city programme modelling); (3) ICT infrastructure and connectivity (backbone upgrade, free public Wi-Fi, data-centre power, disaster recovery, cybersecurity and staff training).
- Spend mix is heavily consulting plus goods; small works share. Loosely scoped, many products (about two dozen) for a modest ticket.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. A long diagnosis lists many numbered problems, each mapped to a component and product, with stated expected results, but there are no explicit assumptions or causal chain beyond that mapping.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK to ADEQUATE. 10 indicators verified; roughly 3 are outcome-type (service uptake, cost reduction, data-centre availability) and the rest are output or capacity measures (approximate judgement). Each has a formula, unit, baseline and target, but no reference years, no target dates within the programme horizon, and no verification source or mechanism.
- Baselines: 6 of 10 carry a non-zero value. 4 are zero and the CC explains none of them (security-service digitisation, cost reduction, data-belt coverage, staff training), so they count as undefined; baseline_defined_share 0.6. No indicator states a baseline year (0.0). The earlier heuristic value of 0.7 was corrected.
- Benchmarks: one indicator uses an external ICT index dimension; the cost-reduction formula is complex and mixes units, with no stated data source.
- Some targets are very large relative to baseline (service digitisation multiplies several-fold; a few indicators move from zero to full coverage), raising feasibility doubts.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Only qualitative: a cross-secretariat digital-government steering committee, the state data-processing company providing technical continuity, and financial continuity through state budget transfers. No recurring O&M, licence, hosting or staff cost table; no quantified post-project funding line.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. Matrix covers only procurement and contract-management risks (delays, empty bids, vendor dependence with knowledge-transfer mitigation). Environmental, social, resettlement and expropriation risks declared "not applicable"; no data-protection or cyber risk row even though cybersecurity is a component. Legal risk limited to the pending state authorising law, with no mitigation.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, financed from state resources; the state authorising law was still to be approved by the state legislature at CC stage ("to be secured"); PPA for the next cycle under preparation with a dedicated digital-government programme.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. New UGP in the central administration secretariat with a strategic committee, focal units in each executing secretariat, and implementation agreements; responsibility matrix present but simplified. Prior experience with the lender and a track record of own-funded digital systems.
