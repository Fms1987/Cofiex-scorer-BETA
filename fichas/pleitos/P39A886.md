## Resumo
- Investment-type external loan to a state government, which will inject the proceeds as equity capital into its state-owned development agency to expand productive credit to micro, small and medium enterprises (including individual micro-entrepreneurs and informal ones) in the post-pandemic context; Union guarantee requested.
- Two components: (1) credit lines in three strands (general MSME and microcredit, women-led businesses under an existing state programme, sustainability/energy-efficiency/renewables), about 98% of cost; (2) a small IT/digital modernisation of the agency's credit-granting process (roughly 2% of cost).
- Delivery is direct by the agency through its existing agent network, correspondents and digital channel; the state acts as borrower and the agency as main executor.
- Counterpart stated at roughly a fifth of project cost (header shows 0.0, so the source of the counterpart is unclear); disbursement over 4 years, 60-month grace and 300-month amortisation, SOFR-based rate with swap options.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Diagnosis (credit access barriers, women entrepreneurs' gap, post-pandemic and climate shocks) and a narrative chain (cheaper capital leads to lower rates, more credit, jobs, wages, tax revenue, GDP) exist, but assumptions are not explicit or testable and no rate-reduction mechanism is quantified.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 8 indicators verified (approximate split: 7 outputs/volumes, 1 institutional-efficiency ratio; no true outcome indicators such as jobs, revenue or default). Four indicators are dollar-denominated credit volumes by strand/IT spend, three count supported enterprises, one is a technology-spend ratio.
- Baselines: only 1 of 8 has a stated baseline value (the IT-spend ratio); 6 baselines are 0 without explanation (new flows, counted as undefined here) and no reference year is given (year share 0). The sustainability strand has a very small beneficiary target relative to the others.
- Verification means and data sources are not stated; no impact evaluation or control group; no benchmarks used. Internal check: the text's beneficiary estimate is higher than the sum of the beneficiary indicator targets (inconsistency).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for a revolving-credit design. Repayments return to the agency and are re-lent; equity injection improves its capital adequacy ratio and funding mix. No O&M or OPEX table; debt-service source is not detailed beyond the state's capacity and the agency's income; IT recurrent costs not quantified.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. No risk matrix; legal, procurement (agency's own procurement regulation), environmental/social (agency socio-environmental policy, central-bank resolution, sector exclusion list, lender safeguards) addressed qualitatively; no licensing, resettlement or expropriation. Credit risk of the end-borrowers is not discussed.
- Counterpart: not yet secured. Authorising state law still to be passed in parallel with the COFIEX process; the plan (PPA) and strategic documents are said to include the credit lines, LOA/LDO compliance promised rather than shown.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Experienced executor with prior lender-funded capitalisation operations, no new hires needed, state credit committee approval already obtained, RACI matrix provided in annexes; no dedicated UGP. IT procurement is still at the study stage.
