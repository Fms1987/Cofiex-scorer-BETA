## Resumo
- Modality: NDB result-based sustainable-development loan (disbursement tied to pre-agreed indicator targets, hence no counterpart requirement), euro-denominated, 5-year disbursement, with a state counter-guarantee and a Union guarantee; borrower and executor is a state-owned water and sanitation utility treated as non-dependent.
- Finances the utility's investment plan towards the national universalisation targets: roughly 90% sewerage connections and associated systems, roughly 10% new water connections, and a small (about 1%) study and diagnosis of water losses.
- Delivery: utility's own project management unit under its financial administration area, supported by a supervision contractor that validates targets for disbursement; works via the state-company procurement statute, with integrated or semi-integrated contracting where designs are not ready.
- Scope is statewide with intervention locations not specified.
