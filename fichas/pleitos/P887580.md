## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a mid-sized municipality (direct execution, no intermediation); counterpart at the 20% minimum of total cost.
- A broad multi-sector urban infrastructure programme: public-management modernization (administrative centre, tax-system and georeferencing consultancy, digital-city connectivity, land-tenure regularization), social facilities (health, schools, culture, sport), green/urban parks, a landfill upgrade with biogas, solar PV on public buildings, drainage and road/sidewalk works, and an upgrade of a regional air-access facility.
- Several components are small relative to the whole (the NT puts sport/culture and energy around or below 5-10% of cost each); urban-development items weigh roughly 30% and management modernization roughly 20%.
- Executing entity: municipal infrastructure secretariat, with a programme management unit (UGP) to be created by decree and supported by supervision consultants.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. The CC gives a problem-solution-result chain by axis (administration, land tenure, social infrastructure, energy, environment, drainage/mobility, regional connectivity), each mapped to an indicator and to PPA objectives, but no explicit assumptions or causal pathway between outputs and outcomes. Many PPA objectives are claimed per indicator, which looks like over-attribution.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 11 indicators (verified); roughly 5 outcome and 6 output/progress measures (approximate judgement). Outputs include count of health facilities, title issuance, share of paved roads, school-works progress. Outcomes include road accidents, flood-exposed area, energy cost, green area per inhabitant, usage of cultural/sport facilities.
- Baselines: all 11 have a stated baseline with a reference year. Zero baselines counted as defined: digitalized administrative processes (the CC explains none exist yet) and school works (explained as not yet started). The air-access indicator is qualitative (baseline: no commercial or cargo flights; target: receive them), so its baseline is a state, not a value; counted as defined with some doubt.
- Verification: data to be collected is listed per indicator, but no frequency, source of verification or responsible unit is stated (tag no_verification). The air-access indicator has no measurable target; the cultural/sport-access indicator mixes absolute baselines with a percentage target and a loose formula.
- Internal inconsistencies: the road-paving indicator moves from a partial baseline to full paving, which looks disproportionate to the resources; the title indicator target is stated as "more than" the baseline stock; the NT itself criticises the air-access and digital-transformation indicators as weak.
- Benchmarks: none (no international index).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual maintenance costs are estimated per municipal secretariat (infrastructure, finance/planning, health, education, culture/sport, environment), with responsibilities assigned, but the CC gives no consolidated O&M-versus-revenue analysis and no legal or budget instrument securing them. Recurrent costs for the new social facilities (education and health being the largest lines) are material for a municipal budget.
- Counterpart: 20% of cost, to be secured; the CC does not cite LOA, PPA or an authorizing law. Counter-guarantees are to be set in the authorizing law (constitutional own-revenue and transfer revenues), with sufficiency left to STN at the PVL stage. No bank no-objection attached.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. A risk matrix with 13 risks (impact/probability, mitigation actions and responsible units), including environmental licensing, legal, expropriation/compensation under land regularization, technological risk for the digital city, solar PV performance, landfill environmental/legal compliance and institutional/operational risk of the air-access facility. Licensing is flagged as high impact, medium probability.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A UGP to be created by decree with four coordinations plus supervision consultants; executing secretariat reports prior experience with credit-financed works. Many sector secretariats participate, so coordination is the weak point.
- Preparation: land-regularization studies, landfill/biogas and several design/diagnostic steps reported as concluded; other interventions are still at planning stage.
