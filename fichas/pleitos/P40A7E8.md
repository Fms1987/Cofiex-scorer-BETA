## Resumo
- Policy-based loan (PBL) to the federal government, single disbursement straight to the Treasury, no counterpart (exempt under the policy-based-loan exception of the COFIEX counterpart resolution); roughly an 18-month disbursement window.
- Finances no specific expenditure: disbursement is tied to a package of institutional and regulatory reform measures on competitiveness and the business environment (the header sector label is a keyword guess; the content is not environmental).
- Four components: institutional governance and coordination; regulatory-improvement policy (impact assessment, regulatory agenda, maturity diagnostics); administrative simplification of services and foreign-trade procedures; digital tools for regulators and investment transparency. Gender-focused measures run across components.
- Borrower and executor is a line ministry's competitiveness secretariat with a small monitoring team; the lender also offers parallel non-reimbursable technical cooperation.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (leaning WEAK). Diagnosis is well referenced (competitiveness rankings, OECD regulatory indicators, cost-of-doing-business estimate) and four axes map to four components, but the causal chain from reform measures to indicator movement is not made explicit and no assumptions are stated; the NT calls the solutions vague and unfocused given the very broad scope.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Three indicators (verified), all outcome-level and objective/computable, but multidimensional and very broad, so attribution to the programme is hard; the NT rated indicator-result coherence at well under half of its maximum.
- Baselines: all three have a stated baseline value (share 1.0); two carry a reference year (a 2022 index score and a 2023 cost estimate), the export-firm count has no year (share 0.67). No zero baselines.
- Verification: sources are named (an external regulatory-capacity index, an economy-wide cost methodology, ministry export statistics) but no verification schedule or frequency is given. Targets are modest point values, no wide ranges, none deferred.
- Inconsistency: the expected result on women entrepreneurs and small exporters has no matching gender-disaggregated indicator and the NT sees it as weakly linked to the measures. Prior-action style measures are listed in the text, not as a formal matrix.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no O&M/OPEX arises (no assets); sustainability rests on legal instruments already enacted, institutional governance bodies and lender technical cooperation for knowledge transfer. NT rated it acceptable. No budget line quantified.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: short list of institutional/operational risks (institutional change, shifting agenda, budget restriction, IT obstacles) with mitigations (inclusion in PPA and ministry strategic plan, monitoring working group, key-risk escalation). Procurement, expropriation, resettlement and environmental/social risks declared not applicable; licensing not relevant. Some measures already adopted by decree, shown as implementation progress.
- Counterpart: none required (PBL exception). PPA alignment documented through an extract attached to the CC; no sectoral plans cited.
- A.4 Implementation: coordinating secretariat with a monitoring team, responsibility matrix only in an annex; NT penalised missing team experience, procurement/safeguard familiarity and absence of recent external-credit experience of the borrower (experience score zero).
