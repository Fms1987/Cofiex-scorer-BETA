## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan from CAF to a state government (subnational, direct execution by the state; no intermediation). Ticket in the 50-100M USD band, counterpart at the 20% minimum; 5-year execution, roughly 17.5-year total loan term with 5 years of grace, floating SOFR-based rate with a spread of about 2%.
- Financed: urban-wetland conservation and climate adaptation in a metropolitan river basin: a mosaic of new protected areas plus an international wetland-site designation, nature-based green infrastructure (linear parks with cycle paths, sustainable drainage, constructed filtering wetlands for a large social-housing estate), and community environmental-education and observation facilities.
- Components (3): conservation and environmental requalification (roughly 60% of cost); community belonging and ecotourism facilities (a bit over a third); programme management with works supervision, a management-support firm and external audit (a few percent).
- Entity type: state planning secretariat as lead executor, with the infrastructure and environment secretariats and a state research institute as partners; funds executed centrally, no transfers between bodies.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree is well developed (wetland degradation and irregular occupation, water pollution and missing sanitation, biodiversity loss, climate vulnerability) with causes, and solution and results sections map to specific objectives; assumptions are not stated as testable, and delivery depends on third-party legal acts (creation of protected areas, international site designation).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 8 indicators, approximately 6-7 outcome and 1-2 output-type (legal protection and area covered); each has a SMART narrative, a named data source, a baseline and a target; verification is by the state environment body, water-quality parameter monitoring and land-use mapping, with no independent verification stated.
- Baselines: all 8 have a stated baseline (share 1.0); 7 of 8 give a reference year or period (0.875); the cycle-path indicator says "currently none" with no year. Zero baselines counted as defined because explained: no legally protected areas exist in the basin (new asset), and no cycle paths exist in target areas.
- Benchmarks: PPA key indicators (adapted) for protected-area coverage, water quality and sanitation; water-quality index band used for the water target.
- Internal inconsistencies: the sanitation-index indicator reports an annual treated volume as baseline while the target is a share of collected sewage treated; the tourism baseline counts foreign arrivals at state level while the target is regional sustainable-tourism growth; the first indicator mixes "protected" and "restored" in one area target.
- Wide or loose ranges: community-engagement index target is a 5-10% band; the irregular-occupation baseline is a 30-40% band; the water-quality target covers only half of nearby water bodies.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. Post-works operating and maintenance envelope stated as about 5% of programme value per year from the state treasury, to be inserted annually in the infrastructure secretariat budget after works end, plus preventive maintenance contracts, cooperation terms with municipalities and community groups, and a sustainability plan to be prepared by the UGP; no itemised OPEX by facility or category, no legal act backing the recurring allocation. The financial-sustainability risk is rated low impact and low probability, citing revenue generation and PPPs without detail.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. 12 risks with impact/probability ratings and mitigations, covering stakeholder engagement, climate extremes, political change, regulatory change, expropriation and relocation of residents (medium/medium), archaeological finds, weak maintenance, the state law authorising the credit operation (high impact, low probability) and procurement delays (high impact, medium probability). No status of environmental licensing per component.
- Counterpart (20%): to be secured; mitigation lists inclusion in state PPA, LDO and LOA, and the state PPA already carries an urban-park goal; relocation and compensation costs may be borne as counterpart. Counter-guarantee on state revenue transfers to be assessed by STN at the limits-verification stage; no creditor no-objection attached.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Planning secretariat executes through a UGP supported by a contracted management firm, works supervision and external audit; RACI matrix annexed; several partner bodies but no inter-agency transfers. Designs at basic level for most facilities and executive level for two; staff profiles described only qualitatively.
