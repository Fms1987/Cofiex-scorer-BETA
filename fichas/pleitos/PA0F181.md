## Resumo
- Investment loan (IDB, second phase of an ongoing education programme) for a state government; direct execution by the state education secretariat through an existing programme management unit (UGP). Cost is roughly 40% works, 20% goods, 20% training, 15% non-consulting services, a small share consulting and admin.
- Four components: (1) expansion of basic-education coverage by building full-time, indigenous, sustainable and standard schools (about 40% of cost); (2) learning progression and quality: acceleration, remedial support, assessment, adult education, instructional resources and connectivity (about a quarter); (3) management, assessment, teacher and manager training, new school-management information system (about a quarter); (4) programme administration (about 6%).
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum, from the state treasury.

No O&M plan for the technology/connectivity items.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Narrative risk sections only, no matrix. Procurement and legal-act risks judged minimal given the existing UGP and bidding sub-commission; environmental risks called temporary and low, to be handled with an environmental management plan and a to-be-prepared environmental and social assessment; licensing via the state environmental agency with no status per site; no expropriation (land donated by municipalities); social risk confined to indigenous schools (consultation promised). - Counterpart: 20% minimum from state treasury; to be secured. Authorising state law for the loan and counter-guarantee still in drafting; education goals said to be in the state PPA and state education plan.
