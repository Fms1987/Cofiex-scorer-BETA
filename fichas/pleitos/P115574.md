## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending funding line. A federal development bank borrows from IBRD (investment project financing with tranche-type disbursements, Union guarantee, SOFR-based floating rate, long grace and amortization up to about 30 years, 4-year schedule in the cronograma) to fund its own loans to sub-borrowers.
- Financed: a single component and single product supporting investment in low-carbon practices, infrastructure and technologies in the energy and industrial value chains (renewables, green fuels, electrification, energy efficiency, technology development, including nascent industries).
- Delivery: intermediated; the bank lends to public or private sub-borrowers under its ordinary credit process, with eligibility criteria and portfolio to be agreed with the lender; beneficiaries are firms in low-carbon chains.
- Entity type: federal state-owned development bank; possible later concessional complement would come via separate CCs.

- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Rationale links financing gaps for low-carbon investment to leveraged private investment, emissions mitigation and jobs, with three specific objectives; assumptions (pipeline maturity of nascent segments, demand) are touched on but not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 6 indicators (verified): about 2 outcome-type (annual GHG mitigation, jobs created) and about 4 output/process (financing volume, average sub-loan term, number of projects as planned/realized %, total investment leveraged); approximate judgment. Baseline and target are 0 for every indicator (target placeholders, unexplained zeros), so baseline_defined_share 0.0 and no reference year (baseline_with_year_share 0.0).
- Formulas are mostly plain sums; no verification mechanism or data source; no benchmark; NT notes average loan term is a feature of the deal rather than a performance measure, and the evaluation period was inferred to equal the 4-year implementation window (NT quality of indicators about 0.16/0.30). Targets are deferred to preparation.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Funding comes from the bank's ordinary lending cycle and repayment by sub-borrowers; no maintenance/OPEX forecast or sustainability instruments (NT about 0.11/0.30). The bank states that the resources are already in its investment budget.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Covers legal (main act is the loan contract with guarantee, risk low), procurement (assessed per sub-loan, technical cooperation for immature projects), expropriation/resettlement (borne by sub-borrower, bank does not finance expropriation), demand risk (low, citing growth in the bank's consultations and approvals), and socio-environmental risk (subprojects subject to licensing and classification, safeguard procedures to be agreed with the lender). No formal matrix; licensing handled per sub-project.
- Counterpart: not applicable/none stated (header 0%); bank financing is from sub-loans.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Bank executes through its existing processes; simple responsibility matrix; no information on a dedicated team (NT zero on that item); strong track record with external credit.
