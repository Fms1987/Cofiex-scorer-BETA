## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IDB to a capital municipality, second phase of a long-running programme, federal guarantee, counterpart at the 20% minimum, ticket in the 200-400M USD band, long tenor (about 23 years total, long grace, 6-year disbursement).
- Financed: integrated urban-watershed sanitation (streambed/channel treatment, sanitary sewage interception, linear/riparian parks, informal-settlement upgrading and resettlement) in roughly ten sub-basins, plus a second theme on climate resilience (hydrological monitoring network expansion, a flood-forecasting system, nature-based-solutions planning).
- Delivery: direct municipal execution through a programme management unit and an executing unit drawing on the housing company, public-works body, urban-water-management office and civil-defence unit; works dominate the budget (roughly 95%), with smaller shares for resettlement/compensation and resilience studies/equipment.
- Entity: municipal government, prior experience with an earlier phase of the same programme with the same lender; two components (integrated environmental sanitation; more resilient city) plus a consultancy/audit subcomponent.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG. Builds on a first phase with the same lender; diagnosis (flood-prone informal settlements, untreated sewage into watercourses, outdated monitoring network) links to specific interventions (streambed treatment, nature-based drainage, resettlement, monitoring upgrade) and explicit prior lessons feed the design.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 11 indicators verified; roughly 4 are outcome/index-type (an environmental-sanitation composite index and three service-coverage ratios) and about 7 are output/count-type (treated streambed length, linear-park length, resettled families, local social-plan count, monitoring stations, share of women employed on site).
- Baseline: the 4 ratio/index indicators carry an explicit non-zero baseline value (about 64% with a stated share, no reference year given for any of the 11); the count-type indicators start at a 0 I treated as defined only where the CC frames it as a genuinely new activity count (new monitoring stations added, new local plans, new linear-park km); the flood-exposure population indicator has a non-zero baseline and a zero target (full elimination), also counted as defined.
- Verification: formulas given for the four ratio indicators (population served / total population, per service type); the other indicators are simple counts with no named data source or periodicity in the CC.
- Weaknesses: no reference year on any baseline; several output counts (resettled families, PTTS plans) are not restated as rates relative to programme scope; women-on-site target is modest (10%) relative to a stated municipal policy floor.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG on the second attempt (full marks on this NT sub-item), ADEQUATE on the first. O&M of new sanitation and park assets is expected to fall on existing municipal structures and a water/sanitation utility already serving the municipality; no dedicated new O&M budget line is quantified in the CC text reviewed, but the NT on the second pass credits full sustainability marks.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE to STRONG. Narrative covers legal acts, procurement (long-standing lender relationship aids familiarity with procurement/safeguard rules), expropriation (roughly a third of relocated families to be formally expropriated, with a dedicated legal unit), environmental licensing (works subject to municipal environmental/urban council licensing under a specific legal provision) and social/resettlement risk; no risk matrix.
- Counterpart: origin and percentage stated (Res. COFIEX 3/2019); CAPAG B rating cited as favourable for new borrowing.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Dedicated management and executing units staffed by existing agencies with programme experience from the prior phase; design studies for most sub-basins already at preliminary or basic design stage; one sub-basin's project still to be procured.
