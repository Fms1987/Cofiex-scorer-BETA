## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (AFD, sovereign guarantee, ticket band above 400M USD once referenced, at a European-currency denomination) to a federal ministry acting as borrower/executor; 5-year disbursement, 20-year total term, floating EURIBOR plus about 1.7%, grace of roughly 5.5 years; no counterpart (100% external).
- What is financed: a capital injection into the same three regional development funds as a companion operation with another lender, on-lent to private borrowers across a wide sector menu (mobility, logistics, renewable energy, sanitation, water, bioeconomy, health/education PPPs, forest concessions); split across the three fund products roughly 30/40/30.
- Delivery model: intermediated/on-lending, identical governance to the companion operation (regional development agencies screen eligibility, accredited operator banks appraise and bear credit risk). Single component, three products; a project-management unit is to be created inside the executor. Explicitly presented as complementary to an earlier, larger operation with a different multilateral for the same funds.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (low end). Same logic as the sibling operation (more fund credit, more private investment, jobs, emissions reductions); diagnosis emphasises logistics/technical-capacity gaps while the instrument is a broad multi-sector credit line, a partial mismatch the NT also flagged by noting several specific objectives (health/education PPPs, social inclusion, reforestation) have no matching indicator at all.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 6 indicators verified; approximately 3 outcome/impact (emissions reduction, forest area under concession, jobs generated) and 3 financing outputs (fund resources invested, growth in projects financed, innovation projects supported).
- Baselines: 4 of 6 counted as defined (baseline_defined_share 0.67): the numeric fund-resources baseline plus three "with/without project" zero baselines (jobs, emissions-reduction formula, innovation-projects formula) accepted as defined because the CC explicitly frames them as counterfactual deltas with a stated base/target year; forest-area zero baseline NOT counted (no counterfactual or new-asset explanation given for that one). All indicators here do carry a stated base year/target year in the table (unlike the sibling CC), so baseline_with_year_share is 0.67, equal to the defined share.
- Verification mechanism absent; two indicators (carbon reduction, forest concession area) judged by the NT as not directly attributable to this fund-capitalisation operation. Several stated specific objectives have no indicator at all (NT-identified gap).
- No wide-range indicators; internal consistency otherwise similar to the sibling CC (same six indicators, same funds, different currency and lender).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for the executor, weak for the assets. O&M of financed assets rests entirely with private borrowers, appraised case by case by operator banks; the funds' administrative structure is said to absorb the extra volume with no new hiring or systems, funded by capital reflows and fixed operator-bank remuneration. No OPEX figures (expected for a fund).
- Counterpart: none required; budget authorisation to be secured (same as the sibling operation — not in the next annual budget bill per the budget-office NT; expenses classified as financial, so no effect on primary-result or spending-cap rules).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Two risks only (economic slowdown reducing credit demand, borrower default) with the same mitigations as the sibling CC (excess demand, operator-bank-required collateral, reallocation flexibility). Environmental/social screening delegated to borrowers and operator banks, but this CC additionally cites the lender's own environmental and social framework and commitment-plan instruments as applicable safeguards; no component-level licensing status (not public-sector executed).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Same RACI matrix and governance as the sibling operation; PMU to be created by ministerial decree; NT recorded the executor had no external-credit project in execution in the system (experience criterion scored zero, though a prior World Bank programme and an operation in preparation with another lender are cited).
