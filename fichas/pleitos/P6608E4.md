## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / financial-intermediation operation — a regional development bank borrows externally and channels credit to municipalities and private firms across several southern states, under a Union guarantee with a state counter-guarantee.
- Finances climate resilience and disaster-recovery credit lines: reconstruction and resilience for one storm-affected state, renewable-energy generation, sustainable urban mobility, sustainable productive infrastructure, and municipal/firm resilience investments (water-related resilience infrastructure, stormwater, production-unit relocation/modernisation).
- Three components organised as sub-lines (economic-recovery credit, energy-transition and sustainable-infrastructure credit, resilience credit for municipalities and firms); delivery is intermediated — final sub-projects are not yet identified and will be prospected and approved case by case by the bank's own multidisciplinary and credit-committee process.
- Entity type: regional development bank (financial intermediary), not a direct executing public entity; this is a repeat sector for the same proponent with several prior approved operations.
- Header flags a lender mismatch (one field names a different multilateral than the CC text, which consistently names a different one) and an undetermined counterpart share; the CC states no counterpart is foreseen given the intermediary's institutional nature.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Climate-risk diagnosis for the region (droughts, extreme-rain disaster) links clearly to three solutions (recovery credit, energy-transition/sustainable-infrastructure credit, resilience credit); causal chain is plausible but assumptions about sub-project pipeline quality are not made explicit (classic on-lending deferral).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE per the NT. Verified count: 8 indicators; approximately 5 outcome-type (portfolio volume to infrastructure, share of disaster-hit municipalities financed, average revenue increase, jobs increase, mobility-portfolio volume) and approximately 3 output-type (renewable capacity added, CO2 avoided, number of resilience projects).
- Baselines: 4 of 8 indicators have an explained zero baseline tied to "non-existence of the programme at submission"; the other 4 use a prior-year portfolio figure (also a stated value) — baseline_defined_share = 1.0 in effect, but this ficha records the judged 0.5 from the header pending final check; all baselines lack an explicit calendar reference year beyond "immediately before the sub-loan", so baseline_with_year_share = 0.0.
- NT flagged an inconsistency: two "percentage increase" indicators present a 100% baseline (post/pre ratio normalised) instead of a 0% baseline, which the NT treats as a partial failure of the baseline-value rule even though it is internally explained.
- Verification mechanism: absent from the indicator table itself; relies on the bank's own portfolio and monitoring systems.
- On-lending deferral: no pipeline or eligible sub-project criteria disclosed yet; sub-projects remain to be originated and approved through ordinary credit process.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL/not applicable as scored by the NT. No OPEX estimate is given because sub-projects are not yet identified; post-implementation O&M is explicitly the responsibility of each final sub-borrower, not the intermediary. The intermediary itself commits to supervision/fiscalisation during execution.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG in breadth, no formal matrix. Legal risk (regulatory change over a long tenor, pending state counter-guarantee law with a named backup counter-guarantor state), procurement/contract risk (asset-liability and portfolio risk framed in banking terms), expropriation (institution will not fund known involuntary-resettlement cases), environmental and social risk screened through an internally accredited socio-environmental and climate risk system, resettlement (none planned; suspension clause if discovered later).
- Counterpart: none required given the intermediary's legal nature (undetermined/zero in header, explained in text).
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Dedicated project-management unit inside the international-funding department, detailed responsibility matrix across financial, legal, procurement, socio-environmental and technical-analysis functions; intermediary has no prior experience with this specific multilateral partner (flagged by the NT as a partial weakness in the financier-choice justification) but broad experience with several other multilaterals and with Union-guaranteed operations generally.
