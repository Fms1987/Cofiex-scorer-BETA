## Resumo
- Investment operation channelled through a subnational, non-dependent state development bank acting as financial intermediary: the bank is the borrower of record, and municipalities in its state are the final sub-borrowers (intermediated / on-lending delivery model), with Union guarantee.
- Finances a programmatic credit line for municipal infrastructure across three axes: urban/transport/mobility works and machinery; energy, water and basic sanitation; and social infrastructure (health, education, housing, leisure).
- Sub-loans are governed by the bank's pre-existing credit-line rules (population and revenue-based eligibility caps); individual sub-projects are not committed at CC stage, and allocations across products are described as indicative/interchangeable pending future origination.
- Entity type: state-owned non-dependent development bank (financial institution), which is why no counterpart is required under the applicable regulatory exemption.
