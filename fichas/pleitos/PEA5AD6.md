## Resumo
Investment operation for a capital municipality, direct delivery by the municipal government, intermediated through a municipal project unit (UGP) under the mobility secretariat. Finances fleet electrification and associated charging infrastructure for a bus rapid transit system, urban-safety/accessibility retrofits along BRT corridors, institutional strengthening (mobility-plan update, operational-management systems, a gender/race workforce-inclusion track), and project management/supervision/external audit. Entity type: municipal government (direct borrower with sovereign guarantee).
