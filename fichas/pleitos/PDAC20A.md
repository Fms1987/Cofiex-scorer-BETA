## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (CAF) for a large capital municipality, resubmitted after an earlier attempt (the approved version is a reduced-scope successor with a smaller ticket and a counterpart share above half of cost).
- Finances flood-control macro-drainage (detention reservoirs in urban stream basins, with pumping and public-space recovery on top of the structures) and a telemetry/early-warning monitoring system tied to the municipal flood alert and emergency-management centres.
- Two components: macro-drainage infrastructure for climate resilience (the bulk of cost) and programme management (supervision, UGP support, external audit). Direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat with a newly structured UGP; the stated framing is climate adaptation under the Art. 25 sublimit.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Explicit problem-solution-result chain (peak-flow deficit and missing early warning to reduced flood footprint, fewer exposed households, fewer road cut-offs), with an adherence synthesis; assumptions (design storm, O&M continuity) only implicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified count: 7 indicators; approximately 6 outcome-type (flood area, exposed residents, precarious dwellings, road interruptions, affected trips, beneficiary population) and 1 output-type (solid waste diverted).
- Verification: strong for flood indicators (as-built reports and rainy-season field surveys by the hydraulics foundation, emergency-centre and traffic-company records); the beneficiary and trips indicators rely on census and origin-destination survey modelling.
- Baselines: all 7 stated with a reference year (data collected in 2024; trips baseline from a 2023 survey). Two zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains them (waste diverted is an additional volume from new structures; beneficiaries zero because no intervention is complete).
- Weaknesses: step-function targets (full result in the year works finish, 100% protection at the 100-year design storm); beneficiary indicator is orders of magnitude above the exposed-resident indicator; one indicator states a final year later than its own table; the NT of an earlier version flagged some indicators as not fully SMART.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M is quantified separately for the reservoirs, the recovered public spaces and the telemetry system; funded from the own municipal budget; responsibility assigned to local sub-prefectures by a municipal decree and to the hydraulics foundation under a recurring services contract. No multi-year O&M table or explicit budget-line reference.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG. Matrix of roughly twenty risks with impact/probability and mitigation, covering judicial, licensing, procurement, social, utilities interference, financial, authorising-law, public-space and environmental risks. Licensing is advanced: one structure with installation licence and under construction, the other with prior licence and heritage/traffic opinions issued; public land, no expropriation or resettlement expected.
- Counterpart: above half of cost, said to come from own municipal resources; not shown as secured in LOA/PPA, though the works are inside the municipal PPA and drainage master plans. Authorising law for the credit operation rated high impact, medium probability.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Large secretariat with prior MDB experience and technical staff, UGP to be set up inside it, responsibility matrix present but brief; no inter-agency fund transfers.
