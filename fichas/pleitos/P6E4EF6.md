## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan under a multi-state fiscal-modernisation credit line (third generation), a state government borrowing with Union guarantee and counter-guarantee; counterpart at roughly 9% of cost.
- Finances modernisation of the state treasury/tax administration: fiscal governance and IT (about a third of cost), tax administration and litigation (about half, including intelligent fiscal inspection and equipment for border/roadside tax posts), and public financial management (about a tenth).
- Cross-cutting themes: alignment with tax-reform harmonisation, data-driven management, green procurement and a small self-generation energy item, gender/race/diversity training.
- Delivery: direct execution by the state finance secretariat through a program coordination unit with product leaders; second attempt after an earlier withdrawal.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Maturity-diagnostic-driven problem lists for three areas map to solutions and to three expected results; supported by an IDB impact study of the earlier generation (higher tax collection versus non-participating states). No explicit assumptions or causal chain from products to fiscal sustainability.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 12 indicators verified; approximately 8 outcome-type (cost to collect, budget deviation, tax revenue growth, IT incident index, strategy execution, first-instance ruling share, online services share, strategy-alignment share) and about 4 output-type (leaders trained, climate studies, own-energy generation). Formulas given for all; no verification-source column.
- Baselines: 9 of 12 defined (0.75). Three zero baselines counted as undefined because the CC does not explain them (climate-alignment share, climate-impact studies, self-generated energy). No reference year on any baseline (year share 0).
- Weaknesses: IT quality index has a baseline above 100 with a decreasing target (unclear scale); gender indicator measures share of women among trained leaders, not capability; targets lack interim values.
- Benchmarks: none external beyond the earlier-generation impact study.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurrent cost quantified at about 1.8% of project value per year, to be paid from the secretariat's own budget after closing; maintenance during execution financed by the loan via terms of reference; a sustainability plan is promised during preparation.
- Counterpart (about 9% of cost) tied to a named budget programme in the draft PPA revision and the LOA, but the authorising law still has to pass the state legislature after COFIEX approval (to be secured). Counter-guarantee to the Union agreed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal/institutional risks detailed (authorising law, fiscal-responsibility compliance, unit creation by decree); procurement delay risks with six mitigations drawn from earlier generations; environmental, social, expropriation and resettlement fields blank.
