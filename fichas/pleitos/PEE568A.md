## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan to a border municipality with federal guarantee, CAF lender, 20% counterpart (largely in-kind via a land parcel plus budget share); small ticket band, direct execution by the municipality.
- Combines a debt-restructuring component (refinancing an existing multilateral loan, about half of total cost) with an urban component (about 45%: linear park on a disused rail corridor, an alternative road pair, paving/drainage in peripheral neighbourhoods, a technology park) and a small programme-management component.
- Entity type: general-purpose subnational government (municipality), executing unit within the infrastructure secretariat with partner agencies for environment, traffic, heritage and finance.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Five problem-solution-result chains (fiscal fragility, degraded public space, congested roads, drainage/environmental risk, economic diversification) link to components, but the chain from refinancing to higher investment share and from the technology park to firm creation relies on unstated assumptions (demand, governance of the park).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE (leaning STRONG on form). 13 indicators, approximately 8 outcome-type (fiscal liquidity, investment share of revenue, sanitation adequacy index, flood-prone area reduction, travel time, park area per capita, vegetation cover, cross-border vehicle flow) and approximately 5 output/activity-type (paved network share, park visitors, firms installed, startup survival, events held).
- Baselines: 13 of 13 stated with reference year 2024; four zero baselines (park visitors, firms, startup survival, events) counted as defined because the CC ties them to assets that do not yet exist. Targets and verification sources (municipal fiscal statements, civil-defence mapping, SNIS, remote sensing, mobility surveys, customs records) are stated for each indicator; no independent evaluation.
- Internal inconsistencies: the paved-network indicator is named an expansion rate but expressed as coverage share; the startup-survival indicator needs three years of operation yet is dated at programme end; the cross-border flow target is largely driven by external factors; the flood-area indicator uses a hectare baseline while another indicator mixes in a composite sanitation index that the works only partly influence.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M is quantified separately for roads/drainage/signage, the park and the technology park, with funding from LOA allocations (property tax, public-lighting contribution, intergovernmental transfers) plus ancillary revenue (concessions, space rental); the technology-park model depends on assumed revenue. Debt-service burden is handled through the refinancing and STN/PVL review.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Six-risk matrix with impact/probability (authorising law, tendering, design quality, environmental licensing of drainage works, social disruption during works, FX and input costs) with mitigations. Additional unmitigated issues: access to the rail-corridor land is still being requested from federal bodies, and the land parcel for the tech park was valued internally after the external appraisal was late.
- Counterpart: 20%, to be secured through the authorising law and municipal budget; the municipal PPA in force is ending and the new one is in preparation; only a small part of the counterpart is identified as a concrete asset (the land parcel).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Dedicated project execution unit with a RACI matrix, executing secretariat with prior experience from a previous multilateral operation, supervision and external audit financed in the management component.
