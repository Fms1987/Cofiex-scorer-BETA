## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a large capital municipality, submitted under the environmental and climate sublimit; counterpart at the 20% minimum; SOFR-based rate, multi-year grace, total term of roughly 17 years.
- Finances the first implementation block of a municipal risk-reduction plan: structural works in high and very-high risk geological and hydrological areas (slope containment, stream channelling and drainage), housing solutions for families removed, and requalification of vacated areas with nature-based solutions.
- Three components: socio-environmental infrastructure (roughly 70% of cost), sustainable housing solutions (rent assistance, new housing units, social work; roughly 20%), and programme management (supervision, UGP support, audit).
- Delivery model is direct and multi-secretariat: the planning secretariat is executing body and chairs a plan-level steering committee; works and housing secretariats execute, civil defence supports.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Context (population exposed, risk-grade breakdown), plan-level prioritisation (multi-criteria method using social vulnerability and litigation), three specific objectives each linked to indicators and PPA areas; assumptions about reoccupation, licensing and housing delivery are discussed in risks rather than as a testable chain.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 indicators, roughly 2 outcome-type (share of high-risk areas formally closed by civil defence; remaining-area requalification with nature-based solutions working) and 3 outputs or counts (families removed, families benefiting from requalification, risk areas registered in the monitoring system). Each has formula, data to collect, official sources and target year; the NT judged the two key indicators as SMART.
- Baselines: 5 of 5 stated as zero for 2026 and explained by the CC (plan implementation only started, no works under way or completed), so all counted as defined = 1.0, with reference year = 1.0.
- Verification mechanism present (civil defence formal closure record, housing registry, municipal strategic monitoring system module). Weak spots: the institutional indicator only measures registration in a system, and the first indicator target is a small share of all risk areas.
- No wide ranges or deferred indicators.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. O&M is absorbed by existing local-government maintenance crews under a municipal decree; annual unit maintenance cost and total yearly spend are quantified from the previous year, with costs to be met from the municipal budget. No separate OPEX line for new structures or housing management after the project.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE/STRONG. Seven risks with impact/probability, causes, effects and mitigations: regulatory/institutional, legal acts, authorising law, contracting and tendering, social (resettlement), operational, financial, plus environmental licensing (variable permit category; early licensing and schedule contingency planned).
- Counterpart: municipal budget provision to be made compatible with PPA, LDO and LOA; yearly counterpart schedule given. Authorising law exists but one of its provisions on complementary collateral is flagged as an open risk.
- Counter-guarantees tied to the authorising law; STN assessment at the later limits-verification stage.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Steering committee by decree, named executing body and partner secretariats with sizeable technical teams and prior external-financing experience; RACI matrix annexed. Lender no-objection files are listed in the annexes.
