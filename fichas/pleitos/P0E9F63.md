## Resumo
- Modality: supplementary external credit from IFAD to a federal development bank with Union guarantee, topping up an earlier IFAD operation by the same borrower that the committee had already cleared; no counterpart declared; 8-year disbursement and execution horizon.
- Finances climate-resilient family-farming in a semiarid region: resilient production systems (agroforestry-type backyards, collective rangeland management, school plots, pilot saline agriculture; about a quarter of cost), water access and harvesting/reuse technologies (about 60%), and communication, knowledge management and micro-entrepreneur support plus a small project unit (about a tenth).
- Delivery: intermediated; the bank passes resources to sub-borrowers (state governments) selected through an earlier public call, with the bank's operational departments appraising and monitoring.
- Entity type: federal state-owned development bank; poverty and extreme-poverty family farmers as target group.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. The diagnosis (water scarcity, degraded native vegetation, inadequate production practices, poverty) maps to the three components and to expected effects on income, food security and youth retention; assumptions and sub-borrower selection effects are not made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified count: 7 indicators; approximately 1 outcome-leaning (area recovered) and the rest outputs (families served, water-storage structures built, household sanitation systems, families receiving information, women's workshops, youth training workshops).
- Baselines: all 7 are stated as zero with no explanation of a counterfactual; counted as undefined (share 0.0). No reference year. Calculation formula is just "sum" for every indicator and no verification source or milestone is given.
- No income, productivity, food-security or water-availability outcome indicator despite those being the stated objectives; no benchmarks; no wide ranges or deferred indicators.
- Internal inconsistency: indicator units mix families, hectares and event counts without a link to component budget shares.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Only the standard credit procedure of the bank for sub-loans is mentioned; no funding source for upkeep of water structures and productive systems, and no maintenance cost estimate.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. No legal, procurement or expropriation risk analysis; environmental and social risk stated as low because investments are small, with reference to a socio-environmental risk management plan. No matrix.
- Counterpart: none declared (zero), though the first operation had a sizeable local counterpart; the bank also announces own-resource and grant support in parallel.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Long experience of the bank with external funding and the earlier operation with the same creditor; operational staffing described only superficially; project unit is small; sub-borrowers will present themselves only after funds are raised, so execution capacity is untested.
