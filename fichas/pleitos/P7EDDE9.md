## Resumo
- Modality: investment-style external loan to a federal state-owned financial institution, with no counterpart (0%), financing a credit line on blended-finance terms combined with a separate national green-finance facility.
- Finances: (1) credit lines to developers for building and retrofitting housing that holds recognised environmental certifications (roughly the bulk of the loan); (2) institutional strengthening and technical assistance for the lender and for public and private agents (carbon-footprint calculator, training, product innovation).
- Delivery model: intermediated; the entity lends to private developers and does not execute works itself, with no direct transfers among participants.
- Five indicators, mostly outputs; the CC frames the programme as addressing a sustainable-housing credit gap.
