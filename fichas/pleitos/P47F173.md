## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a state government, counterpart at the 20% minimum; runs for the environmental/climate annual sublimit.
- Finances a broad state-level forest and bioeconomy programme: deforestation and wildfire prevention (brigade training, remote monitoring), land-use/protected-area studies and management plans, community reserve and forest-management support, agroforestry nurseries and community processing hubs, municipal environmental institution strengthening, and a marine/coastal ecosystem and blue-economy component.
- Six components, the last being management/supervision/audit; direct execution by the state environment secretariat as sole main executor, with the planning secretariat and the fire service as support partners.
- Scope is very wide for the ticket (many themes, many small products); beneficiaries are smallholder and coastal producers, traditional communities and rural landowners.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-cause-consequence trees and a solution-to-result chain exist for each theme, but assumptions are not stated as testable and the marine component has no matching causal chain.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators (verified), approximately 5 outcome-type and 2 output-type (seedling production, campaign/brigade coverage); each has formula, data to collect and a SMART justification, with official satellite/statistical sources for some and documentary evidence for others.
- All 7 have a stated baseline value and a reference year (2023 or 2024); no zero baselines. Baseline shares are therefore 1.0 and 1.0.
- Weaknesses: the marine/blue-economy component has no indicator; the bioeconomy indicator measures one single product as proxy; a few targets are very aggressive (tripling, nearly all municipalities, large multiple of current seedling output); one baseline percentage and its stated team count look internally inconsistent; the PPA alignment table cites indicator numbers that do not exist in the final list of 7 (leftover from a longer earlier list).
- Benchmarks: PPA strategic objectives and national monitoring series; no creditor/LAC comparator.

- Counterpart: asserted as recognised in the state budget guidelines law as a strategic action, with LOA and PPA excerpts attached; no dedicated enabling law or earmarked line described.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Experienced environment secretariat with prior donor-funded projects and thematic superintendencies; fund transfers to partners via agreements; no separate UGP profile, procurement capacity risk acknowledged.
