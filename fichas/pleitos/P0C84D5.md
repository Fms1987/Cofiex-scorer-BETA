## Resumo
- Modality: specific investment programme with IDB financing and federal guarantee; SOFR-based cost, 5-year disbursement, 5-year grace, 25-year amortisation; counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances institutional strengthening of public security, prison and social-prevention systems: crime analysis, intelligence-led policing, internal-control and body-camera systems, forensic and fleet modernisation; prison management, treatment, alternatives to prison and reentry support; mediation, human rights and youth violence-prevention services; plus management, evaluations and audit.
- Spending is split roughly a third each across the three substantive components, with about 5% for administration and evaluation.
- Delivery: direct execution by a state government through a justice and human-rights secretariat with two co-executing secretariats and a steering committee.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG-ADEQUATE. Three problems (lethal and property violence, reoffending, weak access to justice) map to three components, each justified with evidence from impact studies (results-based policing, risk-needs-responsivity model, cognitive-behavioural and restorative interventions); expected impact and specific results are stated per component. Assumptions are implicit rather than tested; territorial targeting of operational support is explained by crime concentration.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 10 indicators verified, labelled by the CC as 4 impact and 6 result indicators (so roughly 4 impact-level, 6 outcome/output-level; labels taken from the CC). Each has a formula, unit, baseline and target; a year-by-year progress spreadsheet was supplied in response to prior GTEC advice. Benchmarks: national justice and prison-system statistics as data sources; no external standard adopted.
- Baselines: 8 of 10 have a stated value. The 2 zero baselines (reduction of reoffending risk level; number of youths receiving specialised prevention) were counted as undefined because the CC does not explain them. The indicator table gives no reference year for any baseline, so baseline-with-year share is 0 (the diagnosis cites sources with years but not tied to the table).
- Verification: programme includes mid-term, final and impact evaluations plus external audit; indicators draw on administrative data. Weak points: no disaggregation of most indicators by race or gender despite the equity framing; some capacity indicators are close to outputs; scale of targets not linked to cost.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-WEAK. Sustainability declaration lists existing maintenance, fuel, IT and works contracts and budget lines, and states treasury funding to maintain acquired assets and effective activities; programme is inserted in the multi-year plan, yet execution runs past that plan period. NT found no projection of post-programme maintenance costs.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK-ADEQUATE. Legal risk (authorising law) and a procurement risk table with mitigations are present, plus technology-specific risks (body cameras, predictive and intelligence systems) with protocols. Environmental, social, expropriation and resettlement risks are declared nil without justification; no risk matrix covering implementation, institutional or political risks of the reform agenda.
- Counterpart: 20%, drawn from the budgets of the three participating secretariats; authorising law and federal counter-guarantee still to be secured.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Executing secretariat with a UGP to be created by ordinance, a steering committee of the three secretariats, two co-executors and a planning body as intervening party, RACI matrix and managerial-capacity declaration annexed. NT flags that knowledge of lender procurement and safeguard policies was not stated, despite earlier experience with the same lender; borrower has external-credit experience with a mid-range portfolio performance score.

## Perguntas GTEC provaveis
- Q1 Post-project sustainability: PARTIAL (declaration listing existing maintenance, fuel and IT contracts and budget lines, but no projected post-programme cost estimate).
- Q3 Cost per beneficiary: ABSENT (no unit-cost comparison with other lender operations in the sector).
- Q5 Inter-secretariat coordination: PARTIAL (steering committee of the three secretariats and a RACI matrix, but the execution unit is still to be created by ordinance or similar act).
- Q6 Counterpart secured: PARTIAL (counterpart shared among the three secretariats' budgets, programme inserted in the multi-year plan; authorising law pending).
- Q8 Disaggregation for vulnerable groups: PARTIAL (one impact indicator targets at-risk groups; the others are not disaggregated by gender or race).
- NO-MATCH: safeguards and rights-protection protocols for surveillance, predictive-policing and body-camera technology (risks only partially mitigated).
- NO-MATCH: environmental and social risks declared nil without justification.
