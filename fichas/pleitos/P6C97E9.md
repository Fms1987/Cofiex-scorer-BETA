## Resumo
Direct investment operation for a state government (not fund intermediation, despite the header keyword guess); borrower is a state entity with a sovereign guarantee. Finances a multi-theme program combining environmental sanitation (sewage collection/treatment, urban drainage, solid-waste collection) in a cluster of tourism-pole municipalities, physical and management upgrades to several state and federal protected/conservation areas (visitor infrastructure, management plans, environmental-police facilities, community-based tourism and species-conservation actions), and institutional strengthening of state and municipal tourism/environment bodies plus program administration (UGP). Delivery is direct through multiple state secretariats and a water/sanitation state company acting as co-executors, not an on-lending or intermediated structure.
