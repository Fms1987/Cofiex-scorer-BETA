## Resumo
Subnational PPP-based investment operation (direct lending to a state government) financing operational systems (signaling, telecommunications, power supply, station auxiliary systems) for an additional phase of an existing mass-transit concession. Delivery is intermediated through the incumbent private concessionaire under an existing PPP contract; the public sponsor contributes most of the capex while the concessionaire absorbs the remainder via contractual rebalancing. Single structural component (systems) with four products; no consulting/design component, justified as already funded by the sponsor outside this operation. Entity type: state government, with a track record of several prior external-credit operations.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG — explicit causal chain from an unserved mass-transit corridor to a capital-adjacent municipality, through the specific systems to be financed, to ridership/time/emissions outcomes; CC and NT both develop this chain in detail and the technical-design score is near-maximum.
A.2 Results Framework: STRONG (approximate) — VERIFIED n=4 indicators, roughly 1 effectiveness/output (ridership) vs 3 outcome/impact (travel time, emissions, social time gain), all SMART with stated formulas and point targets (no wide ranges, no deferred indicators). Baseline count under this spec's rule: all 4 counted as baseline-defined — two carry an explicit baseline value, and two carry a baseline of zero that the CC explains as a "with-project vs without-project" counterfactual (network without the extension in operation), which qualifies as an explained zero. None of the four states a separate reference year for the baseline itself (only the target year is given), so the year-qualified share is 0. Verification mechanism: present but mixed — one indicator uses direct boarding records, the other three rely on demand/emissions modelling rather than independent verification. No external benchmark is used for the indicators. No internal inconsistencies beyond the target years (2028/2030) sitting beyond this tranche's own disbursement period, which the CC explains as due to behavioural lag and alignment with sector planning horizons (reasonable, not a red flag).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE/STRONG — incremental O&M is quantified (tens of millions BRL/year) and funded through contractual rebalancing of the existing concession (added fare revenue, a tariff increase, and a concession-term extension), with no public-budget impact asserted; the mechanism is embedded in the concession contract itself, giving it legal backing.
A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG — a dedicated risk matrix (annexed) covers legal/institutional risks (pending contract addendum, expropriation decree, authorizing law in process), contracting risk (low, single-contract delegation to the incumbent concessionaire), geotechnical/construction risk, and environmental and social risk (soil/groundwater, air quality, vegetation removal, expropriation impact, temporary service disruption), each with stated mitigations; no resettlement or impact on traditional/indigenous populations is identified.
Counterpart: to be secured — a financial counterpart at the regulatory minimum is committed per the applicable counterpart resolution, but the CC states the specific budget line will only appear in the following fiscal year's budget-law proposal, i.e. not yet enacted at CC stage.
A.4 Implementation (UGP quality): ADEQUATE — an institutional/RACI responsibility matrix is presented and the executing entity is familiar with the lender's procurement/safeguards policies; delivery runs through an already-operating concessionaire with a long history of contract addenda. However, the entity's own track record on prior external-credit execution is rated only "regular" (middle tier) in the NT's institutional-capacity check, moderating an otherwise strong setup.
Environmental licensing: in progress, not yet issued — the preliminary environmental report is under revision following a design change and will be refiled with the environmental agency before the operating licenses are requested.
