## Resumo
- Investment loan (FONPLATA, <50M band) to a small-to-medium municipality for an integrated urban and environmental requalification programme: structuring road/mobility works, drainage and environmental recovery for flood prevention, and revitalisation of heritage and public buildings and spaces.
- Five components: mobility works (roughly 45% of cost), environmental recovery and flood prevention (roughly 30%), revitalisation of public facilities (roughly 20%), a small institutional-strengthening component, and programme management/supervision. Mobility includes paving, accessible sidewalks and cycle infrastructure; drainage uses nature-based solutions.
- Direct execution by the municipal works secretariat with a UGP and several co-executor secretariats (a preparation unit created by decree first); external firm to support UGP and supervision. Counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Terms: 24-month grace, 20-year total term, SOFR-180 plus a fixed margin, with commitment and evaluation fees; the lender's thematic lines (economic reactivation, gender/diversity, green) are mentioned among the charges.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- **A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE.** Problem chain (unplanned growth, soil sealing, occupation of floodplains, degraded protected areas, deteriorated heritage) is mapped to three axes and to specific objectives; assumptions and risks to causality are not explicit.
- **A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE.** 7 indicators, approximately 5 outcome-type (paving coverage, travel time, drainage coverage, flood-prone area reduction, vegetation cover) plus a perception indicator, and 1 output-type (share of staff trained). Each has formula, unit, baseline, reference year, periodicity and a stated verification source (works reports, drainage cadastre, civil-defence mapping, satellite imagery, surveys, HR/training records); mechanism present.
- Baselines: 7 of 7 with value and year (2025). One zero baseline (visitor satisfaction) counted as defined because the CC explains it (no systematised data exists). baseline_defined_share = 1.0; with-year share = 1.0.
- Weaknesses: the flood-prone-area indicator is declared in % but its baseline is expressed as an area, and the target is a relative reduction; travel-time indicator relies on navigation apps and a simple average over a few corridors; vegetation baseline taken from satellite imagery; the training indicator has a very low baseline. The NT says some indicators do not fully follow SMART.
- Each indicator is mapped to several PPA strategic areas (including some loosely related ones); no external benchmark index used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- **A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE.** O&M assigned to the municipal maintenance secretariat with preventive/corrective schedules; funding via municipal own resources through named budget actions for road recovery, squares/gardens upkeep and property maintenance, and a maintenance-cost annex; the NT calls the cost estimates compatible with the foreseen appropriation. Community co-management agreements for squares and cultural facilities are mentioned. Amounts not verified here.
- **A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE.** Matrix of 5 risk categories (regulatory/institutional, environmental, expropriation/compensation, contractual/works execution, social) with impact/probability and mitigation; NT rates risk management as comprehensive. Environmental licensing is required for most works and executive designs do not yet exist; expropriation is foreseen with quantities to be fixed after executive design, and the CC says no resettlement is planned, only compensation.
- Counterpart: municipal PPA, LDO (draft) and LOA are cited for alignment; the programme is to be detailed as budget items in the LOA, i.e. specific counterpart line "to be secured" (the CC says inclusion depends on an own appropriation). Fiscal capacity cited (CAPAG B per CC).
- **A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE.** Works secretariat as executor; UGP with dedicated full-time team (coordinators for bidding, administration-finance, plus engineering, legal, socio-environmental support); preparation unit by decree during the preparatory phase; external supervision firm; NT notes a succinct but present responsibility matrix.
