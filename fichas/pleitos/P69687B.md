## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / credit-line operation (IBRD, sovereign-guaranteed) to a federal public bank that would relend to municipalities and companies for low-carbon energy transition; the bank is both borrower and executor.
- Financed (generic): electric-mobility vehicles and supporting infrastructure (roughly 40% of cost), renewable generation and energy-efficiency solutions (roughly 30%), sustainable urban infrastructure incl. efficient public lighting (roughly 20%), plus lender-backed technical assistance (roughly 10%).
- Delivery model: intermediated; sub-borrowers include public entities, private firms and PPP special-purpose companies, selected on demand by the bank's credit-risk and eligibility rules; pipeline not identified at CC stage. Nationwide scope, three-year disbursement period, no counterpart.
- Terms: 15-year maturity, 3-year grace, floating reference rate plus a spread below 1%, upfront and commitment fees of 0.25%; a local-currency (inflation-indexed) tranche is considered as a natural hedge.
