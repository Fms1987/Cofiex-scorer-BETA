## Resumo
- Modality: multi-sector investment loan with Union guarantee to a capital municipality, co-financed by two lenders (100-200M USD band), counterpart at the 20% minimum, 5-year disbursement; lenders split by component (one funds drainage/sewerage and management, the other roads, housing and social infrastructure).
- Finances an integrated urban programme: pumping-station upgrades with macro-drainage and sewer collection (about 45% of cost), road widening with cycle paths and public-transport terminal refurbishment (about 30%), social housing and land regularisation, community and commercial spaces, cultural/sports facilities, a mental-health and addiction facility, an economic-inclusion hub, and programme management (about 2%).
- Delivery: direct execution by the municipality through a UGP to be created by administrative act, attached to the strategic-planning secretariat, with line secretariats and municipal utilities responsible for components; supervision and project-management consultancies.
- Third version of the CC; second attempt.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: WEAK. Rich diagnosis and a list of expected results tied to SDGs, but the programme reads as a bundle of loosely connected works (NT: "little connection between them"); no explicit causal chain or assumptions across drainage, mobility, housing, health and culture.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 14 indicators (verified from the CC table; the NT text says 12 while its own table lists 14). Approximate split: 3 outcome-type (flood-complaint reduction, women/youth participation share, jobs/occupations maintained) and 11 output/beneficiary counts, of which 2 are execution-management indicators (procurement on time, disbursement deviation) that the NT says fall outside the scope of the products.
- Baselines: a value is stated for all 14 but only 5 count as defined (share 0.36): complaints, bed-expansion, occupations, procurement and disbursement-deviation baselines. The 9 zero baselines (beneficiary counts, new network length, housing, regularisation, training places, commercial space, revitalised area) are not explained as new assets in the CC, so counted as undefined. No reference year or period anywhere (year share 0.0).
- Verification: formulas and units given; only the complaint indicator names a data source (municipal call centre and agencies). Internal inconsistency: housing beneficiary population claimed is far above the housing-unit target.
- Benchmarks: none used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. The CC only names the municipal secretariats and utility responsible for maintaining each asset class; the NT notes it does not explain how maintenance will be done or its financial limits. No O&M quantification, funding source or legal backing for the new health and social facilities.
- Counterpart (20%): stated to comply with Res. 3/2019, may include land; the authorizing bill for the external credit is still being drafted, to be sent to the council only after COFIEX approval (to be secured). PPA alignment is detailed (PPA axes, participatory budgeting, sector plans); one sector plan was pending council approval.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE as coverage. Risks and mitigations for each of legal acts, contracting, expropriation, environment, social and resettlement, including approval risk under the guarantee limit; generic mitigations, little component-specific licensing status (licences described as routine). Resettlement and expropriation are possible for the road widening, with a rule that works start only after possession is secured.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP to be created, with general, technical, procurement and fiduciary coordinations plus consultancies; municipality has a long record of external loans. NT: complex co-financing and heterogeneous scope, municipality did not show an operating structure fit for it (to be assessed in preparation); lender disbursement terms differ (extension requested).
