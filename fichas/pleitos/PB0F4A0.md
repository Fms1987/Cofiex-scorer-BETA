## Resumo
- Investment-type public-management modernisation loan (IBRD, 100-200M USD band, counterpart roughly 9% of cost, 12-year term with 2-year grace, floating SOFR plus a variable margin, indicative terms with possible currency options) to a state government with a recent fiscal recovery trajectory; executed directly by the state finance/economy secretariat through a project management unit and implementation units in partner bodies.
- Three components (about 90% of cost in the transversal component): (1) fiscal, financial and asset management across government, covering active and retired payroll, a pension-system census and audit tools, procurement with AI and e-marketplace, planning/budget/investment-pipeline systems, internal audit, property and records management, and a data lake with cloud cost control; (2) a financial-management system for the education sector (school meals stock and decentralised funds), roughly 7% of cost; (3) project and change management.
- Twelve products and seven subcomponents; the programme claims alignment with many federal strategic areas although it is essentially a fiscal/public-management and digital-government operation.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problems (fragmented personnel systems, pension data gaps, manual procurement, weak asset and records control, manual school-meal stock control) are mapped to products and then to indicators, with a wave-based logic (enablers first, then value levers); assumptions and the link from system delivery to fiscal outcome are asserted rather than tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 11 indicators with formula, unit, frequency, polarity, data source, responsible unit and annual targets over five years; approximately 7 are outcome or efficiency measures (payroll growth, procurement lead time, spend growth, savings, execution rate, property net balance, food-waste reduction) and about 4 are adoption/output measures (contract-oversight automation, AI integration, data sources integrated, digitised records).
- Baselines: all 11 have a stated value and a reference year (several given as a 2023/2024 period). Three zero baselines are counted as defined: two are reduction-versus-base-period measures where zero is the starting point by formula, and one is a digitisation share where the CC says only a proof of concept exists.
- Weaknesses: formulas and polarity are inconsistent for some indicators (a "growth" measure defined as 1 minus a ratio; a "reduction" measure with higher-is-better polarity; a deflator applied as one minus inflation); one indicator has no unit for its baseline; several data sources are the executing agencies themselves or a survey of agencies, i.e. no independent verification; targets are year-5 point values with annual milestones.
- Benchmarks: none external (no peer or international comparators for the efficiency gains).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. A sustainability plan is promised for the preparation phase; maintenance of new systems is financed by the loan during execution and recurring costs are estimated as up to about a fifth of implementation cost per year, to move to the annual state budget afterwards. No annual cost table by category and no budget line or legal commitment yet.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Six risks with impact, probability and concrete mitigations (uneven capacity across units, procurement planning delays, authorising-law timing, residual environmental/social, institutional adaptation, political-cycle discontinuity); no explicit risk on cybersecurity or data protection despite the AI and data-lake scope; environmental risk rated low as there are no civil works.
- Counterpart: to be secured (authorising state law not yet approved at filing; the executive was preparing the bill; counter-guarantees from constitutional revenue shares).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Central finance secretariat with prior experience of a multilateral fiscal-modernisation programme, a three-tier governance (strategic, project management unit, implementation units) with an advisory council, a central special procurement committee and a responsibility matrix annexed; lender capacity and fiduciary assessments still to be carried out. Preparatory actions are advanced only for document management and, partly, the pension census; for the other products none are formalised.
