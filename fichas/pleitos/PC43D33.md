## Resumo
- Investment loan, directly executed by a capital-region metropolitan municipality (direct delivery model, own program-coordination unit).
- Finances an integrated urban-upgrading program in two distinct zones: an informal-settlement complex (land-tenure regularization, housing upgrading/new construction, basic sanitation, drainage, slope stabilization, local roads) and a historic-center requalification (active-mobility corridor, plazas, linear park, a mobility-as-a-service pilot).
- Main components: (i) urban & environmental upgrading — the large majority of financed cost, covering the informal-settlement works/studies, a new shared institutional headquarters, an active-mobility plan, works supervision and participatory social-technical work, plus historic-center works/studies; (ii) socio-environmental sustainability, institutional strengthening and technological innovation; (iii) administration, audit, evaluation and monitoring.
- Entity type: capital-region metropolitan municipality with prior experience executing an externally financed mobility-system program with a different lender.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: STRONG — each zone's diagnosis, proposed solution, expected result and indicator number are explicitly cross-referenced in the objectives table, and the evaluator scored the design/coherence sub-criteria near-maximally in both reviewed versions.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK — 11 verified indicators, but only 5/11 (0.45) have a real stated baseline value with a reference year; the other 6 use "0" only as the SIGS system's placeholder for a blank cell ("ainda não foi executado"), which is an unexplained non-baseline rather than a justified counterfactual zero, so they were counted as undefined. Indicators are measurable with explicit calculation formulas and most rely on an end-of-program household survey as the stated verification mechanism.
- Outcome-vs-output split: approximate majority outcome/perception-type (satisfaction, property-valuation, tenure-regularization and active-mobility-completion rates; a water-quality index; a green-building-certification compliance rate), with few pure output counts.
- Benchmarks used: a national water-quality-index methodology (already monitored since a prior year and flagged as poor) and a green-building eco-efficiency certification standard for the new housing units.
- Internal inconsistency flagged: the six zero-baseline indicators are explicitly described as a system artifact standing in for a blank field, not a true "new asset" counterfactual, which keeps the baseline-defined share well below the share needed for a strong results-framework rating despite otherwise coherent indicator design.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: in the final (approved) version, O&M costs and the responsible municipal department/funding source are detailed per facility/network (roads, plazas/parks, public buildings, cycling/walking network, social-inclusion observatory); this is a material improvement over the withdrawn attempt, where the evaluator found the same topic treated only superficially with no specific costing or dedicated instruments.
- Counterpart (20%, matches CC): sourced from the municipal treasury against cited constitutional tax revenues; legislatively PARTIAL only — the authorizing borrowing law is to be submitted to the municipal council only after COFIEX approval, i.e. projected rather than already enacted.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: a risk matrix is present. Legal/institutional risk is acknowledged (authorizing-law approval pending). Procurement risk includes acknowledged inexperience of municipal technical staff with the lender's own bidding procedures, mitigated by lender-provided capacity-building. Resettlement risk: several hundred households are expected to be resettled, housed on municipally owned land, with compensation following the lender's criteria, monitored by the program unit and backed by an existing municipal law on the matter. Environmental/social risk covers construction-phase pollution (soil/air/water), mitigated by conventional measures; the proponent proactively requested a global prior environmental license for both intervention zones to avoid licensing delays.
- A.4 Implementation: a dedicated program-coordination unit under the municipal infrastructure/urban-works secretariat, with a formal responsibility (RACI) matrix; the executing agency has prior experience with a different lender on a mobility-system program, but its portfolio-performance rating was scored only "regular" (mid-tier, not high) in both reviewed attempts.
