## Resumo
- Modality: multi-sector investment programme financed by CAF for a mid-sized municipality (no PPP or intermediation), counterpart at the 20% minimum, federal guarantee.
- Finances an environmental and urban package: a large municipal ecological park and green-area expansion, requalification of an urban drainage canal against recurrent flooding, road/sidewalk/cycleway and accessibility upgrades, bridges and footbridges, plus capacity building, microentrepreneur support and gender-protection services.
- Delivery model: direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat through a programme management unit (UGP), with environment, economic development and social secretariats responsible for their actions.
- Four components (environment and green infrastructure; urban mobility; productive inclusion and gender; management, supervision and financial charges). Disbursement period of 5 years.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problems are organised by thematic axis (green-area deficit and canal degradation, vulnerability and gender violence, informality, mobility) with a matched solution and specific objective each; no explicit assumptions or causal chain beyond that mapping.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified 12 indicators; approximately a third are outcome-type (green area per capita, average travel time, jobs, park visitation) and the rest outputs (kilometres built, people trained, entrepreneurs or women served). Every indicator states a baseline value with a reference year (shares 1.0 / 1.0). Counted zero baselines: five indicators (training of technicians, canal length, gender-service training, jobs, park visits), each with an explicit justification (new initiative or asset not yet built). One baseline is given as an upper bound (below a threshold).
- Verification: present for all (geoprocessing, topographic measurement, attendance lists and certificates, administrative records, ticketing after opening); each has a short SMART rationale.
- Weaknesses: park visitation is a demand projection with no empirical baseline; employment combines direct and estimated indirect jobs; the federal-strategy mapping for several areas is thin relative to indicator relevance.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M table by item (road maintenance, bridges, park management, canal green areas, training/entrepreneurship, social/gender programmes, programme administration) with funding source per line: mainly the municipal budget, with ancillary park revenues and external agreements for some lines. Assets are to be incorporated into municipal maintenance plans and LOA/PPA/LDO instruments; no legal backing yet shown.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Seven risks with impact/probability and responsible bodies: licensing/regulatory, environmental liabilities, expropriation and compensation, procurement under the new procurement law, social resistance, exchange rate and counterpart, climate. Matrix is complete but generic. Environmental licensing is treated as a risk with a mitigation plan rather than a status per component.
- Counterpart: to be secured. The multi-year plan and the annual budget bills were still under review by the municipal legislature when submitted, so legal backing was pending.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP linked to the infrastructure secretariat plus several secretariats and control bodies; staff profiles described, no track record with multilateral-funded projects shown.
