## Resumo
- Modality: policy-based, results-conditioned loan (PBL-type) from IDB to the federal government, two tranches, no counterpart (exempted because disbursement is conditioned on policy targets); disbursed directly to the Treasury with no earmarking to specific spending. Long grace period (5.5 years), 25.5-year total term, SOFR-based rate with a commitment fee.
- Supports federal gender-equality and racial-equality policy reforms through a policy matrix of prior actions (pay equity, care policy, femicide prevention and violence data systems, affirmative action and employability programmes, territorial titling for traditional communities, strengthening of the national racial-equality system).
- Two components of equal weight (racial equality; gender equality), each with three subcomponents (rights and violence, economic autonomy/education, institutional capacity); two federal ministries execute, with a third in support under a joint steering committee.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem, causes, consequences and needs are laid out, and each objective maps to policy actions in the matrix; assumptions are not stated as testable and the link between policy measures and national outcome indicators is asserted, not demonstrated.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified 8 indicators, all outcome-level (approximate judgement), mostly the national key indicators of the relevant PPA strategic objectives (violence, pay-ratio, racial gaps, higher-education access, territorial recognition, youth vulnerability index).
- Baselines: 8 of 8 stated (1.0) with reference years (2021-2024); the composite youth-vulnerability baseline is given by state in an external table. Targets are numeric, aligned to PPA key-indicator targets, mostly 2027.
- Verification: named official sources (national household survey, education statistics, public-safety and ministry records). Weakness: national outcome indicators are far from what the loan-financed policy actions alone can move (attribution); output-level or policy-trigger indicators are absent from the indicator list because they sit in the matrix.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE/WEAK. No O&M quantification; continuity rests on incorporating improved actions into the executing ministries' future budget cycles and on IDB technical-cooperation grants. Policy actions are already in the ministries' budgets (no new transfers).
- Counterpart: none required (results-conditioned disbursement exemption); matrix actions are said to be already budgeted.
- A.3 Risks: 7 risks with impact/probability and owners (inter-ministry coordination, delays and low uptake by subnational governments, environmental, political resistance, legislative dependence, budget restrictions); several rated high impact and high probability. Environmental risk item looks generic for a policy loan.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Joint steering committee with parity between the two lead ministries and a focal point per ministry, supported by a consultant; no UGP; the portal allowed only one executing entity so roles are explained in text.
