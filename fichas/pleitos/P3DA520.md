## Resumo
- Investment programme financed by NDB for a capital municipality; counterpart at the 20% minimum; coordinated by the municipal works secretariat with a programme coordination unit, other municipal bodies executing works, housing and mobility.
- Structural requalification of a major urban arterial corridor: road-safety works, new accesses, ramps and pedestrian crossings that reduce the barrier effect, plus resettlement and new housing for families in risk areas along the corridor.
- Three components in the text: (1) road infrastructure (roughly 80% of cost), (2) resettlement and housing (roughly 20%), (3) institutional strengthening (about 1%); a women-in-construction training product refers to a fourth component that is not listed.
- Municipal operator of the corridor retains O&M; a 5-year execution period is stated.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. Five problems each map to a solution, a component, a result, a specific objective and an indicator, with strategic-area links; assumptions are not explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 5 indicators (verified count), approximately 3 outcome-type (fatal crashes, average BRT speed, share of women employed on works) and approximately 2 output-type (new road connections, resettled families). Each has data source and formula; verification relies on police records, works supervision reports, resettlement registers, vehicle telemetry and contractor payroll.
- Baselines: all 5 state a value with a year (100% defined, 100% with year). The two zero baselines (new connections, resettled families) are counted as defined because the assets and resettlements are new in the programme.
- Benchmarks: road-safety target framed against the global decade-of-action goal; no other external benchmark.
- Inconsistencies: the safety indicator is titled as fatal crashes but its baseline counts all crashes at the intervention points and its target refers to crashes with victims; the gender indicator cites an objective number that does not exist among the five.
