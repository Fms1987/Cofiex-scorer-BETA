## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan co-financed by two multilateral lenders (FONPLATA and a development fund), direct execution by a mid-sized municipality, counterpart at the 20% minimum, 60-month execution.
- Finances integrated urban requalification of vulnerable peripheral areas: complete-street road works with sustainable drainage and active mobility, bridges (including completion of a work already under way) with access roads, a civil-defence command and operations centre, and primary-health units paired with outdoor sport and leisure areas.
- Five components: preparation studies/designs and licensing (small share), urban infrastructure plus disaster-risk management (roughly 80% of cost), primary health (roughly 10%), expropriation and land-contribution management, and programme management (supervision, audit, UGP support).
- Executing entity: the municipal planning and administration secretariat through an existing project coordination unit (UCP), with line secretariats executing.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree (fragmented territory, precarious infrastructure, vulnerability to floods, low primary-care coverage) leads to objectives and components, with intended impacts listed per component and each indicator tied to a specific objective; no explicit assumptions or risks-to-outcome logic.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 13 indicators (verified); roughly 8 outcome-type and 5 output/process-type (approximate judgement). Each indicator carries a SMART self-assessment, source and target; several name the data owner.
- Benchmarks used: national PPA key indicators (an adequate-sanitation index and a water-quality index), a street-design concept, an origin-destination survey, and the municipal health plan.
- Baselines: all 13 stated (share 1.0). Reference year is missing for 3 (humanitarian storage capacity, risk-perception training, camera integration), so the with-year share is about 0.77. Zero baselines counted as defined: storage capacity, camera integration and satisfaction survey, all for new facilities or services that do not exist yet. The travel-time indicator uses one origin-destination pair; the flow-count baseline points to figures not legible in the text (counted as defined with doubt).
- Weaknesses: the sports-area indicator is named as a 20% increase while the stated baseline and target imply a larger increase; the emergency-response target is a very large cut within two years; the flow-count target is a vague "keep above" threshold; indicators for the civil-defence component have thin verification (internal records of the same secretariat).
- Verification mechanism present for most (municipal secretariats, water utility monthly sampling, health system data); weakest for flow counts and land/economic-use indicator.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M is estimated per asset type (public areas and urban infrastructure small; command centre moderate with a staffing/IT breakdown; primary-health network operation large but already in the health secretariat budget, financed by own, state and federal health transfers). The CC says LDO/LOA lines will be created for future maintenance increases; street lighting O&M sits in an existing PPP contract.
- Counterpart: 20% of cost; own resources cover expropriation compensation and municipally owned land is contributed in kind; the CC cites the municipal PPA/LOA annex as showing compatible budget lines, but the authorizing law is still to be enacted (listed as a risk).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE (leaning strong). Matrix with 9 risks (impact, probability, detailed mitigation): slow procurement, deserted/contested tenders, contractor default, new lender procurement policies (first operation with one of the two lenders), delay of the authorizing law, possible UCP dissolution, expropriation, social disruption during works (high impact, high probability), and environmental licensing (high impact, medium probability; dedicated licensing study product anticipated, state licensing agency for the main structuring road).
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Existing UCP created by decree with long-tenured staff, experience with FONPLATA and IDB operations, supervision consultants and external audit funded in a programme-management component. Many secretariats involved but no inter-secretariat transfers.
