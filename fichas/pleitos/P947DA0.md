## Resumo
- Modality: framed as a development policy loan (DPL) from IBRD to a state government, with no counterpart; yet the CC lists a physical-works component structure, so the modality and the investment-style cost table sit uneasily together.
- Objective: strengthen the state's structuring programmes in logistics, mobility, inclusive ecological transition and public-sector management.
- Component 1 (largest, roughly 80% of cost): resilient, safe, low-carbon mobility (road and rail-based transit upgrades, active mobility, safety infrastructure). Component 2 (roughly 15%): inclusive ecological transition (decarbonisation studies, rural electrification, farm-family registry for biofuel value chains, renewable energy in public buildings). Component 3: small project management / institutional strengthening line.
- Delivery: direct execution by several state secretariats under a central planning secretariat with a project management unit; disbursement planned over 2 years.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear diagnosis (inadequate transport infrastructure, climate exposure, road fatalities, energy transition) linked to solutions and expected results; no prior-action or policy-trigger logic and no explicit assumptions despite DPL framing.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Verified count: 8 indicators; about 5 outcome-type (road deaths, travel time, EV registration share, rural three-phase access, renewable share in public buildings) and about 3 closer to output/policy (climate-risk clauses in road contracts, private firms starting hydrogen licensing, farm-family registry).
- Baselines: only 3 of 8 have a stated non-zero baseline value (road deaths, travel time, private green-hydrogen licensing); 5 are 0 without explanation (policy-clause share, EV share, rural access, registry coverage, renewable buildings) and were not counted as defined. None states a reference year, so year share is 0.
- Indicator targets are partly ambitious (e.g. halving road deaths); no deadlines or verification sources per indicator; NT also says some indicators capture deliveries rather than community impact.
- Internal inconsistency: two indicators share overlapping formulas (rural access and farm-family registry both use three-phase connection logic).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Relies on the state's own revenue and hoped-for private investment; no O&M or replacement cost estimates and no specific instruments (NT: gap).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Legal acts pending (low risk claimed because they depend on the governor's initiative, with a deadline in the year of submission); expropriation/resettlement risk recognised (encroachment on road right-of-way) but without mitigation detail; climate and social-disruption risks named without specific actions; contracting risk deemed nil for now. Licensing status of works not stated.
- Counterpart: none, as a DPL; no LOA/PPA counterpart issue, though project is aligned with the state PPA and a long-term development plan.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Multi-secretariat arrangement with a project management unit for fiduciary tasks, but no staff composition or experience and no evidence of knowledge of lender procurement/safeguard policies. Borrower has external-credit experience with a regular portfolio performance rating.
- Preparation status: first-phase components "in preparation"; completion of studies, designs and licences not confirmed, which clashes with the short disbursement window.
