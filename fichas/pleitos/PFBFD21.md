## Resumo
- Investment loan from IBRD to a state government (direct administration, Union guarantee requested), counterpart at the 20% minimum, five-year disbursement; ticket in the lowest band.
- Public-sector digital transformation in three axes plus programme management (4 components): critical digital infrastructure (expansion of a state-owned research/education backbone and metropolitan ring, connectivity equipment, a revised model for contracting carrier services, data-centre modernisation with hybrid cloud); a citizen digital-services platform (single sign-on, interoperability, health-record integration, telehealth, digital skills, technology-based pedagogy); and a digital-governance strategy (executive project office, cyber-incident response unit, data platform).
- Delivery is direct through a programme unit to be created inside a communications secretariat with the state ICT agency; cost mix roughly 60% goods, 30% non-consulting services, remainder consultancy and a very small works share.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (weak connectivity, exhausted on-premise data centre, non-interoperable systems, low digital-service uptake, a recent serious cyber incident) maps cleanly to the three axes and expected results; assumptions (adoption, savings from the new carrier-contracting model) are asserted, not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 7 indicators verified; all are output-type counts or coverage (approximate judgement): no outcome indicator on service uptake, user satisfaction, cost savings or digital inclusion, although those are stated objectives.
- Baselines: 4 non-zero values stated; 3 zero baselines (health-record interoperability, project office portfolio, incident-response unit) counted as defined because they are new assets or an explicitly absent capability. No reference year is stated for any baseline or target year in the table, so year share is zero.
- Verification mechanism ABSENT (no source, frequency or owner per indicator). Internal inconsistencies: target for connected municipalities differs from the expansion described in the component text; target for interconnected health facilities is below the facility count in the diagnosis; the existing digital-service stock is unexplained (NT points out the latter two).
- Benchmarks: external studies cited on digital-service unit-cost reduction and national ranking positions in the diagnosis, not used as targets.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. Relies on state budget plus assumed savings from digital services and a cheaper carrier-contracting model; refers to an existing dispersed ICT budget but gives no OPEX table (cloud, licences, connectivity, staff), no funding line and no legal instrument. NT flags absence of maintenance-cost projections.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Procurement and contract-management risks listed with mitigations (training in the lender procurement rules, clear TORs, standard processes); environmental risk for the network civil works called low, with the state environmental agency as licensor; social and safeguard assessment deferred to preparation; cyber/data-protection risk mitigated by the new incident unit and security policy; no matrix; no expropriation or resettlement (existing ducts).
- Counterpart: to be secured; the planning secretariat is to provision it in the budget, no LOA line cited; the authorizing law must be amended before contracting; current PPA programmes cited, new PPA under preparation.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE on paper, WEAK on track record. Programme unit to be created in a secretariat that has not itself executed MDB-financed programmes; project office and an ICT team described but not evidenced; relies on lender training; the NT places the state portfolio performance in the unsatisfactory band.
