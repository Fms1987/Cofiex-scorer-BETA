## Resumo
- Modality: PBL/DPL-type policy-based debt restructuring (refinancing), IBRD lender, full federal guarantee, no counterpart (waived as a debt restructuring operation).
- Finances early repayment of a pair of domestic, federally guaranteed loans held with a state-owned commercial bank; proceeds go to debt liquidation, not to physical works.
- Delivery model: direct, single component; the municipal finance secretariat is the sole executor, with a few partner secretariats for the policy measures.
- Conditioned on a two-pillar policy matrix (fiscal and environmental legal frameworks); the environmental pillar is the non-financial counterpart agreed with the lender. Fiscal space freed is meant to fund bioeconomy, urban sustainability and PPP pipeline.
- Entity: a capital municipality with CAPAG B.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Causal chain is short and credible for a refinancing (lower debt service and cost, freed fiscal space, then investment), with the policy pillars (fiscal responsibility law, fiscal council, ecological tax incentives) linking to the lender matrix; assumptions on how freed space is actually reinvested are not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified 5 indicators, approximately 2 financial outcomes (debt-service flow and debt cost via NPV), approximately 3 policy/environmental outcomes tied to state legislation.
- Baselines: only the startup-registration indicator states a baseline value with a reference month; the debt indicators compare against existing contracts without a stated baseline value, and the two environmental-fiscal indicators have no baseline (counted as undefined). Baseline share 1 of 5, all with a reference period.
- Verification mechanism: not specified beyond the cash-flow annex; two indicators depend on criteria still to be set by the state government, so targets are partly deferred.
- Targets mix absolute local-currency savings with percentages of negotiated debt, and the savings target is stated both gross and net of prepayment penalties. No external benchmarks used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for a debt operation. No O&M/OPEX (no assets); sustainability is the debt profile itself: longer amortization with no grace, SOFR-indexed floating rate plus a spread, front-fee and commitment fee. Savings are front-loaded and partly offset by higher payments in later years due to the longer term.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Four risks listed (contracting rules, enabling municipal law, early-repayment negotiation, exchange rate), each with short mitigation; no sector or policy-implementation risk (state criteria not yet defined) and no licensing treatment.
- Counterpart: none required (restructuring waiver, Art. 36); enabling law still to be approved by the municipal legislature and flagged by the CC itself as a high-impact risk; debt-limit exceptionality invoked under the Senate resolution, with no PFE plan inclusion needed.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Single finance secretariat executor with RACI matrix annexed; no consultants or new staff needed.
- Bank no-objection file: none attached.
