## Resumo
- Investment programme by a capital municipality, financed by CAF, competing for the environmental/climate sublimit; counterpart at the 20% minimum, roughly five-year disbursement, 60-month grace.
- Finances urban climate resilience: green infrastructure and urban parks, an environmental complex, restoration of degraded areas and water-resource conservation, nature-based solutions, urban tree planting.
- Also covers environmental risk monitoring and alert systems, solid-waste management and circular economy (waste-picker cooperatives, sorting centres), studies, plans and environmental training.
- Six components (the sixth is programme management); direct execution by the municipal environment foundation through a new UGP, with partner secretariats supporting but receiving no transfers.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem tree (heat islands, loss of green cover, water degradation/scarcity) maps to components and objectives, but assumptions are not explicit or testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Six indicators verified (approximate split: three outcome-type, three output/process-type); each has a SMART narrative, formula and a data-collection method (satellite vegetation indices, field tree inventory, technical inspections, cooperative records).
- Baselines: all six have a stated value (6/6), all with a reference year or period (6/6). Zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains them (no local risk plans, no monitored micro-basins, no existing training programme).
- Inconsistencies: the shading indicator's SMART text describes counting seedlings planted rather than canopy share; the recyclables indicator cites two different baseline quantities (formula vs. temporal text); the restored-areas indicator gives its target as a share of a 2023 survey.
- Benchmarks: PPA objective alignment per indicator; no international benchmark. Targets are mostly end-of-programme with annual monitoring.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG-to-ADEQUATE. Annual O&M is quantified by asset class (parks and environmental complex, squares, nature-based solutions, tree planting, monitoring system, sorting centre, construction-waste site) with named sources: own revenues, environmental licensing compensation, converted environmental fines, municipal budget, environmental funds. A financial sustainability plan for parks is to be prepared; revenue streams are only listed, not committed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: matrix with impact/probability and mitigation (political discontinuity across a mandate change, low social participation, low use/vandalism of revitalised areas, others). Licensing is mentioned but not a central risk treatment.
- Counterpart: 20% offered by the municipality; budget alignment cited through the municipal PPA/LDO/LOA, secured-status not detailed.
- A.4 Implementation: experienced municipal UGP from an ongoing CAF loan to be adapted, with a new dedicated UGP structure, technical staff profiles and a RACI matrix; technical and popular commissions to be created by decree.
