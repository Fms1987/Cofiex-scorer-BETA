## Resumo
Investment-modality external credit operation with a capital-municipality government as direct borrower and executor, financed by a multilateral bank (NDB) with contrapartida at the 20% minimum band. Finances urban drainage infrastructure (macro- and micro-drainage works, flood gates, pumping stations), nature-based/sustainable drainage measures, a land-acquisition/resettlement-compensation component, community-capacity and environmental-education programs, a territorial-information and environmental-monitoring system, and program management including external audit. Delivery model is direct municipal execution through a dedicated program-management unit tied to the urban-development department.
