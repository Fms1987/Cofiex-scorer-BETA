## Resumo
- Modality: policy-based loan (PBL/DPL type) from IBRD, single disbursement after agreed policy actions, with the whole amount used to refinance existing federally guaranteed domestic bank debt of a state government (restructuring, no new resources, no grace period, floating-rate SOFR-based pricing).
- Two pillars: fiscal (new fiscal regime with a spending ceiling on current primary expenditure, fiscal management council, debt-profile improvement) and environmental (faster environmental licensing rules, rural environmental registry validation and a payment-for-environmental-services market, forest protection law for a dry-forest biome, state climate policy).
- Delivery: direct, borrower is a state government; fiscal secretariat leads the fiscal pillar and the environmental agency the environmental pillar; legislature and attorney-general office involved indirectly.
- Counterpart not required (debt restructuring exemption); operation does not consume the external-financing limit under the Res. 17/2021 debt-restructuring rule.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear diagnosis (rising debt service and personnel spending, costly CDI-indexed short-tenor debt) leading to refinancing plus legal reforms; assumptions (legislative approval, enforcement of new laws) are only partly made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE/WEAK. 5 indicators (verified): about 2 fiscal/debt-cost, 3 environmental; roughly 3 are outcome-type and 2 are closer to process/output (approximate judgment). All 5 state a baseline value (baseline_defined_share 1.0), but none states a reference year or period (baseline_with_year_share 0.0).
- Two indicators (debt-cost index, registry-analysis index) have formulas/targets that are hard to interpret; the registry baseline is near zero without explanation but is a stated nonzero value. No verification mechanism or data source named for any indicator; no benchmark used.
- Components: 4 components, only the debt-restructuring one carries resources; the others are policy deliverables (laws) with zero cost, so the financed product is a contract signature rather than a result.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. The restructuring itself improves debt-service profile (estimated present-value saving of roughly 15% of restructured stock, net of prepayment penalties; debt service relief in early years, heavier later because of lengthening). O&M/OPEX of new legal/institutional arrangements not quantified; NT says no long-term sustainability measures indicated for the new operating costs (sustainability score about 0.09/0.30).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Legal risk (authorizing law and reform laws) rated low with drafts ready and legal review by the state attorney office as mitigation; environmental risk is limited to weak enforcement after the laws are enacted, with no mitigation; social impacts not addressed despite a request in the prior revision. No matrix. Licensing is itself a reform topic, not a project risk.
- Counterpart: not required (debt restructuring exemption); budget alignment argued via debt service already in LOA/LDO/PPA, restructuring not added to the PPA.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Two lead executing bodies (fiscal secretariat, environmental agency) with a responsibility matrix; no UGP; institutional capacity not described (NT: institutional-arrangement score about 0.21/0.40). Borrower has prior external credit experience.
