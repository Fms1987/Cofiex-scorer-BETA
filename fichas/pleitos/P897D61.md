## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from a German commercial bank, denominated in EUR, to a mid-sized interior municipality (second-tier regional hub); counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances renovation and expansion of an existing municipal science museum: technological replacement of the flagship immersive exhibition equipment, structural refurbishment with accessibility and solar/rainwater systems, new hands-on science centre and outdoor science plaza, plus teacher training and visit-management systems.
- Three components (physical expansion and equipment dominating cost; institutional strengthening; programme management). Direct execution by a municipal foundation that manages the museum, with a UGP to be created by decree, co-executing secretariats (education, planning, finance, works) and a supervising firm to be hired.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem (obsolete projection equipment, limited space, collapsing visitation) leads to modernised facilities, more and more inclusive visits, and a hoped-for effect on school performance; the leap from museum visits to measurable learning gains is asserted rather than evidenced.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE leaning WEAK on the education outcome. Verified count: 7 indicators; approximately 2 outcome-type (school-quality index change for primary and secondary schools) and 5 output/access/operational (visitor growth, vulnerable-public share, out-of-town visitors, partnership agreements, utility-cost reduction).
- Verification: named per indicator (national education statistics institute for IDEB; museum entry and booking records; utility bills; signed instruments); administrative, no independent evaluation.
- Baselines: counted as defined for visitors, scheduled vulnerable public, out-of-town visitors, utility cost (all with a year) and partnership agreements (zero, explained: the museum has no agreements today; year 2025). The two IDEB indicators have no baseline value (to be set from whichever schools participate each cycle) and are counted undefined, though a 2023 reference year is cited.
- Weaknesses: IDEB targets of +15% in the second investment year are implausible given the biennial index and attribution to non-formal visits; the vulnerable-public indicator mixes a share and a growth rate with a target near 70% from a baseline in the 40s; consolidated visitor target stated inconsistently (233% vs 230%); recent visitor series is volatile.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Recurrent costs (staff, outsourced services, consumables, utilities) itemised in an annex cost sheet; funding from municipal Treasury transfers to the foundation, said to be already provided in PPA, LDO and LOA; extra staff to be hired from the municipal budget; secondary revenue (space rental, grants, partnerships) is speculative. Legal backing is the municipal foundation statute and budget laws.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Five risks with impact/probability: authorising law and fiscal compliance (CAPAG B stated), procurement (rated high impact, medium probability), delayed imported equipment, environmental and social. Procurement depends on direct (non-competitive) contracting of specialised imported equipment under the lender arrangement; licensing treated generically ("eventual").
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum; the CC does not clearly show its budget source or LOA line; the authorising law draft is ready but awaits COFIEX approval.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE to WEAK. Small foundation staff, UGP to be created by decree with only a few coordinator roles, heavy reliance on a hired supervision firm; prior experience with one MDB loan and another in contracting.
