## Resumo
Investment operation for a direct subnational (municipal) borrower, financed by IDB; this is the second phase of an existing multi-phase basic-education program, carrying forward a program-management unit and several support programs built up in the prior phase. Finances network expansion (new classrooms/childcare-center units and facilities), school infrastructure upgrades (accessibility, connectivity, water/sanitation, renewable-energy/solar retrofits), pedagogical policies (literacy, special education, learning/assessment), management-practice tools (school-management software, rural/river transport optimization, continuing training for education managers), and program administration/audit/evaluation. Delivery is direct, executed by the municipal education secretariat through its program-management unit, across four components (infrastructure, pedagogical, management practices, administration), with infrastructure taking the largest cost share.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
A.5 Financial Sustainability: funding source is identified in both NT versions (an earmarked share of the municipality's own tax revenue plus transfers from the national basic-education maintenance fund), but post-project maintenance cost (custeio/reposição) was explicitly not estimated in either version — the NT scores that specific sub-item at zero both times. A.3 Risk Assessment: a risk matrix by theme (legal/institutional, procurement, expropriation/resettlement, environmental/social) is present with stated mitigations in both versions; the proponent reports it will avoid siting new facilities on land requiring expropriation/resettlement (preferring already-available public land), while still reserving a budget contingency for that purpose, and the NT itself notes no resettlement-specific risk was actually presented. Licensing is referenced only as a generic preparatory-action category, with no per-component licensing status (NT silent on detail, consistent across both versions). A.4 Implementation: capacity draws on the executing unit's experience and acquisitions/safeguards knowledge from the program's first phase; the main change between the withdrawn and the approved version is that the operational-structure sub-item moved from unscored (no technical-team detail) to scored (named roles and years of experience furnished), which is the principal driver of the management-score improvement between the two attempts.
