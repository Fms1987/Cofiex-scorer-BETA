## Resumo
- Modality: climate policy-based financing (PBL) from AIIB, disbursed as general budget support to the national treasury against previously agreed policy milestones (prior actions), many already completed at submission.
- Proponent is the federal finance ministry; delivery is policy reform, not works. Component amounts are declared as zero for the three thematic pillars; the whole ticket sits in a fourth "policy matrix" component.
- Three pillars: sustainable finance (carbon market, tax-incentivised green bonds, blended-finance auctions), energy transition (sustainable aviation fuel framework), green and resilient infrastructure (health-sector climate adaptation, forest concessions with carbon credits, mangrove programme).
- Developed in coordination with two sibling policy-based operations from other multilaterals; counterpart zero, as expected for PBL.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Pillar-by-pillar chain from policy milestones to expected results is narrative and linked to the national climate strategy and PPA axis, but assumptions are not stated as testable.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Three indicators (verified), approximate split: one financial-mobilisation outcome, one emissions-reduction outcome, one output-type count of concessions; one per pillar.
- Baselines: all three stated as zero for 2025 (new instruments); the CC does not explain the zeros explicitly, so none counted as defined (share 0); the reference year is stated but not counted.
- Verification: named public sources for the aviation and forest indicators; the auction indicator relies on homologated bids reported by the ministry. No external benchmark is cited.
- Inconsistencies: the forest indicator target is compound (a count of concessions plus a minimum area plus further projects in consultation); the emissions-reduction target is very modest relative to the stated ambition.
- Small indicator count is structural for PBL (the prior-action matrix replaces results-type indicators).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: not applicable in the O&M sense (budget support, no assets); no OPEX discussion. Only FX risk on the dollar loan is flagged, to be handled by public debt management.
- A.3 Risks: four risks (legal approval, contracting/FX, technical implementation delay, electoral-institutional) with impact/probability and mitigation; no environmental or social risks identified, justified by the reform nature. The CC itself notes the loan contract has no penalties for missed policy commitments.
- Counterpart: none required (PBL); no LOA/PPA counterpart question arises.
- A.4 Implementation: coordination nucleus (two senior finance/treasury officials) plus an execution nucleus with one leader per responsible agency, formalised by ministerial ordinance; sectoral ministries own the actions. No dedicated UGP; reasonable for PBL.
