## Resumo
- Investment loan from two multilateral lenders (FONPLATA plus an OPEC-type fund; 100-200M band; no local counterpart required) to a non-dependent state-owned company of a subnational government, with federal guarantee and counter-guarantees from the company's own receivables and fund shares.
- Finances shared infrastructure for a knowledge-intensive innovation district: specialised buildings (innovation centre, labs, coworking/incubation spaces, auditoriums), supporting urban infrastructure (mobility, utilities, smart lighting, landscaping) and digital systems.
- Four components: studies and executive designs (roughly 5% of cost), works for infrastructure and strategic buildings (roughly 85%), institutional development (roughly 5%), programme management (roughly 5%). Direct execution by the company itself; private co-funding comes through an investment-fund vehicle.
- Programme claims a first phase plus a much larger long-term build-out; indicator targets are set at an intermediate and a long-term horizon.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- **A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE.** Clear problem (low productive diversification, dependence on the public sector, missing specialised innovation infrastructure) leading to infrastructure, then tenant/firm attraction, then R&D partnerships and local spillovers; relies on occupancy and demand assumptions that are not tested explicitly.
- **A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE.** 11 indicators, approximately 5 output-type (built capacity, urban infrastructure completion, green area, firms installed, people trained) and approximately 6 outcome-type (collaborative R&D, digital public services, cultural events, local suppliers, public bodies served, own-revenue coverage of O&M). Each has formula, scope, inclusion/exclusion rules, data sources and periodicity, with audit-style verification; mechanism present.
- Baselines: 11 of 11 with value and reference year (2025). Nine are zero baselines counted as defined because the CC explains each (nothing built or operating yet); one is an approximate estimate of existing green cover, one an estimate of current annual events. baseline_defined_share = 1.0; with-year share = 1.0.
- Targets are given at two horizons (intermediate and a long-term one about twenty years out); the long horizon exceeds the loan life, so only the intermediate targets are realistically tied to the operation. Some indicators (number of firms, public bodies) are volume counts with weak link to productivity or capacity gains, as the NT also remarks for firm counts.
- No external benchmark index; the CC cites national CT&I indicators and international certification standards as aspirations.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- **A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE.** Self-financing model: O&M and CT&I programmes to be covered by own revenues (rent and use-rights, technology services, events, fund income); a dedicated indicator targets partial O&M coverage at the intermediate horizon and full coverage in the long term, starting from zero; the objectives state operating balance within about five years of asset delivery. No separate O&M cost table seen; no legal act earmarking funds, but the company is non-dependent (no treasury transfers).
- **A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE.** Matrix of 5 risks: financing mismatch/schedule delay, licensing and regulatory, market/occupancy of buildings, cost overruns, O&M shortfall; impact/probability and mitigation given. Installation licence already obtained for the general area; building-specific licences in progress.
- Counterpart: none required (0%), so no LOA/PPA counterpart issue; instead the guarantee package (receivable flows and pledged fund shares) is described as counter-guarantees, still to be detailed in a specific plan.
- **A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE.** Execution by the company's directorates (technical, finance, administration) with a finance directorate managing disbursements and the fund interface; interfaces with licensing and utility bodies named; no distinct UGP structure described in the parts reviewed.
