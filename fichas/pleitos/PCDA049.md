## Resumo
- Policy-based loan (PBL) from KfW to a federal ministry, with disbursement tied to agreed policy actions in a public-policy matrix; no physical works, no goods, no counterpart (waived because disbursement is conditional on milestones).
- Finances national urban-mobility policy: national mobility plan and studies, a georeferenced platform for mass-transit investment, a federal public-transport funding mechanism and legal-framework update, electric-bus standardisation and fleet-electrification guidance, cycling strategy and awards, climate sector plan.
- Three components: planning and financing, decarbonisation, and institutional development with gender and anti-racism cross-cutting training.
- Execution by the ministry's national mobility secretariat; funds enter the Treasury as programmatic support, no transfers among participating bodies.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Six diagnosed problems link to actions in the policy matrix and to five expected results, with explicit mapping of actions to components; assumptions on subnational uptake and legislative timing are treated as risks rather than testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Six indicators verified: approximately three outcome-type (shares of municipalities with mobility plans, with federally supported investment, and fleet renewal; all taken from the federal multi-year plan with annual targets) and three output-type (awards and seals, certificates of training in project preparation, certificates in gender and race approach).
- Verification: calculation methodology and named public data sources/administrative systems for each indicator (public registry, official gazette, training portal). Benchmarks are the PPA indicators themselves.
- Baselines: all six have a stated value (6/6) and a reference year (6/6; five from 2024, one from 2023). No zero baselines.
- Weaknesses: outcome indicators are national and only loosely attributable to the programme; training indicators count certificates, not capability gained; the gender/race training indicator does not support a strategic-area score.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for a PBL. The text states no O&M is needed (no works or equipment); sustainability rests on legal/regulatory frameworks, replicable national guidelines, continuing federative financing arrangements and inclusion in the federal multi-year plan. Fleet-renewal benefits depend on external financing flows.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: five risks with impact/probability and mitigations: targets too ambitious blocking disbursement, delays in local policies, low subnational uptake, legislative delays on the public-transport legal framework, environmental impacts of urban transport. Licensing not applicable.
- Counterpart: none; waiver justified by conditional-disbursement design. Funds enter Treasury as programmatic support under the budget-guidelines law.
- A.4 Implementation: ministry secretariat with two departments and specialised technical staff, RACI matrix, continued KfW technical support (including grant technical cooperation); no dedicated UGP since no project execution.
