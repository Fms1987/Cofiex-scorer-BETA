## Resumo
- Modality: investment project financing with fast disbursement by reimbursement of eligible expenditure, supporting a relaunched national conditional cash-transfer programme (the header sector label is a keyword artefact; this is a social-protection operation).
- What is financed: the early-childhood supplement paid to beneficiary families (essentially all of the loan), plus non-reimbursable technical assistance from the lender, at no cost to the borrower, on conditionality monitoring and on the quality of the social registry.
- Delivery: direct, by the federal social-development ministry through a coordination unit and an execution unit led by the cash-transfer secretariat; two components (financial; technical assistance with two sub-components); disbursement over under two years; no counterpart required.
