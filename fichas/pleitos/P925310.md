## Resumo
- Modality: on-lending / credit-line programme; a federal state-owned bank borrows from a multilateral lender plus a concessional climate/agriculture fund tranche (roughly 70/30 split), with federal guarantee; counterpart none (bank's own funding covers the existing portfolio baseline).
- Finances credit expansion through the borrower's existing product lines (new products only if needed) in five themes: forest bioeconomy, forest restoration and agroforestry, renewable energy, low-carbon agriculture, connectivity.
- Two generic components (bioeconomy about 60%, sustainable development/renewables and connectivity about 40%); delivery is intermediated through the bank's branch network to final borrowers, with technical-cooperation support and a mitigation instrument still to be created.
- Regional scope covers a large multi-state biome region; final subprojects and eligibility criteria are defined during preparation.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Ten expected results mapped to the five themes and four specific objectives; credit mobilisation is presumed to deliver income, carbon and forest outcomes, without explicit assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 13 indicators (verified count); approximately 5 are credit-volume outputs, 1 gender share of credit, and about 7 outcome-type (jobs, avoided and sequestered carbon, restored and converted area, emissions reduction, people reached). Output-heavy mix.
- Baselines: all 13 have a stated numeric baseline (share 1.0); no reference year is given in the indicator table (with-year share 0.0). No zero baselines.
- Verification: some indicators rely on georeferenced measurement, but no independent verification mechanism or benchmarks; the connectivity volume formula text is copied from the renewable-energy one (internal inconsistency). NT also notes that the credit-volume targets do not add up to the total financing requested.
- Specific targets are stated to be aligned later with the bank's own sustainability commitments (partly deferred).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no O&M (financial intermediation); sustainability rests on the lender's own funding commitment for the baseline portfolio, existing products, branch network, and credit-risk tools (minimum collateral, early-repayment clause, agricultural insurance, guarantee funds, a new mitigation instrument to be created). Funding source for the baseline is stated; ten instruments listed.
- A.3 Risks: no formal matrix with probability/impact; environmental and social risk handled through the bank's corporate risk taxonomy and screening tools (licensing-compliance checks, media monitoring, forced-labour list, georeferenced diagnostics). Legal acts: only the loan authorisations. Expropriation and resettlement blank.
- Counterpart: not applicable as a project share; legal acts limited to external-loan authorisations.
- A.4 Implementation: the bank's sustainability unit coordinates with credit and risk directorates; responsibility matrix attached and judged adequate by the NT; most preparatory steps done, a few still in development; the bank has prior external-lender experience.
