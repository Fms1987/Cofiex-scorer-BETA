## Resumo
- Investment loan to a non-dependent state-owned industrial/port development company, financed from concessional climate-fund resources channelled via IBRD (no interest, service fee only, long grace and amortisation), with a small grant top-up outside the guarantee; state counter-guarantee to the Union.
- Complements a previously approved operation by the same borrower (explicitly a second tranche of one combined IBRD project).
- Two components: (i) shared infrastructure for a green-hydrogen value chain (utility corridors, port berth expansions, operational/security systems), roughly three quarters of cost as works; (ii) an applied research and training centre, roughly a fifth of cost.
- No counterpart; direct execution by the borrower through its own UGP.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear chain from missing shared infrastructure/skills to private investment and emissions avoided, but assumptions (offtake contracts, final investment decisions) are only handled as a demand risk, not as testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK-to-ADEQUATE. 8 indicators verified in the CC table; approximately 5 are impact/outcome-level 2034 targets (volumes moved, renewable and electrolyser capacity, avoided emissions, leveraged private capital, jobs) and 3 are output-level (people trained, studies, plus jobs/leverage depending on reading).
- Baselines: all 8 stated as zero; counted as defined because the hub and value chain are new (counterfactual). No baseline reference year; targets are year-2034 end-points with a progressivity annex.
- No verification column (source/frequency) in the indicator table; several targets depend on third-party private decisions, outside the borrower's control and beyond the disbursement window.
- Internal inconsistency: the headline impact indicators measure the whole hydrogen hub while financed outputs are enabling infrastructure; attribution is weak.
## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE-to-STRONG. Post-project OPEX stated as a percentage of capex (about 5%), funded from the borrower's own resources under a long-term maintenance plan (multi-year projection annexed) and a business plan approved by its governing bodies; hub revenue estimates and cash-flow statement annexed.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative per category (legal, procurement, expropriation, environmental, social, resettlement) mostly rated low; demand risk added as an extra item with mitigants (pre-contracts, MoUs). Heterogeneous quality; no matrix; no procurement risk for works.
- Licensing: environmental impact study and preliminary licence already obtained for the land-side hub; port-side works said to be licensed but documentation is thinner. No expropriation or resettlement (state-owned land ceded to the borrower).
- Counterpart: none required (concessional fund, no counterpart); state counter-guarantee offered.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP inside the borrower with a RACI chart, state technical group as adviser; team composition and experience not detailed; borrower has no external-credit track record in the last decade.
