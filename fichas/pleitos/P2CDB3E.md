## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a capital municipality, with a federal guarantee; counterpart at the 20% minimum, fully allocated to the public-sector payment (aporte) to the private partner.
- Finances a PPP concession (works plus long-term operation) for an urban public-space redevelopment in a dense central district: a continuous pedestrian esplanade with green areas, sidewalk and accessibility upgrades, and a cultural/entrepreneurship facility with a historical exhibition.
- Delivery model: direct municipal executing secretariat supervising a concessionaire that builds, operates and maintains; financing covers part of the public payment, not the works themselves.
- Components: two (esplanade works; cultural and economic-support space). Roughly nine-tenths of cost sits in the first component.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Five-problem diagnosis maps to three specific objectives and two components, with PPA objective cross-references per indicator; assumptions are implicit and no explicit results chain is drawn. The climate/emissions objective has no matching indicator.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified 4 indicators; approximately half are outputs/physical (public-space area measures) and half outcome-type (weekend visitation, bookings at municipal entrepreneurship spaces). All four have a stated 2025 baseline value and reference year (share 1.0 / 1.0; no zero baselines). Baselines come from municipal GIS layers and third-party visitation data; targets are expressed as percentage increases at a defined horizon (5th concession year) with annual monitoring.
- Verification: present and concrete (GIS re-measurement, field counts, official booking system reports, photo records); SMART tables are included for each indicator.
- Weaknesses: the visitation target is borrowed from an analogous pedestrianisation case; two indicators measure area built rather than use; no indicator for emissions or modal shift despite the stated objective.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M is quantified by cost line (security, cleaning, maintenance, utilities, concessionaire administration, insurance, cultural activation); security is the largest line. Funded by monthly municipal counter-payments plus the concessionaire's ancillary revenues; long-term obligations sit in the concession's operating-obligations annex. No explicit statement that counter-payments are secured in the budget cycle.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Three risks with impact/probability, cause, effect and mitigation: works delay in a dense centre, traffic disruption, and conflict with long-established street vendors needing relocation (social transition plan, relocation areas). Environmental, heritage and municipal licensing is described through the institutional arrangement (several licensing and heritage bodies) rather than a status table.
- Counterpart: 20% of total cost, municipal, to be secured via authorising law and budget; counter-guarantees tied to constitutional revenue vinculations, sufficiency to be checked by STN in the PVL stage. PPA alignment is documented against the municipal multi-year plan.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A municipal secretariat for subprefectures is executor, with a multi-secretariat arrangement, a RACI matrix annex and a state-owned structuring company with prior concession experience; no dedicated UGP described.
