## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from CAF to a large capital municipality under the general subnational sublimit; counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances urban flood control: off-line detention reservoirs (one beneath a roadway) with pumping, plus telemetric rainfall and level monitoring feeding the existing municipal flood early-warning system; rooftop slabs are converted into public leisure space.
- Two components: macrodrainage works with environmental services and monitoring (about 95% of cost) and programme management (supervision, project-unit support, external audit).
- Delivery: direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat with a project unit housed there; operation and maintenance sit with local sub-municipal administrations and a hydraulics foundation for the monitoring network.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Explicit problem-solution-result chain (insufficient drainage capacity and absent early warning, detention volume and monitoring, smaller flooded area, fewer exposed residents, fewer road interruptions); design is hydraulically grounded (100-year return period modelling), though assumptions are not listed as such.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators verified; approximately 6 outcome-type (flooded area, residents in flood area, precarious dwellings at risk, road interruptions, trips affected, population benefited) and 1 closer to output (solid waste diverted from streams).
- Baselines: all 7 have a baseline value with a reference year (2023 or 2024). Two zero baselines were counted as defined because they are new-asset effects (waste diverted, population benefited with no intervention completed). baseline_defined_share 1.0, baseline_with_year_share 1.0.
- Verification: strong. Technical reports with as-built drawings and modelling, municipal emergency-centre and traffic-company records, census and origin-destination survey data, reservoir cleaning records; annual yearly target paths given.
- Inconsistencies: the trips-affected indicator has a very large baseline falling to zero, with year-by-year percentages that do not match the stated values; the population-benefited indicator (very large scale) sits far from the residents-in-flood-area indicator; the road-interruption target year is labelled differently in two places; basin names differ between sections; the flooded-area baseline is small relative to the area said to be protected.
- NT view: some indicators judged not fully SMART, otherwise targets viable.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: STRONG. Annual maintenance cost is quantified separately for reservoir operation (pumping and cleaning), the leisure slabs and the monitoring network, each with a named responsible body (sub-municipal administrations by municipal decree; hydraulics foundation for monitoring) and funded from the municipal budget.
- Counterpart (20% minimum): scheduled by year alongside the loan; no LOA or PPA line cited for it, though the municipal plan and drainage master plan include the works and a credit-authorising law exists.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: 20 risks in a categorised matrix (regulatory, legal, contractual, social, operational, interference with utilities, financial, environmental). Notable: legal uncertainty on a federal-transfer counter-guarantee clause in the credit-authorising law, with a bill to amend it being prepared; environmental licences reported as issued or in progress.
- A.4 Implementation: a project unit to be set up inside the infrastructure secretariat, backed by a large technical staff and experience with lender-financed programmes; a responsibility matrix is annexed but described as succinct by the NT.
- Safeguards: works under a roadway and utility relocations flagged; no resettlement planned as the design avoids expropriation.
