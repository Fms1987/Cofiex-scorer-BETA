## Resumo
- Global credit / on-lending operation: a federal regional development bank borrows (guaranteed by the Union, counter-guaranteed with federal bonds) and lends directly to private energy-sector clients; funding is split in two roughly equal halves, an IDB loan and a concessional climate-fund loan, plus a small grant for a project-structuring facility.
- Single component with three products: modernisation and digitalisation of transmission/distribution (roughly half, mostly goods), energy storage projects (about a third) and renewable generation plants (the remainder); no specific allocation per technology.
- No counterpart required (state-owned financial institution exempt); executor is the bank itself with staff from its own structure; currency risk hedged by swap and passed on to sub-borrowers.
- Eligible sub-projects are drawn from the bank's existing pipeline but not named.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Barriers to integrating variable renewables (grid digitalisation, storage immaturity, no ancillary-services market) are tied to eligible investment types, but the link from sub-loans to system-level outcomes is not tested and assumptions are implicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. 4 indicators verified, all regional-level outcomes or impact (renewable capacity growth, renewable generation growth, storage capacity installed, emissions avoided over five years); none measure grid modernisation, the largest product.
- Baselines: 2 of 4 defined with values (capacity and generation, with 2023 reference in the formula and public data sources); storage baseline stated as zero with the CC saying no data exist (counted as undefined); emissions baseline zero, unexplained (undefined).
- Verification: public national databases and the bank's economic research unit are named as sources; targets look mis-scaled relative to the stated baselines (a baseline of tens of thousands of GWh against a target of a fraction of a percent), an internal inconsistency in units.
- Attribution weak: regional capacity growth depends on many financiers, not only this line.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE for the intermediary (no extra staff or consultancies; costs absorbed by existing teams; margin from blended local funding), but post-project O&M of financed assets is borne by private sub-borrowers and not quantified.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative by category; demand risk quantified against the bank's energy pipeline (unmet demand well above the new funding), currency risk hedged, credit risk handled by the standard credit process; environmental-social management follows the lender's framework. No matrix, no per-technology risk (storage, hydrogen, hydro-reversible).
- Counterpart: none required; counter-guarantee through federal bonds for the Union guarantee. No LOA/PPA issue.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Strategic management at headquarters, operations through state-level units; prior experience with several multilaterals cited; roles via responsibility matrix annexed; ratio of private sub-borrowers as executors means limited control over delivery.
