## Resumo
- Investment loan (IDB, BRL-denominated) to a capital municipality's health secretariat; direct execution by a project unit inside the secretariat, with works contracted by a municipal public-works agency and IT support from the municipal data company.
- Four components: (1) primary-care network expansion through a PPP concession (roughly 60% of cost; new standard-design primary-care units, PPP contract-management capacity, modelling studies for extending the PPP); (2) specialised and urgent-care infrastructure (mental-health, rehabilitation, urgent-care, zoonosis surveillance and supply-logistics facilities); (3) digital transformation and telehealth, cost-management systems, records digitisation and continuing education; (4) programme management, external audit, supervision and impact evaluation.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum; 60-month disbursement period. Follow-on to an earlier IDB operation with the same borrower.
