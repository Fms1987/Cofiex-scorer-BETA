## Resumo
- Modality: investment programme with sovereign guarantee, delivered as a credit line through a federal development bank (intermediated; end borrowers are states and municipalities).
- Finances digital-transformation projects of subnational governments across four axes: digital infrastructure and connectivity, digital economy and innovation ecosystem, digital government, and enabling factors (training, regulatory framework, cybersecurity).
- Single component and single product (credit granted); eligibility criteria, pipeline and results matrix are deferred to the operating regulation to be agreed with the lender.
- Counterpart is about a sixth of total cost, funded from the bank's own resources and sub-borrowers; 4-year disbursement, long grace and amortisation.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (subnational digital gap, uneven municipal capacity) links to four axes and three specific objectives, but there is no explicit causal chain or assumptions; the CC itself says results design comes later.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified 4 indicators, all outcome-type by label but generic "planned/realised %" ratios; every baseline is 0 and target 100 with no explanation, so counted as undefined (share 0). No reference year, no verification means, no frequency, no year-by-year path.
- NT noted that measurement criteria and methodologies were not defined and that indicators were only preliminary; deferred to the operating regulation.
- Indicators are coherent with objectives but one (education access) is narrow relative to a multi-axis programme; no benchmarks cited.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ABSENT in substance. CC says no additional costs because the executing units already exist inside the bank; no O&M or post-project cost of financed digital assets, no funding source for sub-borrower maintenance.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. Legal acts, expropriation, social impact and resettlement are "not applicable"; contracting risks deferred to later lender/bank analysis; environmental risk stated as low without matrix or mitigation.
- Counterpart: to be provided from the bank's ordinary resources and sub-borrowers once eligible operations are defined; no LOA/PPA issue since the borrower is a federal state-owned bank.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A defined bank department handles sub-loan origination and follow-up; long track record with the lender; prior MoU, pilot diagnostics and a municipal self-assessment platform give a pipeline basis (NT rated prior actions well).
