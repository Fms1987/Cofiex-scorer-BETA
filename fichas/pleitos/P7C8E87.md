## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan with federal guarantee, IBRD, 80/20 lender/counterpart split, 6-year disbursement, 5-year grace and 20-year amortisation.
- Finances the first phase of a segregated bus rapid transit corridor (concrete busway with drainage, grade-separated structures and signalling), stations, interchange terminals, footbridges, bus bays and sidewalks; later phase is outside this request.
- Roughly 90% of cost is works (about 80% the busway itself); the rest is institutional strengthening (pavement-management system, barrier study, fleet-electrification study) and programme management (supervision, UGP, external audit).
- Direct execution by a subnational government through the transport secretariat and the roads department, with a new UGP to be created by decree.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE (leaning WEAK on coherence). Diagnosis (car-oriented dispersed urban form, low-income periphery, rising motorisation) leads to a trunk-and-feeder busway, benchmarked on an existing sister corridor; no explicit assumptions. Expected results are expressed for the whole corridor (both phases), not for the phase financed, and it is unclear whether the first phase operates on its own.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. 7 indicators verified (all outcome or proxy-outcome type, none pure outputs; split approximate): GHG/pollutant reduction, daily passengers, jobs accessible within a time threshold, travel time, composite urban well-being index, road accidents, Gini index.
- Baselines: 5 of 7 have a stated value (passengers, travel time, well-being index, accidents, Gini); GHG reduction and jobs-accessible have a baseline of 0 with no explanation, counted as undefined. Baseline with reference year: 4 of 7 (passenger baseline has no year).
- Design flaws: jobs-accessible target is illogical against the stated ridership; GHG indicator has no measurement point or protocol; Gini is not tied to any specific objective and attribution is not addressed; travel-time target uses the full-corridor length rather than phase one; "area of influence" undefined.
- No data-collection or verification mechanism described; only a few indicators carry a time marker. No standard benchmark adopted (only the sister corridor experience).

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Maintenance by the roads department and the urban works agency under an existing continuous concrete-pavement maintenance contracting process; annual maintenance estimated at about 0.5% of works cost, from the treasury (not loan funds); legal backing via a patrimony-maintenance decree. Station/terminal operation and maintenance through service contracts and concessions. No operating-subsidy or fare-revenue analysis for the bus operations.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Risks listed by theme (legal acts, procurement delay, expropriation/utility interference, environmental, social, resettlement) with mitigations. Works sit inside an existing right-of-way, so no expropriation expected; utility interferences already budgeted. Environmental studies done, prior licence plus water-use grant obtained, environmental programmes approved; installation licence pending and the tree inventory needs updating. Safeguards via the lender's environmental and social framework; resettlement only contingent (informal occupation of the right-of-way).
- Counterpart: 20% minimum, to be placed in the LOA after COFIEX recommendation (to be secured); state authorising law still required.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE-WEAK. UGP to be created by decree, small multi-profile team, 5-year management horizon; NT notes no stated knowledge of lender procurement/safeguard policies and no team experience information. Prior external-credit experience exists but rated poorly in the portfolio performance metric.
