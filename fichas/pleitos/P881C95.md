## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (CAF) to a mid-to-large municipal government, with Union guarantee; cost share with counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances a municipal urban-upgrading package in precarious, informal neighbourhoods: macro/micro drainage, road pavement reconstruction, sidewalk reconstruction (accessibility elements) and hillside containment in mapped landslide-risk areas.
- Delivery: direct execution by the municipal public-works secretariat through a UGP to be created by municipal decree, supported by a supervision/management consultancy.
- Three components: civil works (large majority of cost, with hillside containment the biggest single product), programme management, and financial charges.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE-to-WEAK. Diagnosis rests on a municipal risk-reduction plan (risk sectors, informal housing share), and each solution lists expected results, but no explicit causal chain or assumptions; the need for repaving and sidewalks is weakly tied to the stated problems.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Verified count: 4 indicators, all outputs (they restate the four works products); 0 outcome indicators. No indicator measures accident reduction, mobility, flooding or landslide exposure even though these are the stated results.
- Baselines: all 4 have a stated value (100% defined; none is a zero-baseline), but none states a reference year or period (baseline_with_year_share 0). One baseline for slopes protected is a single unit, which looks like a placeholder.
- No verification mechanism or data source described; no benchmarks; targets are cumulative physical quantities (baseline plus programme output).
- Internal inconsistency: physical quantities in the components section and in the solution text differ from each other and from indicator targets.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. O&M to be absorbed by the existing annual budget of the works secretariat, with an incremental cost asserted at under 10% of that budget and no extra staffing; no quantified OPEX table, no legal backing for the allocation, and the CC does not set out concrete maintenance measures.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. Only a procurement/legal risk matrix (8 risks, mostly operational, low probability, critical impact, with generic mitigations); sections on expropriation, environmental impact, social impact and resettlement are empty despite hillside works in informal areas; no licensing status.
- Counterpart: programme said to be in the draft LOA for the following year and in the PPA; authorizing municipal law still pending (administrative process opened); counterpart rules declared compliant with Res. COFIEX 3/2019, though a financing-commission line sits in the components (NT flags this as not allowed against counterpart).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP to be created by decree under the works secretariat with three coordinations, minimum share of career civil servants and a management consultancy; no prior experience with external credit; preparatory studies only partially described (basic designs for drainage/paving/sidewalks, studies for slopes).
- Fiscal context: CC states the municipality has CAPAG A.
