## Resumo
- Modality: investment project loan from IBRD to a state government with Union guarantee; counterpart at the 20% minimum of project cost; about 5-year disbursement period.
- Finances a chronic non-communicable disease care network: adaptation of existing hospital structures into oncology high-complexity centres (roughly 60% of cost, works/services), outpatient dialysis equipment to decentralise service to regional hospitals (about 10% of cost), a pharmaceutical-assistance management system plus expansion of a home medicine-delivery programme (small share), a hospital-network management information system (about a fifth), and consulting, training and a project unit.
- Delivery: direct execution by the state health secretariat through a dedicated UGP, with legal and procurement agencies supporting; no intermediation.
- Structure: three components with subcomponents and products; the first component holds about 70% of total cost.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (chronic disease burden, weak regulation, fragmented information, no structured digital investment programme) links coherently to components, but assumptions are not stated as testable and no explicit causal chain is drawn.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Verified count: 5 indicators; approximately 3 outcome-type (waiting time to start cancer treatment, share of emergency pharmaceutical purchases, avoidable stock losses) and 2 output/coverage-type (decentralised dialysis posts, programme beneficiaries).
- Baselines: all 5 have a stated value; the zero baseline for decentralised dialysis posts is counted as defined because the service does not yet exist at the target hospitals. No reference year/period is given for any baseline, so year share is 0. No verification source or method is stated for any indicator.
- Targets are point values with timing inside the execution period; no wide ranges or deferred indicators. No external benchmark used.
- Gap: the hospital information system and the training/consulting products have no outcome indicator.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. No OPEX projection; only a qualitative argument that service billing from the SUS (accreditation and incentives) will sustain the new oncology and dialysis services, and that shared hospital structure lowers costs. Software maintenance and licences after the project not quantified.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE/WEAK. Legal, procurement, expropriation and resettlement risks addressed (the last two as not applicable); environmental and social risks treated as low and deferred to safeguard documents under the lender framework. No risk matrix; mitigation is mostly centralisation of procurement in the UGP and a dedicated team.
- Counterpart: to be secured; the authorising state law, UGP decree and inclusion in PPA/LDO/LOA are announced as pending after approval.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Dedicated UGP in the state; prior experience with the lender and earlier health programmes cited; limited detail on knowledge of lender procurement rules.
