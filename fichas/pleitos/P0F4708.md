## Resumo
Investment operation for a state government, executed directly (direct delivery model) by the state's rural-development agency, with an environmental secretariat and an agronomic-research institute acting as co-executors for specific components. Finances agroecological/organic family-farming production support (investment plans, water-harvesting social technologies, technical assistance, processing and commercialization infrastructure, participatory organic certification), an environmental-management component (ecological restoration/reforestation, renewable-energy incentives, local environmental-agent capacity building), institutional strengthening, and a program-management component (UGP/territorial units, M&E, audits). Follows on from a prior rural-development operation with the same lender.
