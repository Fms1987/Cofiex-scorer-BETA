## Resumo
- Investment loan from NDB to a state government, direct execution by a state works secretariat through an existing UGP (second phase of a programme already well advanced, co-financed by NDB and CAF in phase one). Counterpart at the 20% minimum; guarantee of the Union requested.
- Finances urban road paving with complementary kerbs/sidewalks and deep stormwater macrodrainage in a few dozen municipalities (metropolitan area of the capital plus interior cities), plus a project bank (engineering designs and PPP/concession structuring studies) and programme management. Roughly 80% of cost is civil works, about a tenth is studies/project bank, remainder management, supervision, audit and front-end fee.
- Works are handed to the municipalities (Termo de Compromisso) for O&M; municipal licensing; no resettlement or expropriation claimed (consolidated urban areas).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-results chain is coherent per component (low paving/drainage coverage and project-stock deficit lead to works and a project bank, then to mobility, flood and procurement-speed gains), but assumptions are not stated or testable (e.g. municipal uptake, bank projects actually financed).
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators verified; roughly 3 outcome-type (people affected by floods, procurement time, leveraged investment volume) and 4 output-type (paving coverage, drainage coverage, number of projects ready, number of PPP/concession studies) - approximate judgement.
- All 7 have a baseline value and reference year plus a target year; zero baselines (3: project bank, leveraged volume, studies) counted as defined because the CC frames them as a new asset with no prior stock.
- Verification mechanism ABSENT (no sources, frequency or responsible party); the drainage indicator reuses the paving formula text (internal inconsistency); the leveraged-investment indicator is expressed in BRL while the loan is in USD and depends on future financing decisions. SNIS is cited as benchmark only in the diagnosis.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK. O&M is transferred by Termo de Compromisso to each beneficiary municipality, which must allocate own budget; an O&M manual is delivered with the works. No O&M cost estimate, no municipal budget evidence, no legal backing beyond the template instrument (the NT noted this was requested in a revision round and not supplied).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative risks by category (legal acts, procurement delay, expropriation, environmental, social, resettlement); main risk is slow procurement, mitigated by experienced teams and a supervision firm. Social risk dismissed as only positive; utility-interference relocation deferred to executive designs. No quantified matrix.
- Licensing: both works packages "to be licensed" by municipal environmental bodies; no expropriation or resettlement expected.
- Counterpart: tax-revenue sources named per Res. 3/2019; LOA inclusion and the authorizing law to be secured after COFIEX recommendation; action exists in the current PPA and is expected in the next one (under preparation).
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Existing UGP created by decree, multidisciplinary team, RACI matrix and organogram, lessons learned from phase one, supervision and audit contracts; five-year disbursement justified by rainy-season constraints.
