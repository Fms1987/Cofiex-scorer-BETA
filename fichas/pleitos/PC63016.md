## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IBRD to a capital municipality that is also a border municipality, with counterpart of about one-sixth of total cost; 4-year grace, 20-year amortisation; sovereign counter-guarantees proposed via municipal participation-fund and other net current revenues.
- Finances an integrated urban mobility programme: bus-fleet electrification and charging, road opening/requalification and resurfacing, sidewalks and cycle network, urban greening and nature-based drainage, and an upgrade of the urban section of an international freight corridor.
- Five components, roughly 20% public transport, 30% road network, 20% active mobility, a few percent urban sustainability, 20-25% freight-corridor integration; no dedicated project-management component.
- Delivery: direct execution by the municipal mobility secretariat with several partner secretariats, a steering committee and an existing works-monitoring system.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Five problems (insufficient transit, degraded and unconnected road grid, fragmented active-mobility network, climate exposure, weak regional logistics) map to five specific objectives and components; assumptions and attribution (e.g., logistics export growth from a corridor upgrade) are not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 10 indicators verified, each with baseline value and reference year (all 10 counted as defined; the single zero baseline, for infiltration by nature-based solutions, concerns a newly built asset and is counted as explained). Approximately 6 are outcome-type (travel time, emissions, crowding, road saturation, regional trade share, freight flow) and about 4 outputs (accessible sidewalks, cycle network, tree cover, infiltration).
- Verification: data definitions given per indicator (GPS/ITS, origin-destination surveys, ticketing, traffic counts, municipal inventories, customs/foreign-trade statistics); targets mostly to a single horizon, with a three-step schedule for the freight indicator. Benchmarks: HCM-style saturation bands, accessibility standard, IPCC emission factors.
- Inconsistencies: road-saturation formula is written as capacity over volume while the interpretation bands follow volume over capacity; emission baseline is expressed in a very small absolute tonnage for a bus fleet and the indicator mixes percent and tonnes; the regional-trade formula subtracts imports from exports; the "fluidity" indicator actually measures the share of freight vehicles using a link; tree-cover baseline sourced from a press article rather than an official inventory.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: ADEQUATE. Added annual maintenance cost per km of new urban road and of cycle lane is estimated (per-km rates, 2024 reference), assets are incorporated into the municipal conservation plan, and the CC states these costs are already in the LOA and the multi-year plan; funding from own revenues and transfers, PPP for intensive maintenance only as an option. Bus operating/fleet-renewal costs after the loan are not quantified.
- A.3 Risk assessment: ADEQUATE. Fifteen risks with impact, probability, prevention and mitigation, including failed/deserted tender, expropriation, occupied areas and resettlement, environmental impact, legal acts, non-payment, tender challenges and pre-emption rights; mitigations are generic. Licensing is mentioned as handled by the municipal environment secretariat, with no per-component status.
- Counterpart: stated as budgeted (LOA and multi-year plan); the plan cited as framework is the 2022-2025 one while the maintenance text cites a 2026-2029 plan, an internal timing inconsistency. Legal authorisation for the operation not shown.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. Lead mobility secretariat with partner secretariats, steering committee, RACI annex and prior experience with nationally financed road works, but no lender-experienced UGP is described.
