## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (IBRD) whose proceeds exclusively fund the public counter-payments (capital contributions) owed by a state government to a private partner in a PPP; no counterpart (PPP exemption), 20-year grace-plus-amortization profile.
- Complementary pleito to an already approved PPP for a state ferry-crossing system: it covers extra cost from relocating and rebuilding one passenger and vehicle terminal as a greenfield facility.
- Components: civil works (about 95% of cost), socio-environmental liabilities and licensing, and systems and signage (CCTV, automated fare collection, control centre).
- Delivery model: direct via a state PPP secretariat as executor, with a PPP company supporting structuring and a sector regulator supervising the private concessionaire.
- Entity: a state government (not on the frontier).

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear problem-solution chain (insufficient land and water-side capacity, long peak queues, poor weather protection, fragmented contracts) leading to a relocated terminal under a performance-based PPP; assumptions on demand growth and tourism spill-overs are stated but not tested.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Verified 3 indicators (passengers per year, vehicles per year, queue index), approximately 1 outcome (queue time) and 2 volume/output-type measures; all three have baselines, targets and a reference horizon tied to operation start or concession year.
- Baselines: all 3 stated as current values (annual passengers, annual vehicles, annual hours of long queues) but with no explicit reference year, so with-year share is zero. No zero baselines.
- Verification: present and contractual (concessionaire counts reported to the regulator; queue time measured by the traffic authority with the concessionaire's support, supervised by the regulator); queue indicator has a stated method and exclusion for extreme weather.
- Targets: mid-teens percent growth in volumes over the concession horizon, which is far below the specific objectives' stated capacity gains; objectives mention larger uplifts than indicators, a mild inconsistency. No external benchmarks.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. O&M and renewal fall to the concessionaire under a 20-year contract with performance-linked remuneration; no tariff increase foreseen, helped by operating-cost savings of roughly a fifth from more efficient vessels. Public capital contributions are covered by this loan; full PPP financial model annexed (not quantified in the text).
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Five risks rated with impact/probability and mitigation owner: slope instability, solid waste and effluents, siltation, regulatory (port-area land-use assignment still being drafted), and environmental licensing (high probability, low impact, liabilities inventoried and priced).
- Counterpart: none required (PPP counter-payment exemption, Art. 36 VI); the authorizing state law is not yet enacted and the CC says it will follow the standard counter-guarantee wording; budget and PPA programmes are referenced.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. A dedicated PPP secretariat executor with RACI annex, a structuring company and a regulator; public consultation and hearings already held for the parent PPP; no separate UGP described.
