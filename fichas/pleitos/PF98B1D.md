## Resumo
- On-lending credit line: a federal development bank borrows from a European lender under a federal guarantee and on-lends to public and/or private sanitation projects; ticket split roughly half external loan and half counterpart from the bank's own sources and sub-borrowers (counterpart at 50% of cost).
- Single component with a single product category, all as works: water supply, sewerage and urban stormwater among the segments, with the project portfolio to be defined jointly with the lender from the bank's existing and future credit pipeline; eligibility criteria still to be negotiated.
- Delivery is intermediated: funds are disbursed to the bank, which lends to sub-borrowers under its ordinary credit process and operational policies; the lender validates subprojects. Disbursement window up to four years; indicative floating or fixed pricing and long tenor with grace.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear sector diagnosis (coverage deficit, investment gap against the national sanitation plan and legal framework, health and productivity externalities) and a simple link from more long-term funding to more projects; no explicit assumptions on pipeline, additionality or sub-borrower capacity.
- A.2 Results Framework: WEAK. Five indicators (verified), approximately three output-type (sewer network extension, sewer connections, water meters installed) and two outcome-type (sewer and water coverage as share of population served by the projects), all with SNIS-style sector logic but sector-agnostic aggregation.
- Baselines: every baseline and target cell is shown as zero with no explanation (portfolio not yet defined), so none is counted as defined (share 0.0) and none has a reference year (share 0.0). Targets are therefore deferred, the NT also notes the indicators lack a time horizon.
- Verification: formulas given (sum or share of local population served), no data source, frequency or evaluation arrangement stated.
- Inconsistency: objectives text appears twice in the revised CC (NT used the second version, which carries legal-framework coverage targets not mirrored in the indicator table); NT describes two products (sewerage and water) while the form text shows one.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: no O&M or tariff analysis; sustainability delegated to the bank's standard credit analysis of each sub-borrower. NT scored it low. Counterpart is stated (about half of programme cost, from the bank's traditional funding sources and sub-borrowers) but without a budget instrument; the investment budget of the bank is said to cover the on-lent resources.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: no matrix; sub-borrower risks assumed by the bank and not passed to the lender; expropriation and resettlement risks left to sub-borrowers (expropriation costs not financed); socio-environmental classification and environmental licensing required; demand risk rated low as sanitation is a traditional bank segment. Social impact and indigenous areas declared out of scope.
- A.4 Implementation: simplified macro-level arrangement, no team or experience description, no statement on lender procurement/safeguard policies; NT noted strong borrower track record with external lenders (top experience score).
