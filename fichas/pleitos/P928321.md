## Resumo
- Investment loan for a municipal government (direct execution by a works secretariat acting as borrower's executor), roughly 85% civil works, about 12% consultancy/supervision/audit, the rest expropriation and financing fee.
- Components: (1) urban mobility works - a new structural north-south road link plus pavement resurfacing (roughly 60% and 15% of total cost); (2) a single urban stream channelisation / macro-drainage work (roughly 10%); (3) programme management (studies, management support, supervision, external audit, expropriation); (4) financing fee.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum; Union guarantee requested; 4-year disbursement period; header sector says water but the weight is clearly road mobility with drainage secondary.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Problem-solution-results chain is coherent (saturated crossings and flooding -> new link, resurfacing, channelisation -> travel time, modal share, flood exposure) but assumptions are implicit and no causal diagram is given.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE (borderline WEAK). 11 indicators verified (header's heuristic count of 12 was off); approximately 9 outcome-type (travel time, speed, mobility index, modal share, flood area/occurrences) and 2 output-type (passenger capacity by direction).
- All 11 carry a baseline value (none is zero), so baseline_defined_share = 1.0; no baseline states a reference year or period, so baseline_with_year_share = 0.0.
- No verification mechanism, data source, measurement date or target deadline for any indicator (the NT also flags missing deadlines/data availability); no external benchmark used.
- Internal inconsistencies: two flood indicators (exposed area and disease-susceptible area) share identical formula, baseline and target; the two capacity indicators share one formula; several formulas are written as % change while the unit is hours/m2/counts; the "areas with flooding potential" indicator is computed from occurrence counts; the public-transport cost-burden indicator is only loosely attributable to the works.
- Targets are modest (about +10% on mobility metrics, about -80% on flood-exposed area) and are not tied to any model or study cited in the CC.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: partly quantified - the executing secretariat commits an annual maintenance allocation of about 0.5% of invested value, to be written into budget bases, with the finance secretariat responsible for meeting budget requests. No breakdown by category, no multi-year table, no legal instrument beyond budget inclusion.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: narrative risk sections (no matrix) covering legal acts, procurement of engineering designs, expropriation, environmental licensing (water-use grant, licences for all works, possible vegetation suppression with compensation), social impact and resettlement; mitigations are generic (communication plan, RACI matrix, early contracting). Licensing status per work not given.
- Resettlement is small (a small number of families on public land, plus limited expropriation); compensation funded from the borrower's own resources through a separate budget allocation.
- Counterpart: assigned to the executing secretariat and linked to the PPA/LDO/LOA programmes, but the authorising bill still has to pass the municipal legislature - "to be secured".
- A.4 Implementation: UGP is not yet created (to be institutionalised by decree after approval); the executing secretariat has prior domestic-credit works experience but no external-creditor contract recorded in SIGS (NT gave the neutral score). Main design nearly final; drainage design not yet started; supervision/audit procurement at an early stage.
