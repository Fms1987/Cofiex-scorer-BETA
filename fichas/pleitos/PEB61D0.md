## Resumo
- Investment loan (FONPLATA) with federal guarantee to a mid-sized municipality, ticket band below 50M USD, counterpart at the 20% minimum (own tax revenues); 5-year disbursement, roughly linear. It follows an earlier, almost fully disbursed operation from the same lender with similar design.
- Multi-sector urban programme: sewerage collection and treatment plus neighbourhood stormwater macro-drainage (roughly 40% of cost); urban and rural road paving with a river crossing (roughly 40%); public facilities (a few schools, a tourism and crafts centre, a green public square, a small municipal solar plant; roughly 10%); studies, supervision, management, audit and financial costs for the rest.
- Delivery model direct: municipal executor (economic development secretariat) with the existing UEP created by decree for the earlier loan, supported by an RACI matrix across several municipal bodies and the municipal water and sewer utility.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Each problem (low sewer coverage, flooding, poor roads and rural isolation, school shortage, energy cost) is paired with a solution and expected results and ten specific objectives; causal assumptions are not stated, and the cultural/tourism and square components are loosely linked to the core objective.
- A.2 Results Framework: STRONG. Ten indicators (verified), approximately 8 outcome-type (sewer coverage, waterborne-disease consultations in the affected basins, school places, flood and isolation events per year, share of renewable energy, rural and neighbourhood travel times, tourist visits, registered artisans) and 2 output-leaning; all give baseline, target, base year and target year (target year at the end of the programme horizon) and a formula. Verification sources are implicit in the formulas (health-unit records, registers) rather than a monitoring plan.
- Baselines: 10 of 10 defined (share 1.0), counting the single zero baseline (renewable energy produced by the municipality, a new asset introduced by the programme) because the CC explains the plant as new; all baselines carry a reference year (share 1.0). Flood and isolation targets are zero events, an ambitious point target.
- Inconsistencies: tourist-visit indicator mixes monthly (formula) and annual (title) wording; the two travel-time indicators describe baseline and target as ranges in the formula but give point values in the table (Q29-type issue). No external benchmark is used.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M for the new works is estimated by category and compared with the municipality's existing maintenance contracts; each facility has a named responsible body, no new staff is needed, and funds are said to be allocated in the annual budget. Weak points: no multi-year O&M table, no tariff or utility-revenue discussion for the sewerage system, and no legal commitment beyond annual allocation.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal (municipal authorising law, fiscal-responsibility compliance, counter-guarantee), procurement and environmental risks are treated with mitigations, but the NT found environmental, social and expropriation risk treatment thin; no risk matrix. Licensing: sewer network licence already held, the drainage, bridge, schools, centre, square and solar plant licences still to be requested after executive designs; works on municipal land, no expropriation or resettlement.
- Counterpart: to be secured. It is in the municipal multi-year plan; LOA inclusion is promised only after COFIEX recommendation, and the authorising law is still pending.
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Existing UEP with decree, completed international and national bidding under the lender's rules in the earlier loan, supervision consultancy and RACI matrix; lender procurement and safeguard policies are known. Several executive designs still in preparation.
