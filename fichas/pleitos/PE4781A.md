## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IBRD to a state government, federal guarantee, counterpart at the 20% minimum, ticket in the 100-200M USD band, 8-year disbursement, 9-year grace.
- Financed: rehabilitation and proactive, performance-based maintenance of paved state roads (design-build-maintain contracts, long-term, pay-for-quality), with climate-resilient drainage ("green roads for water"), iRAP-based road-safety works and enforcement equipment.
- Also: institutional strengthening (road-asset management, PPP-type contract modelling, decarbonisation and climate studies, drones for right-of-way monitoring) and a pilot skills programme for women and racial minorities in construction/logistics.
- Delivery: direct execution by the state roads department through a UGP, with the planning secretariat as executing coordinator; roughly 85% of cost is works and safety works; components: road works and maintenance, institutional strengthening, road safety, project management.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Diagnosis (reactive maintenance, pavement degradation, high crash rates, climate exposure, low minority participation) maps to specific measures and objectives, with a priority-criteria list for road segments; assumptions (state capacity to manage performance contracts, contractor appetite for long contracts) are only partly stated.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 7 indicators verified; about 5 outcome-type (road roughness share, crash deaths, iRAP star share, community access, climate-adapted road share) and 2 output-type (roads covered by a decarbonisation plan, people trained or employed in the pilot).
- Baselines: all 7 are written as 0. I counted only the 2 count-type indicators (new plan, new pilot programme) as defined zeros, since the CC implicitly treats them as new activities; the 5 percentage indicators with a 0 baseline are unexplained (e.g. share of roads in good roughness class at 0%, a before/after ratio with a 0 baseline) and were counted as undefined. No reference year is stated for any baseline.
- Verification present: each indicator names data source (administrative records, national health data, statistics bodies, iRAP surveys) and a measurement cycle (annual or biennial); target timing given as mid- or end-of-project. Benchmarks: iRAP star rating, roughness (IRI) classes.
- Inconsistencies: the objective cites one IRI threshold while the expected-results text cites a different one; the crash indicator is a before/after ratio rather than a rate; savings claims rely on an analogous operation elsewhere, with a more conservative 20% assumed.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK to ADEQUATE. Argument is that proactive contracts cost less than current conventional maintenance (per-km annual cost comparison given in the CC), and a state logistics-infrastructure fund fed by a grain-production levy is named as the funding source; but no post-project maintenance cost forecast or budget commitment is provided.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Narrative sections per risk class (legal, procurement with novel long-term contracts, expropriation, environmental, social, resettlement); no matrix. Licensing: works are classed as low-impact functional recovery, licensed by exemption; safeguards instruments to be prepared with the lender.
- Counterpart: origin stated (tax revenue, Res. COFIEX 3/2019); authorising law pending and no LOA line shown ("to be secured"). PPA alignment stated for programmes and a state management plan.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP inside the roads department with procurement, financial, safeguards and engineering staff, RACI matrix, prior lender experience; the long-term performance contracting model is new to the state, mitigated by workshops, lender training and audit-body dialogue. Segments to be chosen later by criteria (final priority set in preparation).
