## Resumo
- Investment loan, directly executed by a capital-region municipal government (direct delivery model, own executing secretariat).
- Finances a diversified municipal urban-environmental and social program: urban drainage, recovery of degraded urban parks/water bodies and environmental governance, a gender-violence-prevention and livelihoods/capacity-building track, urban mobility works (streets, sidewalks, cycling infrastructure), upgrades to social/education/health facilities, and digital-government modernization.
- Main components: (i) socio-environmental & urban infrastructure (drainage, parks, environmental governance, gender-violence prevention, livelihoods), (ii) urban mobility, (iii) urban/social infrastructure (public facilities, schools, health units), (iv) program management and digital transformation; mobility is the single largest subcomponent (roughly half of financed cost).
- Entity type: capital-region municipal borrower with prior experience executing an externally financed program with a different lender.
