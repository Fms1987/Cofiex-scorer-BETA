## Resumo
- Modality: conventional investment loan (IBRD) to a state government for an urban socio-productive inclusion programme aimed at poor and food-insecure self-employed workers and their families; delivered directly by the state social-development secretariat with an executive committee, with civil-society organisations contracted through a public call to provide the technical assistance service.
- Components by approximate cost share: technical assistance and socio-professional qualification (roughly 50%); grants of productive assets and financial support, plus associative ventures such as urban gardens, community food facilities and waste-picker support hubs (roughly 30%); institutional strengthening (small); project management, monitoring and impact evaluation (roughly 10%).
- Income-transfer benefits are explicitly not financed; the loan funds inclusion actions integrated with existing federal and state transfers and services.
- Counterpart at the 20% minimum, from tax revenue under the Res. COFIEX 03/2019 rule.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Clear diagnosis (poverty, severe food insecurity, informality, unemployment, with statistical data) leading to a three-pillar strategy (income guarantee, socio-productive inclusion, access to services) and three expected results; the CC acknowledges external drivers (economy, labour market, other policies) but does not formalise testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. Twelve indicators verified: three outcome indicators (real income change of supported businesses, share of families overcoming severe food insecurity, share overcoming monetary extreme poverty) and nine output/coverage indicators (approximately 75% output), several disaggregated by gender, race and youth. Formulas given; income indicator is sample-based.
- Baselines: all twelve are stated as zero with no explanation, so none is counted as defined (baseline_defined_share 0.0; baseline_with_year_share 0.0). The zeros are inherent for cumulative counters and for change-over-time measures, but the CC does not say so.
- Verification: a monitoring system and impact-evaluation studies are budgeted; no external benchmarks cited; no wide-range or deferred indicators. Outcome targets are 80% conversion rates, ambitious relative to the intervention.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: WEAK to ADEQUATE. No forecast of post-project maintenance or recurring costs (NT 2.2: 0.194/0.300); continuity is to be planned from monitoring results ahead of the next PPA cycle, keeping budget lines in future PPA/LOA and considering federal voluntary transfers or new loans. A state enabling law for the inclusion programme and a food-security law exist.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal, procurement, environmental and social risks with mitigations (waste generation addressed through waste-picker support, political discontinuity); no expropriation or resettlement (NT 3.2: 0.376/0.400). No licensing-heavy works.
- Counterpart: 20% from tax revenue, stated as available; no specific LOA/PPA line shown ("to be secured" in budget terms).
- A.4 Implementation: STRONG. Social-development secretariat as executor with an executive committee, a responsibility matrix, long prior experience of the technical-assistance method under an earlier IBRD loan, and manuals annexed (NT 2.1: 0.376/0.400, 3.1: full marks). Portfolio performance score was mid-range (NT 3.3: 0.320/0.400).
