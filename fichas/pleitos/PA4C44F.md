## Resumo
Investment-modality external credit operation with a capital-municipality government as direct borrower and executor, financed by a multilateral bank (IDB) with contrapartida at the 20% minimum band, building on an earlier tourism-development program with the same lender. Finances tourism/cultural infrastructure and heritage-area urban revitalization (building retrofits for mixed housing/commercial use, waterfront and boat-landing facilities, a cultural center), a racial-equity economic-development component targeting the Black population (entrepreneurship and mentoring), destination marketing, institutional strengthening of municipal tourism management, and climate-adaptation/environmental-management actions (coastal-water-quality studies, a navigation-channel clean-up). Delivery model is direct municipal execution through a dedicated program-coordination unit under the municipal tourism/culture department (header `sector` of fund_intermediation appears to be a keyword-match artifact; the CC describes a direct tourism/urban-development investment program, not a financial-intermediation fund, so `sector_verified` is set to regional_development with a social secondary theme).
