## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan from IDB denominated in BRL to a capital municipality, with counterpart at the 20% minimum; values in the CC are in reais.
- Finances an integrated urban climate-resilience programme in a vulnerable peripheral basin: nature-based drainage and a linear park, urban infrastructure and public facilities, resettlement and land regularisation, community development (women, youth, urban gardens) and municipal monitoring/institutional modernisation.
- Three components: (C1) environmental and infrastructure works, about 60% of cost; (C2) socio-environmental development and institutional modernisation, about 35-40%; (C3) programme management, small share.
- Delivery model: direct execution by the municipal infrastructure secretariat through a new UGP, many municipal secretariats in a RACI matrix, no transfers between bodies; IDB environmental and social policy framework applies.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE to STRONG. Four thematic axes (environmental, land/housing, community/organisational, infrastructure/facilities) linked to specific objectives and each indicator is mapped to objectives and federal strategic areas; assumptions (sanitation plan prioritising the area, legislative approval, resettlement acceptance) are discussed in the risk section.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE to STRONG. 11 indicators, approximately 6 outcome-type (flooding points, water quality, buildings at risk, access to green space, land regularity, emissions) and 5 output-type (vegetation recovery, nature-based coverage, women and youth programme reach, community participation).
- Verification mechanism present: data to collect, formula and annual frequency stated for each; civil-defence mapping, laboratory water sampling, satellite/drone land-use imaging, municipal social registry data; benchmark used is the national water-quality standard for the water indicator; a municipal socio-environmental monitoring system is a component.
- Baselines: all 11 counted as defined with a value; all state a reference year or period. The GHG indicator uses an explicit counterfactual zero (no capture without the programme), counted as defined. Several targets are expressed as ratios whose denominators are only partly explained.
- Inconsistencies: targets mix horizons (some to the earlier year, some later); one vegetation-recovery target figure appears with slightly different values in description versus target; very large increase targeted for green-space access.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: ADEQUATE. Annual O&M quantified per asset class (linear park, public facilities, neighbourhood infrastructure, community programmes, monitoring system) with named responsible municipal bodies and sources (municipal budget, environmental fund); additional options mentioned (betterment levy, progressive property tax, concession of strategic areas, urban development fund already fed by building-rights charges). No binding legal earmark yet.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: STRONG to ADEQUATE. Detailed matrix (regulatory/legislative approval, procurement, expropriation, resettlement, environmental, social) with impact/probability and mitigation; resettlement and expropriation are treated at length (compensation options, social work plan, resettlement plan, dedicated commission); licensing under the municipal environmental body with studies planned.
- Counterpart: to be secured; municipal authorising law and UGP creation law pending in the city council, flagged as low-probability risk; no LOA/PPA line shown.
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE to STRONG. A UGP already operates for another multilateral-funded municipal programme and a new dedicated UGP will be created; supervision and programme-management consultancy included.
