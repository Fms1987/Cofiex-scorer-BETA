## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (CAF) to a municipal mixed-capital water utility, with Union guarantee; no counterpart (the CC says the borrower is a state company outside the counterpart resolution) and fully external funding in the last version.
- Finances urban water-supply security: a new raw-water source and production system (intake, raw-water main, treatment plant, treated-water main) at roughly 80% of cost, a supply-system optimisation master-plan study, and replacement of ageing asbestos-cement distribution mains.
- Delivery: direct execution by the utility, with a dedicated UGP under its technical directorate and supervision firms for the large works; the project plugs into an existing operational control centre.
- The last version is smaller in scope and value than earlier ones (components reduced from five to three), which is relevant to the iteration history.
