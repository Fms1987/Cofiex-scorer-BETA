## Resumo
- Investment operation (direct credit) for a capital-industrial municipality, financing a multi-theme program covering social housing production, urban river/linear-park revitalization with drainage works, and institutional strengthening; the header's "fund intermediation" sector keyword is a mistag — there is no on-lending in the CC.
- Delivery is direct, executed by the municipal government through a dedicated program coordination unit under the planning/administration secretariat, with other municipal secretariats as co-executors for their respective components.
- Main components: social housing construction and related public facilities; river-corridor revitalization and urban upgrading (linear park, cycling/transit corridors, sediment removal, future drainage works design); and technical support/institutional strengthening (municipal planning instruments, staff capacity-building).
- Entity type: capital-industrial municipality with some prior experience in external financing (a different lender) but none yet with the lender proposed here.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial sustainability: WEAK. No incremental staff is planned; upkeep of the new park/green-space assets, urban-infrastructure works and the new health facility is said to fall to existing municipal secretariats' maintenance units, and program expenses are simply stated to be "appropriated in the budget pieces (LDO/LOA) with a specific line item at the time of execution" — the NT found this explanation insufficiently developed.
- A.3 Risk assessment: ADEQUATE. Legal/institutional, procurement, environmental-licensing and social-impact risks are each listed with mitigation measures (e.g. a standing bidding commission for empty/contested tenders, an environmental/social framework shared in advance with the lender, a communication plan for construction-period disruption); no expropriations or resettlement are foreseen, with a fallback safeguard policy referenced should either become necessary.
- Counterpart: secured via land assets (municipal-owned plots valued from the municipal cadastre, no appraisal report attached) rather than a cash budget line — the NT flagged the lack of a formal valuation/appraisal report for the land-based counterpart as a gap, even though the land itself was said to be available and unencumbered.
- A.4 Implementation (UGP): ADEQUATE. A program coordination unit (created by municipal decree, tied to the planning/administration secretariat) already exists and is responsible for procurement across all components; however, the NT noted this would be the proponent's first operation with this specific lender, with no prior-portfolio track record against it.
- Outcome note: the NT recommends approval, citing that the operation reached the minimum requirements to proceed to COFIEX despite presentation gaps to be fixed during preparation; the ficha header records this attempt as withdrawn for exceeding the portfolio limit available at the relevant meeting — a later, separate administrative event not described in the available source text.
