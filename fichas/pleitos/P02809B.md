## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (IBRD, Union guarantee) to a federal regional development bank, no counterpart required for state-owned financial entities; ticket in the top band.
- Finances credit lines to private firms and subnational entities (sub-borrowers) in three components: industrial decarbonisation (low-carbon fuels, equipment, supply chains), energy transition (renewable generation, transmission/distribution, efficiency, public lighting) and supporting infrastructure (logistics, water and sanitation, connectivity).
- Delivery: intermediated on-lending; the bank screens sub-projects with its usual credit pipeline and environmental/social management system, sub-borrowers execute; the management unit is staffed from the bank, with no project funds for management.
- Four indicators, all aggregate sums over a five-year horizon.
