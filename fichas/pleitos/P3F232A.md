## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan (IDB) to a capital municipality, direct execution by the municipal education secretariat through a project management unit (UGP) linked to the secretary; counterpart at the 20% minimum.
- Finances a basic-education programme in four components: (1) pedagogical practices and educational technology (platforms, connectivity, devices, assessment and teacher training, an inclusive/anti-racist sub-component), roughly 45% of cost; (2) early-childhood and full-day school infrastructure plus "city as school" integration, roughly 40%; (3) management and communication systems, under 10%; (4) programme administration, impact evaluation and audit, under 5%.
- Outputs are largely studies, executive designs, platforms and reference-unit works rather than mass construction; most civil works are to be on municipal-owned land.
- Long-tenor IDB terms (about 5 years disbursement, 5.5 years grace, 25 years total); the CC justifies the lender by comparing spreads with IBRD.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Three fronts (pedagogy/technology, network expansion/infrastructure, management) map to three specific objectives and to a list of expected results, but there is no explicit causal chain with testable assumptions.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE leaning STRONG. 10 indicators verified, all outcome-level (approximate judgement: none is a pure output); all 10 state a baseline value, so baseline_defined_share = 1.0; no reference year is visible in the extracted table, so baseline_with_year_share = 0.0 (extraction may have truncated cells).
- Benchmarks: national basic-education quality index (IDEB) for early and final grades plus counts of schools under a threshold, age-grade distortion, dropout, full-day enrolment, literacy at end of 2nd year; sources cited are the national statistics institute and administrative records.
- Verification: primary and secondary public sources named for the IDEB-type indicators, administrative records for the rest; impact evaluation (mid-term and final) is a funded product.
- Weaknesses: no equity-disaggregated indicator despite the equity rhetoric; two paired indicators partly duplicate each other (average score and count of schools below cut-off); the expected-result text (e.g. IDEB targets "above 7") is more ambitious than the indicator targets.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. The CC admits recurring cost growth (building maintenance, staff) borne by the municipality and lists offsetting measures (class-size optimisation, teacher absenteeism control, resilient designs, lower training/assessment costs) but gives no quantified OPEX table and no budget line.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: WEAK. No risk matrix; environmental, social and resettlement risks are declared nil on the grounds of municipal-owned land; procurement risk is addressed with IDB-rules training and a peer municipal executor. Licensing of new school works is not discussed.
- Legal backing: authorising municipal law approval was still in progress, with a stated fallback of a new authorising law; the PPA cited was ending its cycle. Counterpart: no LOA/budget line shown, i.e. "to be secured". The financial-conditions section carries a counterpart figure that conflicts with the funding table (internal inconsistency).
- A.4 Implementation: ADEQUATE. UGP to be created under the education secretary, an inter-secretariat legal instrument with the works secretariat is still to be drafted, and two committees are only "may be established".
