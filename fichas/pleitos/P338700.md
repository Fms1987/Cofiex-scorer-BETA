## Resumo
- Modality: investment loan to a mid-sized municipal government (direct execution, no intermediation), CAF-financed with sovereign-guaranteed structure; counterpart at the 20% minimum; 5-year disbursement path, grace period of 5 years, variable base rate plus a spread.
- Integrated urban-development programme in five components: roads, drainage and mobility (about half of cost); socio-environmental and cultural enhancement (about 30%: sports facilities, culture/tourism sites, green-area recovery, social assistance centers); health and education facilities (about 10%); programme management (about 10%); minor other expenses.
- Executor is the municipal planning and management secretariat acting as the programme management unit (UGP), supported by line secretariats, a works-supervision consultancy and an external audit.

## Teoria da mudança e indicadores
- A.1 Theory of Change: ADEQUATE. Seven diagnosed problems map one-to-one to solutions, results and six objectives (A-F), each cross-referenced to indicators and PPA objectives; assumptions and causal pathways beyond the output-to-result link are not made explicit.
- A.2 Results Framework: ADEQUATE. 13 indicators verified (the summary list shows only 12, the detailed section has 13; version 5). Approximately 7 outcome/use-type (flooding share of households, walking trips share, access to leisure/sport, cultural events, visitor flows, school coverage, outpatient and social-assistance volumes) and about 6 output-like or mixed; point targets, target years and a per-indicator data-source list (census, school census, health information system, municipal records, field counts).
- Baselines: 11 of 13 counted as defined, all with a reference year (0.85 and 0.85). Zero baseline counted as defined only for the sports-facility use indicator (CC explains the facilities do not yet exist). Two zero baselines left undefined because the CC gives no explanation (accessibility of a hillside population, area of green space recovered).
- Internal inconsistencies: the pedestrian-flow indicator states a target as a percentage increase but its baseline is a count of residents; the social-assistance indicator formula is confusing; the school-coverage indicator denominator is loosely described. No external benchmarks cited.
- Verification mechanism present on paper (administrative records, georeferenced mapping, before/after counts) but no independent evaluation or frequency stated.

## Sustentabilidade e riscos
- A.5 Financial Sustainability: PARTIAL. An annual programme-maintenance estimate is broken down by category (management, M&E, audit, technical support, communication, reserve) but covers programme administration, not O&M of the new facilities (health units, schools, sports and cultural sites, park); funding relies on future budget lines, a proposed dedicated municipal fund and possible federal/state transfers.
- A.3 Risk Assessment: six risks (legal, contracting, environmental, social, expropriation, resettlement) with mitigation lists; legal risk rated medium (dependent on a municipal authorising law and inclusion in the budget guidelines law), others low. Expropriation and resettlement stated as not expected (public land) but with contingent plans. Licensing: environmental secretariat supports preliminary, installation and operation licences; preparation timeline foresees licences before tendering.
- Counterpart: at the 20% minimum, "to be secured" (authorising law, budget guidelines and annual budget not yet in place); counter-guarantee from constitutional revenue shares.
- A.4 Implementation: UGP inside the municipal planning secretariat with several management units, line secretariats as partners, supervision consultancy and external audit planned (terms of reference in draft; tenders depend on loan signing); an in-house monitoring system for priority projects is cited. Basic designs only partly finished at submission.
- The municipal PPA for the next cycle was still in drafting; alignment with the federal PPA is argued by programme.
