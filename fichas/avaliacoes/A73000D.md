- Technical analysis (Annex I): all requirements met; the NT recommends approving the preparation of the project. NT silent on the reason the file ended as not approved (header reason: cap exceeded).

- AE drivers: three areas scored. Competitiveness and business environment reached tier B (high impact, significant resources, a leverage indicator, multiplier effect expected). The partial sum of these area points is what the NT reports as adequacy; the header total is blank.
- Zero-scored areas: logistics efficiency and international trade had aligned components but no indicators or targets showing the objective; jobs and income indicators were judged means rather than the decent-work/poorest-inclusion target; regional inequality, cities, disaster resilience and international cooperation lacked dedicated objectives, components or indicators.
