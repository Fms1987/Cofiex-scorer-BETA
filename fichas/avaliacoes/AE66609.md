## Motivos e achados da NT
- This NT corresponds to an earlier, larger version of the pleito (total near the top of the 200-400M band, i.e. above the 100-200M band of the final version in the pleito ficha), consistent with this header's own ticket_band field; the later version reduced the proposed amount.
- Justification/coherence scored high (~88% of weight) but NT explicitly faults the diagnosis for not demonstrating the problem situation with sufficient clarity and precision, limiting a full assessment of the problem-solution-result correlation.
- Objective/component alignment scored ~88%, with NT flagging that a management-practices component does not correspond to any stated solution or result, and that a large specialised institute product (about a fifth of program value) had its link to program objectives found undemonstrated in the CC.
- Indicator coherence scored lower (~74% of weight): NT credits a well-selected, simply-calculated indicator set with result/impact indicators present, but flags the absence of calculation memory for targets and of indicated timeframes for their achievement.
- Disbursement schedule scored very low (~53% of weight): NT found the schedule unbalanced (around 55% of resources in the first two years, 93.5% by year three) and inconsistent with works-dependent products scheduled concurrently with prerequisite steps.
- Institutional arrangement scored unsatisfactory (~59%): the executing secretariat's current operational structure was assessed as lacking robustness for a program of this scale; its proposed restructuring appears only as an annexed proposal without a formal government commitment.
- Financial sustainability scored very low (~29%): despite narratives on increased federal education transfers and mining-royalty earmarking, NT found no information on post-program maintenance measures or cost forecasts.
- Risk identification and prior-action sub-criteria scored moderately (NT describes the risk mapping as "generic," without the depth needed for coherent mitigation measures; environmental and social risks "not properly mapped").
- Borrower's external-financing track record rated satisfactory based on identified prior contracts with external agents.
- Strategic-area score was comparatively low, driven mainly by low-income employment/income generation, education-condition improvement and technology-innovation criteria; most other strategic criteria were not addressed per NT's enumeration.
- NT's own recommendation at this cycle was for approval ("indicativo de aprovação") to the corresponding Cofiex meeting; the NT text is silent on any reason for this attempt's eventual withdrawal — the header's reason_cat "other" is not explained or corroborated anywhere in this NT, so no withdrawal justification is asserted here. Header score fields (ae/idh_pts/capag_pts/debt_pts/total) are blank, so no "total" is asserted; NT instead reports separate technical (~3.1/4.0 band) and strategic-area (~0.4/2.0 band) scores as calibration inputs.
