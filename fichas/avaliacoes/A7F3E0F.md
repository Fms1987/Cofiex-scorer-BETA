## Motivos e achados da NT
- Cycle match uncertain: the NT available is a later change request on an already-authorised programme, not the original scoring note, so no AE tiers, IDH, CAPAG or debt components can be taken from it (NT silent on AE drivers); the header total is not explained by this document.
- The change request asked for approval of removing the renewable-generation product (roughly a fifth of cost), with consequent removal of one objective, one indicator and the sub-component, and redistribution to the two remaining products (transmission/distribution modernisation and storage).
- Reasons given: the concessional climate fund no longer finances new generation plants, other credit lines already cover generation, and the funding amount is too small to finance many plants; unifying both funding sources also required aligning disbursement terms (shorter disbursement window, uniform grace and amortisation).
