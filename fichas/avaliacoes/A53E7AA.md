## Motivos e achados da NT
- NT reports, under Res. 17/2021: Technical Analysis 3.590/4.000 (Design 1.698/1.800; Management 0.940/1.000; Risk management 0.952/1.200) and Strategic Areas/Impacts (AE) 0.399/2.000; IDH points are not stated in the NT text available. These NT-reported figures are not cross-checked against the ficha header's total field, which is unpopulated.
- Within AE, the NT credits environmental-sustainability mitigation/monitoring programs, energy efficiency (smart LED lighting with intelligent controls) and drainage works adjacent to basic sanitation as the drivers of the AE sub-score — still a modest fraction of the 2.000 ceiling.
- Design sub-score drivers: a coherent, traffic-study-backed diagnosis-to-solution-to-results chain; indicators characterized by the NT as outcome/impact-level, simple and measurable, with only end-of-program targets (justified by the works having no function before completion); and an adequately phased disbursement schedule.
- Management sub-score drivers: an adequate institutional arrangement/RACI matrix; a sustainability plan channeling O&M through an existing municipal maintenance program plus a parallel administrative-modernization initiative, with estimated annual O&M at a small single-digit percentage of total investment; and strong alignment with the municipality's multi-year and sectoral planning instruments.
- Risk-management sub-score drivers: adequate completion of prior actions (studies, draft authorizing law, sustainability and expropriation plans) and generally adequate risk identification/mitigation across legal, procurement, expropriation and environmental dimensions. The sub-score is held back partly because the proponent's lack of prior experience with external financiers is scored at full credit under the applicable rule (not a penalty) and partly because the NT itself notes the proponent did not address potential negative social risks from construction works.
- No non-approval reason applies: outcome recorded as approved. The NT concludes there is no obstacle to approval, echoed unanimously by all sign-off levels (coordinator, general coordinator, secretary).
